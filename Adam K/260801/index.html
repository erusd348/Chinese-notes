📖 Vocabulary
换 huàn to change; to exchange
💬 Sentences
我想换钱。 wǒ xiǎng huàn qián I want to change money.
你想换多少钱？ nǐ xiǎng huàn duō shǎo qián How much do you want to change?
我想换100美元。 wǒ xiǎng huàn yī bǎi měi yuán I want to change 100 dollars.
我换了一个女朋友。 wǒ huàn le yī gè nǚ péng yǒu I changed my girlfriend.
我换了一个computer。 wǒ huàn le yī gè computer I changed my computer.
