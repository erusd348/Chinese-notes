📚 Grammar
A 在 location + V (Structure) Subject + 在 + place + verb — doing an action at a place
我在家喝水。 wǒ zài jiā hē shuǐ I drink water at home.
我在家玩游戏。 wǒ zài jiā wán yóu xì I play games at home.
我在健身房健身。 wǒ zài jiàn shēn fáng jiàn shēn I work out at the gym.
Joe在他的游戏店卖游戏。 Joe zài tā de yóu xì diàn mài yóu xì Joe sells games at his game store.
我在办公室办公。 wǒ zài bàn gōng shì bàn gōng I do office work at the office.
我在家办公。 wǒ zài jiā bàn gōng I work from home.
我在洗手间洗手。 wǒ zài xǐ shǒu jiān xǐ shǒu I wash my hands in the bathroom.
N 在 location (Structure) Subject + 在 + place — being at a place
我在家。 wǒ zài jiā I am at home.
我在健身房。 wǒ zài jiàn shēn fáng I am at the gym.
X 在哪儿？ (Structure) Where is X? — asking location
你在哪儿？ nǐ zài nǎ ér Where are you?
洗手间在哪儿？ xǐ shǒu jiān zài nǎ ér Where is the bathroom?
