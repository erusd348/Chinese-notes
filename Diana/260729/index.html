📚 Grammar
去 + Place + 了 went to somewhere (completed action with 了)
我去旅游了。 wǒ qù lǚ yóu le I went traveling.
我去了法国。 wǒ qù le fǎ guó I went to France.
Country + 菜 + Adj describing a country's food
法国菜好吃吗？ fǎ guó cài hǎo chī ma Is French food tasty?
法国菜不好吃，法国面包很好吃。 fǎ guó cài bù hǎo chī, fǎ guó miàn bāo hěn hǎo chī French food isn't tasty, French bread is very tasty.
意大利菜很好吃。 yì dà lì cài hěn hǎo chī Italian food is very tasty.
瑞士菜很简单。 ruì shì cài hěn jiǎn dān Swiss food is very simple.
Country + 的 + 风景 / 山水 describing scenery
法国很美，也有很多游客。 fǎ guó hěn měi, yě yǒu hěn duō yóu kè France is beautiful and has many tourists.
瑞士的风景和山水很美。 ruì shì de fēng jǐng hé shān shuǐ hěn měi Switzerland's scenery and landscape are beautiful.
更 + Adj more; comparative with 更
纽约是一个很吵的大城市。 niǔ yuē shì yī gè hěn chǎo de dà chéng shì New York is a very noisy big city.
我更喜欢安静的环境。 wǒ gèng xǐ huān ān jìng de huán jìng I prefer a quiet environment.
Adj 描述 describing objects
我的卧室很亮，也很漂亮。 wǒ de wò shì hěn liàng, yě hěn piào liang My bedroom is bright and pretty.
我的衣服很舒服，也很薄。 wǒ de yī fu hěn shū fu, yě hěn báo My clothes are comfortable and thin.
这本书很好看。 zhè běn shū hěn hǎo kàn This book is good.
这个电影很好看。 zhè ge diàn yǐng hěn hǎo kàn This movie is good.
像 to look like; to resemble
他们很像。 tā men hěn xiàng They look alike.
你和你妹妹像吗？ nǐ hé nǐ mèi mei xiàng ma Do you and your sister look alike?
我和我妹妹有一点像。 wǒ hé wǒ mèi mei yǒu yī diǎn xiàng My sister and I look a little alike.
我像我爸爸。 wǒ xiàng wǒ bà ba I look like my dad.
外貌与品质 appearance and qualities
你爸爸帅吗？ nǐ bà ba shuài ma Is your dad handsome?
我爸爸很帅。 wǒ bà ba hěn shuài My dad is handsome.
他很老也很丑。 tā hěn lǎo yě hěn chǒu He is old and ugly.
他很坏。他是坏人。 tā hěn huài. tā shì huài rén He is bad. He's a bad person.
你疯了吗？ nǐ fēng le ma Are you crazy?
我妈妈不高不矮，很美，很聪明，对人很好。 wǒ mā ma bù gāo bù ǎi, hěn měi, hěn cōng míng, duì rén hěn hǎo My mom is neither tall nor short, very beautiful and smart, very kind to people.
我爸爸很高很帅，对人很好，也很聪明。 wǒ bà ba hěn gāo hěn shuài, duì rén hěn hǎo, yě h��n cōng míng My dad is very tall and handsome, kind to people, and also very smart.
