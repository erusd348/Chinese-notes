📚 Grammar
音乐剧 musical
我看过很多音乐剧。 wǒ kàn guò hěn duō yīn yuè jù I have seen many musicals.
百老汇 Broadway
百老汇在纽约。 bǎi lǎo huì zài niǔ yuē Broadway is in New York.
冥界 underworld
《冥界》是一部音乐剧。 míng jiè shì yī bù yīn yuè jù Hadestown is a musical.
上映 to release (a film)
这部电影什么时候上映？ zhè bù diàn yǐng shén me shí hòu shàng yìng When is this movie releasing?
悲剧 vs 主角 tragedy vs main character
这是一部悲剧。 zhè shì yī bù bēi jù This is a tragedy.
他是这部电影的主角。 tā shì zhè bù diàn yǐng de zhǔ jué He is the lead role in this movie.
导演 director
这位导演很有名。 zhè wèi dǎo yǎn hěn yǒu míng This director is very famous.
彼此 each other
我们彼此都很了解。 wǒ men bǐ cǐ dōu hěn liǎo jiě We understand each other well.
几乎 almost
我几乎每天都去健身房。 wǒ jī hū měi tiān dōu qù jiàn shēn fáng I go to the gym almost every day.
不闻不问 to ignore
他对家里的事不闻不问。 tā duì jiā lǐ de shì bù wén bù wèn He ignores everything at home.
隔膜 estrangement
我们之间有隔膜。 wǒ men zhī jiān yǒu gé mó There is estrangement between us.
长辈 elder
我们要尊重长辈。 wǒ men yào zūn zhòng zhǎng bèi We must respect our elders.
己所不欲，勿施于人 Golden Rule
如果你想别人对你好，你就要对别人好。 rú guǒ nǐ xiǎng bié rén duì nǐ hǎo, nǐ jiù yào duì bié rén hǎo If you want others to treat you well, you must treat others well.
