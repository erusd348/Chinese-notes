📚 Grammar
蓬荜生辉 to bring honor to a humble home
您的到来让我家蓬荜生辉。 nín de dào lái ràng wǒ jiā péng bì shēng huī Your visit brings honor to my humble home.
满脸 one's whole face
他满脸笑容。 tā mǎn liǎn xiào róng His face was full of smiles.
挣钱 vs 赚钱 earn money vs make money
他每天工作挣钱。 tā měi tiān gōng zuò zhèng qián He works every day to earn money.
这个生意很赚钱。 zhè gè shēng yì hěn zhuàn qián This business is very profitable.
怀疑 vs 可疑 suspect vs suspicious
我怀疑他在说谎。 wǒ huái yí tā zài shuō huǎng I suspect he is lying.
这个人很可疑。 zhè gè rén hěn kě yí This person is very suspicious.
推脱 to make excuses
他喜欢推脱责任。 tā xǐ huān tuī tuō zé rèn He likes to shirk responsibility.
边界感 sense of boundaries
人与人之间要有边界感。 rén yǔ rén zhī jiān yào yǒu biān jiè gǎn There should be a sense of boundaries between people.
健谈 talkative
我的同事很健谈。 wǒ de tóng shì hěn jiàn tán My colleague is very talkative.
房产中介 real estate agent
我的朋友是房产中介。 wǒ de péng yǒu shì fáng chǎn zhōng jiè My friend is a real estate agent.
擦肩而过 to brush past
我们在地铁站擦肩而过。 wǒ men zài dì tiě zhàn cā jiān ér guò We brushed past each other at the subway station.
兴趣 vs 嗜好 interest vs hobby
攀岩是我的兴趣。 pān yán shì wǒ de xìng qù Rock climbing is my interest.
他有很多嗜好。 tā yǒu hěn duō shì hào He has many hobbies.
网约车 ride-hailing car
我每天叫网约车上班。 wǒ měi tiān jiào wǎng yuē chē shàng bān I call a ride-hailing car to work every day.
