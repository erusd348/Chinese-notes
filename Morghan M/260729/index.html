📚 Grammar
S + V + 了 + O completed action — 了 before object
我吃了一碗面。 wǒ chī le yī wǎn miàn I ate a bowl of noodles.
S + V + O + 了 change of state — 了 at end
我吃一碗面了。 wǒ chī yī wǎn miàn le I (now) eat a bowl of noodles.
人 + 说 + Language people of a country speak a language
德国人说德语。 dé guó rén shuō dé yǔ Germans speak German.
法国人说法语。 fǎ guó rén shuō fǎ yǔ French people speak French.
韩国人说韩语。 hán guó rén shuō hán yǔ South Koreans speak Korean.
加拿大人说英语和法语。 jiā ná dà rén shuō yīng yǔ hé fǎ yǔ Canadians speak English and French.
自己 self
我来介绍一下我自己。 wǒ lái jiè shào yī xià wǒ zì jǐ Let me introduce myself.
我不自己吃饭。 wǒ bù zì jǐ chī fàn I don't eat by myself.
我一个人吃饭。 wǒ yī gè rén chī fàn I eat alone.
有 + A、B + 还有 + C have A, B, and also C
我有猪肉、苹果还有面条。 wǒ yǒu zhū ròu, píng guǒ hái yǒu miàn tiáo I have pork, apples, and noodles.
你有 + A，我 + 也 + 有 + A you have A, I also have A
你有猫，我也有猫。 nǐ yǒu māo, wǒ yě yǒu māo You have a cat, I also have a cat.
房间 / 乱 / 厚 / 薄 / 新 / 旧 describing objects in a room
我房间有一点小，也有一点乱。 wǒ fáng jiān yǒu yī diǎn xiǎo, yě yǒu yī diǎn luàn My room is a bit small and also a bit messy.
