📚 Grammar
最 + V (Structure) zuì + V — like/love doing sth the most
我最喜欢猫。 wǒ zuì xǐ huan māo. I like cats the most.
我最喜欢吃猪肉。 wǒ zuì xǐ huan chī zhū ròu. I like eating pork the most.
我最喜欢喝咖啡。 wǒ zuì xǐ huan hē kā fēi. I like drinking coffee the most.
我最喜欢打壁球。 wǒ zuì xǐ huan dǎ bì qiú. I like playing squash the most.
我最喜欢我男朋友。 wǒ zuì xǐ huan wǒ nán péng you. I like my boyfriend the most.
我最不喜欢吃这个。 wǒ zuì bù xǐ huan chī zhè ge. This is what I like eating the least.
最 + Adj (Structure) zuì + Adj — the most Adj
我男朋友最帅。 wǒ nán péng you zuì shuài. My boyfriend is the most handsome.
我的猫最可爱。 wǒ de māo zuì kě'ài. My cat is the cutest.
怎么 + V……？ (Structure) how do you V...? / how do you say...?
怎么打壁球？ zěn me dǎ bì qiú? How do you play squash?
怎么写这个字？ zěn me xiě zhè ge zì? How do you write this character?
样子 the look (of sth)
这个手机的样子很好看。 zhè ge shǒu jī de yàng zi hěn hǎo kàn. This phone looks really nice.
怎么样？ how about...? / how is it going?
我们去公园散步，怎么样？ wǒ men qù gōng yuán sàn bù, zěn me yàng? How about we go for a walk in the park?
你最近怎么样？ nǐ zuì jìn zěn me yàng? How have you been recently?
你今天怎么样？ nǐ jīn tiān zěn me yàng? How are you today?
身体 body; health
你最近身体怎么样？ nǐ zuì jìn shēn tǐ zěn me yàng? How has your health been recently?
我身体很好。 wǒ shēn tǐ hěn hǎo. My health is very good.
身材 figure, physique
他身材很好。 tā shēn cái hěn hǎo. He has a great figure.
我喜欢身材好的帅哥。 wǒ xǐ huan shēn cái hǎo de shuài gē. I like handsome guys with good figures.
比较 adv. relatively
我身材比较好。 wǒ shēn cái bǐ jiào hǎo. My figure is relatively good.
A 比 B + Adj (Structure) A is more Adj than B
我比她漂亮。 wǒ bǐ tā piào liang. I am prettier than her.
猪肉比蔬菜好吃。 zhū ròu bǐ shū cài hǎo chī. Pork tastes better than vegetables.
中国人比美国人多。 zhōng guó rén bǐ měi guó rén duō. There are more Chinese people than Americans.
马斯克比我有钱。 mǎ sī kè bǐ wǒ yǒu qián. Musk is richer than me.
马斯克的钱比我多。 mǎ sī kè de qián bǐ wǒ duō. Musk has more money than me.
B 没有 A + Adj (Structure) B is not as Adj as A
她没有我漂亮。 tā méi yǒu wǒ piào liang. She is not as pretty as me.
蔬菜没有猪肉好吃。 shū cài méi yǒu zhū ròu hǎo chī. Vegetables don't taste as good as pork.
我没有马斯克有钱。 wǒ méi yǒu mǎ sī kè yǒu qián. I am not as rich as Musk.
