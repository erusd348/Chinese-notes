飞龙：怎么了？你的房间有问题吗？
李钟文：不是。我跟我的同屋生活习惯完全不一样。

李钟文：她既没说同意，也没说不同意，只说要等等。

📝 Notes
1. 才 (cái) — indicating a small quantity
才
“才” is an adverb that indicates a small amount. When used before a verb + quantity, it means “only” or “merely”, suggesting the speaker thinks the quantity is less than expected.
他一天才吃了两顿饭。tā yī tiān cái chī le liǎng dùn fàn。He only ate two meals in a whole day — thats very little.
我才学了三个月汉语。wǒ cái xué le sān gè yuè hàn yǔ。Ive only studied Chinese for three months.
这个房间才十平方米。zhè ge fáng jiān cái shí píng fāng mǐ。This room is only ten square meters.
2. 偏偏 (piānpiān) — contrary to expectations
偏偏
“偏偏” is an adverb indicating that something happens contrary to ones wishes or expectations. It often conveys disappointment or frustration.
下午我学习的时候，他偏偏要睡觉！xià wǔ wǒ xué xí de shí hou， tā piān piān yào shuì jiào！In the afternoon when Im studying, he just has to sleep!
好不容易到周末了，偏偏下起雨来。hǎo bù róng yì dào zhōu mò liǎo， piān piān xià qǐ yǔ lái。I finally made it to the weekend, and of course it had to rain.
大家都同意了，他偏偏反对。dà jiā dōu tóng yì le， tā piān piān fǎn duì。Everyone agreed, but he just had to oppose it.
3. 既……也/又…… (jì...yě/yòu...) — both...and...; neither...nor...
既……也/又……
“既……也/又……” connects two verbs or adjectives to indicate that two qualities or situations exist simultaneously. Use “也” for a milder tone and “又” for a stronger one.
她既没说同意，也没说不同意。tā jì méi shuō tóng yì， yě méi shuō bù tóng yì。She neither agreed nor disagreed.
他既聪明又努力。tā jì cōng ming yòu nǔ lì。He is both smart and hardworking.
这件衣服既便宜又好看。zhè jiàn yī fu jì pián yi yòu hǎo kàn。This piece of clothing is both cheap and nice-looking.
4. 能 (néng) vs. 会 (huì) — expressing ability
能 (néng) vs. 会
“能” means “can” — having the ability, condition, or permission to do something. “会” means “know how to” — a skill learned through practice.
我会说汉语。wǒ huì shuō hàn yǔ。I can speak Chinese — I know how to.
我不能去，明天有事。wǒ bù néng qù， míng tiān yǒu shì。I cant go — I have something to do tomorrow.
学了汉字才能看书。xué le hàn zì cái néng kàn shū。Only after learning characters can one read books. [能 = objective possibility].
5. 怎么 + Verb + 得/不 + Complement — rhetorical “how can”
“怎么” in this structure is a rhetorical question meaning “how can/how could”. It implies the speaker believes something is impossible or unreasonable.
天天睡不好，怎么能有精神？tiān tiān shuì bù hǎo， zěn me néng yǒu jīng shen？If you cant sleep well every day, how can you have energy?
不练习，怎么能学好汉语？bù liàn xí， zěn me néng xué hǎo hàn yǔ？If you dont practice, how can you learn Chinese well?
这么贵的手机，我怎么买得起？zhè me guì de shǒu jī， wǒ zěn me mǎi de qǐ？Such an expensive phone — how could I afford it?
6. 不是……吗？(bú shì...ma) — rhetorical question
不是……吗？
“不是……吗？” is a rhetorical question pattern. The speaker uses it to point out something obvious, expecting agreement. It translates to “Isnt it...?” or “Dont you...?”
咱们的宿舍楼不是临街吗？zán men de sù shè lóu bù shì lín jiē ma？Isnt our dormitory building facing the street? — It obviously is.
你不是中国人吗？怎么不会用筷子？nǐ bù shì zhōng guó rén ma？ zěn me bù huì yòng kuài zi？Arent you Chinese? How come you cant use chopsticks?
你不是说过要来吗？nǐ bù shì shuō guò yào lái ma？Didnt you say you would come?
7. 或者……或者…… (huòzhě...huòzhě...) — either...or...
或者……或者……
“或者……或者……” presents two or more alternatives in declarative sentences. For questions, use “还是” instead.
你或者想办法换个房间，或者快点儿习惯新环境。nǐ huò zhě xiǎng bàn fǎ huàn gè fáng jiān， huò zhě kuài diǎn ér xí guàn xīn huán jìng。Either find a way to change rooms, or quickly get used to the new environment.
周末我或者看书，或者看电影。zhōu mò wǒ huò zhě kàn shū， huò zhě kàn diàn yǐng。On weekends I either read or watch a movie.
你或者坐地铁去，或者打车去。nǐ huò zhě zuò dì tiě qù， huò zhě dǎ chē qù。You can either take the subway or take a taxi.
8. 再说吧 (zàishuō ba) — lets talk about it later
再说吧
“再说吧” means not wanting to discuss or decide something now, but to deal with it later when the time is right. “再” means “later/further”, “说” means “discuss”. 🔄 Clear ✓ Done 🖨 Print
今天太晚了，明天再说吧。jīn tiān tài wǎn le， míng tiān zài shuō ba。Its too late today. Lets talk about it tomorrow.
A： 你打算什么时候结婚？ B： 以后再说吧。A： nǐ dǎ suan shén me shí hou jié hūn？B： yǐ hòu zài shuō ba。A: When do you plan to get married? B: Well see later.
你先找工作，其他的事以后再说。nǐ xiān zhǎo gōng zuò， qí tā de shì yǐ hòu zài shuō。Find a job first; well worry about other things later.
