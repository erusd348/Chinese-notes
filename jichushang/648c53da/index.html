📝 Notes
1. 怎么 + Verb + 都 + (不/没) — no matter how
怎么 + Verb + 都 +
“怎么 + Verb + 都 + 不/没...” means “no matter how one tries, the result doesnt change”. Expresses frustration that despite all efforts, something cant be achieved.
可像我这样的人怎么减肥都没用。kě xiàng wǒ zhè yàng de rén zěn me jiǎn féi dōu méi yòng。But for someone like me, no matter how I try to lose weight, it doesnt work.
他怎么学都学不会。tā zěn me xué dōu xué bù huì。No matter how hard he studies, he cant learn it.
这东西怎么修都修不好。zhè dōng xi zěn me xiū dōu xiū bù hǎo。No matter how you fix it, this thing just wont work.
2. 谁/哪儿/什么/怎么 + 也/都 — universal reference
谁/哪儿/什么/怎么 + 也/都
An emphatic structure meaning “(no matter) who/where/what/how”. The question word + “也/都” covers all possibilities.
谁也不知道。shuí yě bù zhī dao。Nobody knows.
我哪儿都不想去。wǒ nǎ ér dōu bù xiǎng qù。I dont want to go anywhere.
他什么都能吃。tā shén me dōu néng chī。He can eat anything.
怎么劝都没用。zěn me quàn dōu méi yòng。No matter how you persuade him, its useless.
3. 太 + Adj + 了 (tài...le) — too; extremely
太 + Adj + 了
“太 + Adjective + 了” means “too (adjective)”, “extremely (adjective)”, or “(adjective) to excess”. Can be positive or negative depending on the adjective.
你说得太绝对了吧？nǐ shuō de tài jué duì le ba？What you said is too absolute, isnt it?
太好了！tài hǎo le！Great! / Wonderful!
太多了，吃不了。tài duō le， chī bù liǎo。Too much — I cant eat it all.
4. 一天比一天 (yì tiān bǐ yì tiān) — day by day
一天比一天
“一天比一天” means “day by day” or “more and more each day”. Describes a progressive increase or decrease over time.
那你说为什么现在的胖子一天比一天多？nà nǐ shuō wèi shén me xiàn zài de pàng zi yī tiān bǐ yī tiān duō？Then tell me, why are there more and more fat people every day?
天气一天比一天暖和了。tiān qì yī tiān bǐ yī tiān nuǎn huo le。The weather is getting warmer day by day.
他的汉语一天比一天进步。tā de hàn yǔ yī tiān bǐ yī tiān jìn bù。His Chinese is improving day by day.
5. 既然 (jìrán) — since; now that
既然
“既然” introduces a reason/fact that is already established. The second clause explains the logical conclusion. “就” is often used in the second clause.
既然吃药也没用，那就算了。jì rán chī yào yě méi yòng， nà jiù suàn le。Since taking medicine doesnt work either, lets just forget it.
既然你已经决定了，我就不劝你了。jì rán nǐ yǐ jīng jué dìng le， wǒ jiù bù quàn nǐ le。Since you have already decided, I wont try to persuade you.
既然大家都来了，我们就开始吧。jì rán dà jiā dōu lái le， wǒ men jiù kāi shǐ ba。Since everyones here, lets start.
6. 万一 (wànyī) — just in case; in the event that
万一
“万一” introduces an unlikely but possible situation — something one hopes wont happen but prepares for just in case. Often used when warning or being cautious.
万一让我爱人知道了，就麻烦了。wàn yī ràng wǒ ài rén zhī dao le， jiù má fan le。If my spouse finds out, it will be trouble.
带上伞吧，万一下雨呢。dài shàng sǎn ba， wàn yī xià yǔ ne。Bring an umbrella, just in case it rains.
万一他不同意，我们怎么办？wàn yī tā bù tóng yì， wǒ men zěn me bàn？What if he doesnt agree? What should we do?
7. 一 + Verb + 就是 + Quantity/Time — once started, it lasts for...
This structure indicates that the action, once started, lasts for a surprisingly long time or involves a large quantity. 🔄 Clear ✓ Done 🖨 Print
一玩儿就是好几个小时。yī wán ér jiù shì hǎo jǐ gè xiǎo shí。Once he starts playing, it is for several hours straight.
一睡就是半天。yī shuì jiù shì bàn tiān。Once he falls asleep, it is for half a day.
一买就是一大堆。yī mǎi jiù shì yī dà duī。Once he starts buying, it is a whole pile of stuff.
