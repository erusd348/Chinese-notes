课文 1
张英：季红，今天咱俩去哪儿玩儿？
季红：有名的地方差不多都去过了，只有香山和天坛公园还没去。
张英：香山比天坛近一些，我建议今天去香山。
季红：行啊。那咱们怎么去呢？
张英：坐公交或者骑车都行，你说吧。
季红：坐公交太挤了。这几天我天天坐，已经坐腻了。
张英：不想坐公交，那咱们就骑车去。
季红：骑车去有点儿远，而且还有山路。
张英：没关系，要是累了，咱们就停下来歇会儿。
季红：去可能没问题，回来的时候就很难说啦。
张英：要是骑不动了，咱们就在路上找个饭馆儿先吃饭。

📝 Notes
1. 差不多 (chàbuduō) — almost; nearly; about the same
差不多
“差不多” means “almost”, “nearly”, or “about the same”. It indicates a small difference between two things — they are close but not exactly the same. It can also be used before quantities.
咱们俩差不多高。zán men liǎ chà bu duō gāo。The two of us are about the same height.
这两个餐厅的价格差不多。zhè liǎng gè cān tīng de jià gé chà bu duō。The prices at these two restaurants are about the same.
教室里坐了差不多20人。jiào shì lǐ zuò le chà bu duō20 rén。There are almost 20 people sitting in the classroom.
2. 比 (bǐ) — comparative structure
比
The preposition “比” is used to make comparisons: A + 比 + B + Adjective + (Difference). The “Difference” can be a quantity or words like “得多”, “多了”, “一点儿”.
香山比天坛近一些。xiāng shān bǐ tiān tán jìn yī xiē。Fragrant Hills is a bit closer than the Temple of Heaven.
他比我高得多。tā bǐ wǒ gāo de duō。He is much taller than me.
今天比昨天冷多了。jīn tiān bǐ zuó tiān lěng duō le。Today is much colder than yesterday.
3. 腻 (nì) — to be tired of; fed up with
腻
“腻” describes the feeling of being tired of something because you have done it too many times or had too much of it. Commonly used for food, activities, or entertainment.
天天吃方便面，我都吃腻了。tiān tiān chī fāng biàn miàn， wǒ dōu chī nì le。Eating instant noodles every day — Im sick of them.
这首歌我听了好多遍，都听腻了。zhè shǒu gē wǒ tīng le hǎo duō biàn， dōu tīng nì le。Ive listened to this song so many times — Im tired of it.
同一个电影看三遍，你不腻吗？tóng yī gè diàn yǐng kàn sān biàn， nǐ bù nì ma？Watching the same movie three times — dont you get tired of it?
4. 要是 (yàoshì) — if; in case (hypothetical)
要是
“要是” is a conjunction used in the first clause to introduce a hypothesis. “就”, “那”, or “那么” often appears in the second clause to introduce the result.
明天要是下雨，我们就不去了。míng tiān yào shì xià yǔ， wǒ men jiù bù qù le。If it rains tomorrow, we wont go.
要是你不来，那我也不去了。yào shì nǐ bù lái， nà wǒ yě bù qù le。If youre not coming, then Im not going either.
要是你有时间，我们就一起去。yào shì nǐ yǒu shí jiān， wǒ men jiù yī qǐ qù。If you have time, lets go together.
5. 千万 (qiānwàn) — must; be sure to; by all means
千万
“千万” expresses “must” or “be sure to”. It is used when urging someone earnestly or expressing a strong wish. Usually followed by “要” or a negative expression.
明天的晚会你千万要来呀。míng tiān de wǎn huì nǐ qiān wàn yào lái ya。You absolutely must come to tomorrows party.
你千万别忘了带护照。nǐ qiān wàn bié wàng le dài hù zhào。Dont forget to bring your passport — whatever you do!
千万别告诉他这个消息。qiān wàn bié gào sù tā zhè ge xiāo xi。Be sure NOT to tell him this news.
6. 因为……所以…… (yīnwèi...suǒyǐ...) — because...therefore...
因为……所以……
The most common structure for expressing cause and effect. “因为” introduces the reason, and “所以” introduces the result.
那是因为故宫在市区，沿途都是热闹的地方，所以很容易堵车。nà shì yīn wèi gù gōng zài shì qū， yán tú dōu shì rè nao de dì fang， suǒ yǐ hěn róng yì dǔ chē。Thats because the Forbidden City is downtown and the route is full of busy areas, so traffic jams are common.
因为下雨，所以比赛取消了。yīn wèi xià yǔ， suǒ yǐ bǐ sài qǔ xiāo le。Because it rained, the match was cancelled.
因为他很努力，所以进步很快。yīn wèi tā hěn nǔ lì， suǒ yǐ jìn bù hěn kuài。Because he works hard, hes improving quickly.
7. 再说 (zàishuō) — moreover; besides; furthermore
再说
“再说” (as a conjunction) is used in spoken Chinese to give a further reason or add new supporting arguments. It usually appears at the beginning of the second sentence.
再说，飞龙骑车的技术不高，他不敢骑车去。zài shuō， fēi lóng qí chē de jì shù bù gāo， tā bù gǎn qí chē qù。Besides, Feilongs cycling skills arent good — he doesnt dare to ride there.
这本书没什么意思，再说你已经有一本差不多的书了，别买了。zhè běn shū méi shén me yì si， zài shuō nǐ yǐ jīng yǒu yī běn chà bu duō de shū le， bié mǎi le。This book isnt interesting — besides, you already have a similar one. Dont buy it.
我们是好朋友，不用客气。再说，互相帮忙是应该的。wǒ men shì hǎo péng you， bù yòng kè qi。 zài shuō， hù xiāng bāng máng shì yīng gāi de。Were good friends — no need to be polite. Besides, helping each other is only right.
8. 再 + 说 (zài + shuō) — to talk about it later
再 + 说
“再 + 说” literally means “to talk (about it) later”. One doesnt want to do or decide something now, but will do so when the right time comes. NB: Note the difference from “再说” (conjunction, meaning “besides/moreover”) vs. “再+说” (adverb+verb, meaning “talk about later”). 🔄 Clear ✓ Done 🖨 Print
先找工作吧，其他的事以后再说。xiān zhǎo gōng zuò ba， qí tā de shì yǐ hòu zài shuō。Find a job first — well talk about other things later.
今天太晚了，明天再说吧。jīn tiān tài wǎn le， míng tiān zài shuō ba。Its too late today. Lets talk about it tomorrow.
你先找地方住下，工作的事过几天再说。nǐ xiān zhǎo dì fang zhù xià， gōng zuò de shì guò jǐ tiān zài shuō。Find a place to stay first — well talk about work in a few days.
