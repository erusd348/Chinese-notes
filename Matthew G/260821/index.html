📚 Grammar
S + 在 + Place (Structure) S is located at Place
你家在哪儿？ nǐ jiā zài nǎr Where is your home?
我家在1号楼2楼201室。 wǒ jiā zài yī hào lóu èr lóu èr líng yī shì My home is Room 201, Floor 2, Building 1.
想 + V (Structure) want to do
我晚上想留在你这儿。 wǒ wǎnshang xiǎng liú zài nǐ zhèr I want to stay here tonight.
我想留在中国。 wǒ xiǎng liú zài zhōngguó I want to stay in China.
去 + Place + 留学 (Structure) go to Place to study abroad
我想去中国留学。 wǒ xiǎng qù zhōngguó liúxué I want to go to China to study.
他去美国留学了。 tā qù měiguó liúxué le He went to America to study abroad.
Pronoun + 这儿/那儿 (Structure) at someone's place (here / there)
我在Tanner这儿。 wǒ zài Tanner zhèr I'm at Tanner's place.
我在我爸妈这儿。 wǒ zài wǒ bàmā zhèr I'm at my parents' place.
我们去他那儿吃饭。 wǒmen qù tā nàr chīfàn We're going to his place for dinner.
💬 Sentences
我女朋友是留学生。 wǒ nǚpéngyou shì liúxuéshēng My girlfriend is an international student.
她来美国留学。 tā lái měiguó liúxué She came to America to study abroad.
