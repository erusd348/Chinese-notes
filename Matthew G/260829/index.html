📚 Grammar
常常 + V (Structure) often do (something)
我在伦敦和迈阿密之间来回跑。 wǒ zài lúndūn hé màiāmì zhījiān láihuí pǎo I go back and forth between London and Miami.
来 + 这儿/那儿 (Structure) come to my/your place
我想我朋友来我这儿，但是他们想我去他们那儿。 wǒ xiǎng wǒ péngyou lái wǒ zhèr, dànshì tāmen xiǎng wǒ qù tāmen nàr I want my friends to come to my place, but they want me to go to theirs.
回 + Place (Structure) return to (place)
我回家了。 wǒ huí jiā le I went home.
来来去去 (Structure) coming and going
我们公司有很多人来来去去。 wǒmen gōngsī yǒu hěn duō rén láilái qùqù Many people come and go at our company.
炒鱿鱼（被开除） (idiom) to be fired
他工作不好，公司炒他鱿鱼。 tā gōngzuò bù hǎo, gōngsī chǎo tā yóuyú He didn't work well, so the company fired him.
因为… (Structure) because...
他没来，因为他病了。 tā méi lái, yīnwèi tā bìng le He didn't come because he was sick.
如果…就… (Structure) if...then...
如果她来我这儿，我就去。 rúguǒ tā lái wǒ zhèr, wǒ jiù qù If she comes to my place, I'll go.
就（强调） (Structure) just; exactly
我就是马修。 wǒ jiù shì mǎxiū I am Matthew.
就在那儿。 jiù zài nàr It's right over there.
护士/男护士/病人 (vocab) nurse / male nurse / patient
她是护士。 tā shì hùshi She is a nurse.
他是男护士。 tā shì nán hùshi He is a male nurse.
病人来了。 bìngrén lái le The patient has come.
病 (vocab) to be sick
他有病！ tā yǒu bìng He is crazy!
他病了。 tā bìng le He is sick.
他好了。 tā hǎo le He has recovered.
