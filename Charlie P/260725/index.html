📚 Grammar
取 to take; to withdraw
我去银行取钱。 wǒ qù yín háng qǔ qián I go to the bank to withdraw money.
我去取快递。 wǒ qù qǔ kuài dì I go to pick up a package.
接 to pick up (someone)
我去机场接你。 wǒ qù jī chǎng jiē nǐ I will pick you up at the airport.
一 + V1 + 就 + V2 as soon as... then...
我一做完饭就来。 wǒ yī zuò wán fàn jiù lái I will come as soon as I finish cooking.
我一吃完早饭就去上班。 wǒ yī chī wán zǎo fàn jiù qù shàng bān I go to work as soon as I finish breakfast.
我一下课就去睡觉。 wǒ yī xià kè jiù qù shuì jiào I go to sleep as soon as class ends.
我一看到你就爱上你了。 wǒ yī kàn dào nǐ jiù ài shàng nǐ le I fell in love with you as soon as I saw you.
麻烦 to trouble; troublesome
他很麻烦。 tā hěn má fan He is very troublesome.
这个菜很麻烦。 zhè gè cài hěn má fan This dish is very troublesome (to make).
转告 to pass on (a message)
我可以麻烦您转告他一件事吗？ wǒ kě yǐ má fan nín zhuǎn gào tā yī jiàn shì ma Could I trouble you to pass on a message to him?
清楚 clear
你听清楚了吗？ nǐ tīng qīng chu le ma Did you hear clearly?
我看得很清楚。 wǒ kàn de hěn qīng chu I see it very clearly.
他说得很清楚。 tā shuō de hěn qīng chu He explained it very clearly.
我不清楚。 wǒ bù qīng chu I am not clear (I do not know).
