📚 Grammar
V + 住（结果补语） (Structure) V + 住 — hold / stop / remember firmly
站住！ zhàn zhù Stop! Stand still!
看住他，别让他跑了。 kān zhù tā，bié ràng tā pǎo le Watch him, don't let him run away.
你给我记住！ nǐ gěi wǒ jì zhù You'd better remember this!
你记住了吗？我记住了。 nǐ jì zhù le ma？wǒ jì zhù le Did you remember? Yes, I did.
我记住这个生词了。 wǒ jì zhù zhè gè shēng cí le I remembered this new word.
V + 完（结果补语） (Structure) V + 完 — finish doing V
我的作业做完了。 wǒ de zuò yè zuò wán le I finished my homework.
我看完这本书了。 wǒ kàn wán zhè běn shū le I finished reading this book.
V + 到（结果补语） (Structure) V + 到 — manage to do V / result reached
我接到他了。 wǒ jiē dào tā le I picked him up (successfully).
我接到球了。 wǒ jiē dào qiú le I caught the ball.
V + 好（结果补语） (Structure) V + 好 — V done properly / completed
我的手表修好了。 wǒ de shǒu biǎo xiū hǎo le My watch is fixed.
V + 懂（结果补语） (Structure) V + 懂 — understand (from listening)
老师讲得很清楚。我听懂了。 lǎo shī jiǎng de hěn qīng chǔ。wǒ tīng dǒng le The teacher explained very clearly. I understood.
📞 课文：找保罗 (text) Looking for Paul — a short dialogue
你看见保罗了吗？我有急事找他。 nǐ kàn jiàn bǎo luó le ma？wǒ yǒu jí shì zhǎo tā Did you see Paul? I have something urgent to find him.
我刚才看见他往南门那边走了。 wǒ gāng cái kàn jiàn tā wǎng nán mén nà biān zǒu le I just saw him walking towards the south gate.
你知道他去哪儿了吗？ nǐ zhī dào tā qù nǎ ér le ma Do you know where he went?
我不知道。他没看见我。我叫他的名字，他也没听见。 wǒ bù zhī dào。tā méi kàn jiàn wǒ。wǒ jiào tā de míng zi，tā yě méi tīng jiàn I don't know. He didn't see me. I called his name, but he didn't hear either.
你记住他的手机号了吗？我得给他打电话。 nǐ jì zhù tā de shǒu jī hào le ma？wǒ děi gěi tā dǎ diàn huà Did you memorize his phone number? I need to call him.
他关机了。 tā guān jī le He turned off his phone.
那你就给他发个短信。他一开机就会看见。 nà nǐ jiù gěi tā fā gè duǎn xìn。tā yī kāi jī jiù huì kàn jiàn Then send him a text message. He will see it as soon as he turns on his phone.
