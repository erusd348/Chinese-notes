📚 Grammar
房租 rent
房租太贵了，我想搬家。 fáng zū tài guì le, wǒ xiǎng bān jiā. The rent is too expensive, I want to move.
平时 usually
平时我很忙，周末有空。 píng shí wǒ hěn máng, zhōu mò yǒu kòng. I'm usually busy, free on weekends.
公寓 apartment
我住的公寓离公司很近。 wǒ zhù de gōng yù lí gōng sī hěn jìn. The apartment I live in is close to the company.
节省 to save
坐地铁可以节省时间。 zuò dì tiě kě yǐ jié shěng shí jiān. Taking the subway can save time.
搬家 to move house
下个月我要搬家。 xià ge yuè wǒ yào bān jiā. I'm moving next month.
有主见 to have one's own opinion
他很有主见，不太听别人的。 tā hěn yǒu zhǔ jiàn, bù tài tīng bié rén de. He has his own opinions and doesn't listen to others much.
辍学 to drop out
他17岁辍学，去工作了。 tā shí qī suì chuò xué, qù gōng zuò le. He dropped out at 17 and went to work.
阻止 to stop; to prevent
谁也阻止不了他。 shuí yě zǔ zhǐ bù liǎo tā. No one can stop him.
浪费时间 to waste time
别浪费时间，快工作吧。 bié làng fèi shí jiān, kuài gōng zuò ba. Don't waste time, get to work.
学费 tuition
英国的学费很贵。 yīng guó de xué fèi hěn guì. Tuition in the UK is expensive.
费 fee
这个费太高了。 zhè ge fèi tài gāo le. This fee is too high.
销售 sales
他在销售团队工作。 tā zài xiāo shòu tuán duì gōng zuò. He works in the sales team.
