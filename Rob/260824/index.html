📚 Grammar
A 是 A，但是/不过/就是 + 转折 (Structure) A is indeed A (concede), but... — admitting a quality then adding a contrast with 但是/不过/就是
苹果手机质量好是好，不过太贵了。 píngguǒ shǒujī zhìliàng hǎo shì hǎo, búguò tài guì le. The iPhone's quality is good, sure, but it's too expensive.
这个东西便宜是便宜，就是质量不好。 zhège dōngxi piányi shì piányi, jiùshì zhìliàng bù hǎo. This thing is cheap, sure, but the quality isn't good.
我们的狗可爱是可爱，但是不听话。 wǒmen de gǒu kě'ài shì kě'ài, dànshì bù tīnghuà. Our dog is cute, sure, but it's disobedient.
这个人好看是好看，但是不聪明。 zhège rén hǎokàn shì hǎokàn, dànshì bù cōngming. This person is good-looking, sure, but not smart.
📖 课文：点菜 (Text) Ordering food
这是菜单，请点菜。 zhè shì càidān, qǐng diǎn cài. Here's the menu. Please order.
来一个鱼香肉丝，一个麻婆豆腐，再来两碗米饭，一个酸辣汤。 lái yí ge yúxiāng ròusī, yí ge mápó dòufu, zài lái liǎng wǎn mǐfàn, yí ge suānlà tāng. One yuxiang pork, one mapo tofu, plus two bowls of rice and one hot and sour soup.
要什么饮料？ yào shénme yǐnliào? What drinks would you like?
来一壶茶。 lái yì hú chá. A pot of tea, please.
请稍等。 qǐng shāo děng. Please wait a moment.
