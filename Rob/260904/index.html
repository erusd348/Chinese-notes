📚 Grammar
Adj + 的（=名词） (Structure) Adj + 的 = "the ... one(s)" — an adjective used as a noun for food/kinds
我喜欢吃辣的。 wǒ xǐ huan chī là de. I like to eat the spicy ones (spicy food).
甜的我不想吃。 tián de wǒ bù xiǎng chī. I don't want the sweet ones.
请 + 人 + V + O (Structure) 请 + person + verb + object = to treat/invite sb to do sth; 今天我请 = it's on me today
我请你吃晚饭。 wǒ qǐng nǐ chī wǎn fàn. I'll treat you to dinner.
今天你请我，以后我请你。 jīn tiān nǐ qǐng wǒ, yǐ hòu wǒ qǐng nǐ. Today it's your treat; next time it's mine.
你请客吗？我请客，你付钱！ nǐ qǐng kè ma? wǒ qǐng kè, nǐ fù qián! Are you treating? I'll treat — and you pay!
还没 + V；我 + 来 + V (Structure) 还没 + V = haven't V yet; 我来 + V = "I'll get it" (volunteering to pay)
你还没付钱。我来付。 nǐ hái méi fù qián. wǒ lái fù. You haven't paid yet. — I'll get it.
还没买单呢。 hái méi mǎi dān ne. (We) haven't paid the bill yet.
唱 + 歌（VO） (Structure) 唱歌 = to sing (verb + object); also 唱情歌 / 唱中文歌
你喜欢唱歌吗？ nǐ xǐ huan chàng gē ma? Do you like singing?
我不喜欢唱歌。 wǒ bù xǐ huan chàng gē. I don't like singing.
再 + 数量词 (Structure) 再 + quantity = "another / some more..."
一张纸巾。 yī zhāng zhǐ jīn. A (sheet of) tissue.
一包纸巾。 yī bāo zhǐ jīn. A pack of tissues.
再来一杯咖啡。 zài lái yī bēi kā fēi. One more coffee, please.
📖 课文1：好吃是好吃，但是太油了 (Text) Tasty, sure — but too oily
你觉得中国菜好吃吗？ nǐ jué dé zhōng guó cài hǎo chī ma? Do you think Chinese food is tasty?
好吃是好吃，但是太油了。 hǎo chī shì hǎo chī, dàn shì tài yóu le. It's tasty, sure, but it's too oily.
你喜欢吃什么菜？ nǐ xǐ huan chī shén me cài? What kind of food do you like?
我最喜欢吃韩国菜。 wǒ zuì xǐ huan chī hán guó cài. I like Korean food the most.
我喜欢吃辣的。你呢？ wǒ xǐ huan chī là de. nǐ ne? I like spicy food. How about you?
酸的、辣的、咸的、苦的都喜欢吃，就是不喜欢吃甜的。 suān de, là de, xián de, kǔ de dōu xǐ huan chī, jiù shì bù xǐ huan chī tián de. Sour, spicy, salty, bitter — I like them all; I just don't like the sweet ones.
📖 课文2：服务员，买单！ (Text) Waiter, check please!
服务员，买单！ fú wù yuán, mǎi dān! Waiter, the check please!
再给我们两张餐巾纸。 zài gěi wǒ men liǎng zhāng cān jīn zhǐ. Give us two more napkins.
我来付钱，今天我请客。 wǒ lái fù qián, jīn tiān wǒ qǐng kè. I'll pay — it's my treat today.
好的，以后我请你吃韩国菜。 hǎo de, yǐ hòu wǒ qǐng nǐ chī hán guó cài. OK, next time I'll treat you to Korean food.
