English to Chinese

Translate each sentence into Chinese

1.
The service at this restaurant is excellent!
💡 💡 好极了 = extremely good
2.
This dish is a bit oily, but very tasty.
💡 💡 不过 = but (softer)
3.
I like this song very much.
💡 💡 这首歌 = this song
4.
Could you bring us two more cups of tea?
💡 💡 再 + 数量 = two more
5.
I'll pay today; you can treat me next time.
💡 💡 我来 + V = I'll get it

Chinese to English

Translate each sentence into English

1.

2.
