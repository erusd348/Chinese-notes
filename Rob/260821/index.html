📚 Grammar
S + 能 + V (Structure) S can / is able to do V
他能吃苦。 tā néng chī kǔ He can endure hardship.
有的人不能吃苦。 yǒu de rén bù néng chī kǔ Some people can't endure hardship.
只要 + S + V，就 + 结果 (Structure) as long as ... then ... (proverb pattern)
只要能吃苦，就有吃不完的苦。 zhǐ yào néng chī kǔ，jiù yǒu chī bù wán de kǔ As long as you can endure hardship, there will always be hardship to endure. (ironic proverb)
