📚 Grammar
听说 + 我听他说 + 句子 (hearsay) I heard (sb) say (that)...
我听我老婆说她要去夏威夷。 wǒ tīng wǒ lǎo pó shuō tā yào qù xià wēi yí I heard my wife say she's going to Hawaii.
我听我老婆说我们的狗饺子比包子听话。 wǒ tīng wǒ lǎo pó shuō wǒ men de gǒu jiǎo zi bǐ bāo zi tīng huà I heard my wife say our dog Dumpling is more obedient than Baozi.
这个孩子很听话。 zhè ge hái zi hěn tīng huà This child is very obedient.
听说四川菜很好吃。 tīng shuō sì chuān cài hěn hǎo chī I heard Sichuan food is delicious.
有事 / 没事 to have something to do / to be free; OK
我老婆在夏威夷有事。 wǒ lǎo pó zài xià wēi yí yǒu shì My wife has something to do in Hawaii.
你有事吗？ nǐ yǒu shì ma Do you have something to do?
我有事。 wǒ yǒu shì I have something to do.
你没事吧？ nǐ méi shì ba Are you OK?
我没事。 wǒ méi shì I'm fine.
服务 service
很高兴为您服务！ hěn gāo xìng wéi nín fú wù Glad to serve you!
谢谢你的服务。 xiè xie nǐ de fú wù Thank you for your service.
这个饭馆的服务很好。 zhè ge fàn guǎn de fú wù hěn hǎo This restaurant's service is very good.
这个酒店的服务很差。 zhè ge jiǔ diàn de fú wù hěn chà This hotel's service is very bad.
