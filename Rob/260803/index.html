📖 Vocabulary
带 dài to bring
皮带 pí dài belt
伞 sǎn umbrella
雨伞 yǔ sǎn umbrella (for rain)
太阳伞 tài yáng sǎn parasol
用 yòng to use
不用 bù yòng no need
算 suàn to calculate
计算机 jì suàn jī computer
📚 Grammar
带 + O + 去 + Place bring sb/sth to a place
我带午饭去办公室。 wǒ dài wǔ fàn qù bàn gōng shì I bring lunch to the office.
我带我老婆去中国旅行。 wǒ dài wǒ lǎo pó qù zhōng guó lǚ xíng I bring my wife to travel to China.
下雨的时候 + 要 + V when it rains, should + V
下雨的时候要带伞。 xià yǔ de shí hou yào dài sǎn You should bring an umbrella when it rains.
💬 Sentences
在美国，用太阳伞的人不多。 zài měi guó, yòng tài yáng sǎn de rén bù duō Not many people use parasols in America.
你算一下，一共多少钱？ nǐ suàn yī xià, yī gòng duō shǎo qián Calculate it, how much is it in total?
算了！ suàn le Forget it!
