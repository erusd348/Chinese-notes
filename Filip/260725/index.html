📚 Grammar
差不多 almost; about
我差不多学了7个月。 wǒ chà bu duō xué le qī ge yuè I have studied for about 7 months.
差点 nearly (negative connotation)
我差点死了。 wǒ chà diǎn sǐ le I almost died.
书面 written language
书面语和口语不一样。 shū miàn yǔ hé kǒu yǔ bù yī yàng Written language and spoken language are different.
英雄联盟 League of Legends
我晚上和朋友一起玩英雄联盟。 wǒ wǎn shàng hé péng yǒu yī qǐ wán yīng xióng lián méng I play League of Legends with friends at night.
除了 + N + 都/不/什么都 + V except... does nothing but...
我妹妹除了躺平摆烂，什么都不做。 wǒ mèi mei chú le tǎng píng bǎi làn, shén me dōu bù zuò My younger sister does nothing except lie flat and give up.
躺平 lying flat (slang)
年轻人不应该选择躺平。 nián qīng rén bù yīng gāi xuǎn zé tǎng píng Young people should not choose to lie flat.
摆烂 giving up (slang)
遇到困难就摆烂，不是好习惯。 yù dào kùn nán jiù bǎi làn, bù shì hǎo xí guàn Giving up when facing difficulties is not a good habit.
福利 welfare; benefits
瑞典的福利很好，有低保。 ruì diǎn de fú lì hěn hǎo, yǒu dī bǎo Swedens welfare is very good with minimum subsistence.
低保 minimum subsistence allowance
低保只能保障基本生活。 dī bǎo zhǐ néng bǎo zhàng jī běn shēng huó Minimum subsistence can only cover basic living.
税 tax
我要交很多税。 wǒ yào jiāo hěn duō shuì I have to pay a lot of tax.
分级收税 fēn jí shōu shuì progressive taxation
税收减免 shuì shōu jiǎn miǎn tax reduction
房租 rent
房租是每个月最大的开销。 fáng zū shì měi ge yuè zuì dà de kāi xiāo Rent is the biggest expense every month.
房贷 mortgage
房贷要还三十年。 fáng dài yào huán sān shí nián The mortgage needs to be paid for thirty years.
赡养 to support (elderly)
赡养老人是子女的责任。 shàn yǎng lǎo rén shì zǐ nǚ de zé rèn Supporting elderly parents is the childrens responsibility.
抚养 to raise (children)
抚养孩子的压力很大。 fǔ yǎng hái zi de yā lì hěn dà The pressure of raising children is very high.
减轻 to reduce; lighten
运动可以减轻压力。 yùn dòng kě yǐ jiǎn qīng yā lì Exercise can reduce stress.
压力 pressure; stress
工作压力太大了。 gōng zuò yā lì tài dà le The work pressure is too high.
间接 indirectly
间接经验也很重要。 jiàn jiē jīng yàn yě hěn zhòng yào Indirect experience is also very important.
抑郁症 depression
我哥哥大学毕业后得了抑郁症。 wǒ gē ge dà xué bì yè hòu dé le yì yù zhèng After graduation my older brother got depression.
治疗 treatment
现在他在接受治疗，每天都有好转。 xiàn zài tā zài jiē shòu zhì liáo, měi tiān dōu yǒu hǎo zhuǎn Now he is receiving treatment and improving every day.
就算不工作，政府也会给你钱。 jiù suàn bù gōng zuò, zhèng fǔ yě huì gěi nǐ qián Even if you do not work the government gives you money.
房租和房贷是最大的支出。 fáng zū hé fáng dài shì zuì dà de zhī chū Rent and mortgage are the biggest expenses.
