📚 Grammar
但是 but — expressing contrast
食堂的饭很好吃，但是很贵。 shí táng de fàn hěn hǎo chī, dàn shì hěn guì The canteen food is tasty, but expensive.
一边……一边…… doing two things at the same time
我一边工作一边学中文。 wǒ yī biān gōng zuò yī biān xué zhōng wén I work while studying Chinese.
我一边吃饭一边看电视。 wǒ yī biān chī fàn yī biān kàn diàn shì I eat while watching TV.
我喜欢在办公室一边吃午饭，一边上网。 wǒ xǐ huān zài bàn gōng shì yī biān chī wǔ fàn, yī biān shàng wǎng I like to eat lunch in my office while surfing the web.
附近 nearby
你们公司附近有什么？ nǐ men gōng sī fù jìn yǒu shén me What's near your company?
我们公司附近有饭店、体育馆、邮局和银行，但是没有医院。 wǒ men gōng sī fù jìn yǒu fàn diàn, tǐ yù guǎn, yóu jú hé yín háng, dàn shì méi yǒu yī yuàn Near our company there are restaurants, gym, post office and bank, but no hospital.
健康 / 便宜 / 新 / 旧 describing food and objects
我今天的午饭很健康。 wǒ jīn tiān de wǔ fàn hěn jiàn kāng My lunch today is very healthy.
三明治很便宜。 sān míng zhì hěn pián yí Sandwiches are cheap.
我的东西都很新，我没有旧的东西。 wǒ de dōng xi dōu hěn xīn, wǒ méi yǒu jiù de dōng xi All my things are new, I don't have old things.
食堂对话 canteen Q&A
你们公司有食堂吗？ nǐ men gōng sī yǒu shí táng ma Does your company have a canteen?
对，我们公司有食堂。 duì, wǒ men gōng sī yǒu shí táng Yes, our company has a canteen.
你的同事去食堂吃饭吗？ nǐ de tóng shì qù shí táng chī fàn ma Do your coworkers eat at the canteen?
对，他们去食堂吃饭。 duì, tā men qù shí táng chī fàn Yes, they eat at the canteen.
食堂里有什么吃的？ shí táng lǐ yǒu shén me chī de What food is in the canteen?
食堂里有意大利面、沙拉和三明治。 shí táng lǐ yǒu yì dà lì miàn, shā lā hé sān míng zhì There is pasta, salad and sandwiches.
在食堂，一份意大利面多少钱？ zài shí táng, yī fèn yì dà lì miàn duō shǎo qián How much is a serving of pasta at the canteen?
你为什么不去食堂吃饭？ nǐ wèi shén me bù qù shí táng chī fàn Why don't you eat at the canteen?
