📚 Grammar
办公室 / 办公 office / to work
我在办公室办公。 wǒ zài bàn gōng shì bàn gōng I work in the office.
你在哪儿办公？ nǐ zài nǎr bàn gōng Where do you work?
我有时候在家办公，有时候在公司办公。 wǒ yǒu shí hòu zài jiā bàn gōng, yǒu shí hòu zài gōng sī bàn gōng Sometimes I work from home, sometimes at the office.
昨天我去公司办公了。 zuó tiān wǒ qù gōng sī bàn gōng le Yesterday I went to the office to work.
栋 / 层 / 楼 measure words for buildings
我们公司500人都在一栋楼。 wǒ men gōng sī wǔ bǎi rén dōu zài yī dòng lóu Our company's 500 people are all in one building.
我家在1号楼3楼。 wǒ jiā zài yī hào lóu sān lóu My home is in Building 1, 3rd floor.
我办公室在3楼。 wǒ bàn gōng shì zài sān lóu My office is on the 3rd floor.
我们的办公楼有3层。 wǒ men de bàn gōng lóu yǒu sān céng Our office building has 3 floors.
你的卧室在几楼？ nǐ de wò shì zài jǐ lóu What floor is your bedroom on?
我的卧室在3楼。 wǒ de wò shì zài sān lóu My bedroom is on the 3rd floor.
💬 Sentences
你吃午饭了吗？ nǐ chī wǔ fàn le ma Have you had lunch?
我吃午饭了。 wǒ chī wǔ fàn le I've had lunch.
你吃了什么？ nǐ chī le shén me What did you eat?
我吃了三明治。 wǒ chī le sān míng zhì I ate a sandwich.
这个三明治是谁做的？ zhè gè sān míng zhì shì shuí zuò de Who made this sandwich?
这个三明治是我爱人做的。 zhè gè sān míng zhì shì wǒ ài rén zuò de My spouse made this sandwich.
这个三明治里有鸡肉和面包。 zhè gè sān míng zhì lǐ yǒu jī ròu hé miàn bāo This sandwich has chicken and bread inside.
这个三明治好吃吗？很好吃。 zhè gè sān míng zhì hǎo chī ma? hěn hǎo chī Is this sandwich tasty? Very tasty.
你吃午饭很早。 nǐ chī wǔ fàn hěn zǎo You eat lunch very early.
你中午常吃什么？ nǐ zhōng wǔ cháng chī shén me What do you usually eat at noon?
我每天中午都吃三明治。 wǒ měi tiān zhōng wǔ dōu chī sān míng zhì I eat sandwiches every day at noon.
我们晚上吃意大利菜。 wǒ men wǎn shàng chī yì dà lì cài We eat Italian food in the evening.
在瑞士有什么好吃的？ zài ruì shì yǒu shén me hǎo chī de What good food is there in Switzerland?
瑞士没有好吃的。 ruì shì méi yǒu hǎo chī de Switzerland has no good food.
