📖 Vocabulary

然后 rán hòu then; after that
从 cóng from

待 dāi to stay

📚 Grammar
先 + V，然后 + V first do A, then do B
我先坐飞机从上海到西安，然后坐火车从西安到成都。 wǒ xiān zuò fēi jī cóng shàng hǎi dào xī ān, rán hòu zuò huǒ chē cóng xī ān dào chéng dū First I fly from Shanghai to Xi'an, then take the train from Xi'an to Chengdu.
从 + place + 到 + place from place to place
从上海到西安。 cóng shàng hǎi dào xī ān From Shanghai to Xi'an.
我家附近有图书馆。 wǒ jiā fù jìn yǒu tú shū guǎn There's a library near my home.
我们不常去图书馆。我们在网上买书。 wǒ men bù cháng qù tú shū guǎn, wǒ men zài wǎng shàng mǎi shū We don't often go to the library. We buy books online.
我的书很多。 wǒ de shū hěn duō I have many books.

📝 Class Homework
1.
We first fly to Beijing, then take the train to Shanghai.
💡 先 / 坐飞机 / 北京 / 然后 / 坐火车 / 上海
2.
Is there a library near your home?
💡 你家 / 附近 / 有 / 图书馆 / 吗
3.
I often borrow books from the library.
💡 常 / 去 / 图书馆 / 借书
4.
My books are very many. I buy them online.
💡 书 / 很多 / 网上 / 买
5.
We stay in Chengdu for about five days.
💡 在 / 成都 / 待 / 大概 / 五天
6.
I don't often go to the library.
💡 不常 / 去 / 图书馆
7.
They have no vacation.
💡 没有 / 假期
8.
We'll stay in China for about 22 to 23 days.
💡 在 / 中国 / 大概 / 待 / 22到23天
9.
He likes to read business books.
💡 喜欢 / 看 / 商业 / 书
10.
First take the subway, then take the bus.
💡 先 / 坐地铁 / 然后 / 坐公交
