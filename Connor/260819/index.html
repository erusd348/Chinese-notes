📚 Grammar
在 V (Structure) be doing (action in progress)
我刚刚在工作。 wǒ gāng gāng zài gōng zuò I was just working.
……怎么说？ (Structure) how to say ...
这个生词怎么说？ zhè ge shēng cí zěn me shuō How do you say this new word?
Time + Location + Action (Structure) time, location, action
老师今天在教室教我中文。 lǎo shī jīn tiān zài jiào shì jiāo wǒ zhōng wén The teacher taught me Chinese in the classroom today.
Adj + 极了 (Structure) extremely + adj
好极了。 hǎo jí le Extremely good.
漂亮极了。 piào liang jí le Extremely pretty.
