📚 Grammar
A 比 B + Adj (Structure) A is more adj than B
你比我高。 nǐ bǐ wǒ gāo. You are taller than me.
B 没有 A + Adj (Structure) B is not as adj as A
我没有你高。 wǒ méi yǒu nǐ gāo. I am not as tall as you.
澳大利亚人没有中国人多。 ào dà lì yà rén méi yǒu zhōng guó rén duō. There are not as many Australians as Chinese.
食堂没有外面贵。 shí táng méi yǒu wài miàn guì. The canteen is not as expensive as eating out.
没（有）+ V (Structure) 没 + verb = did not / have not (done)
我没（有）吃饭。 wǒ méi yǒu chī fàn. I have not eaten.
太 + Adj + 了 (Structure) too + adj (emphatic)
今天太冷了。 jīn tiān tài lěng le. It's too cold today.
不太 + Adj (Structure) not very + adj
今天不太冷。 jīn tiān bú tài lěng. It's not very cold today.
