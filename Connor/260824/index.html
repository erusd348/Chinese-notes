📚 Grammar
V + 了 completed action (了 marks it done)
你今天做什么了？ nǐ jīn tiān zuò shén me le What did you do today?
我今天上了一节安全课。 wǒ jīn tiān shàng le yī jié ān quán kè I attended a safety class today.
你学了什么？ nǐ xué le shén me What did you learn?
还没 + V have not done yet
你吃晚饭了吗？ nǐ chī wǎn fàn le ma Have you eaten dinner?
我还没吃晚饭。 wǒ hái méi chī wǎn fàn I haven't eaten dinner yet.
我下课后吃。 wǒ xià kè hòu chī I'll eat after class.
