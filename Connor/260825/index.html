📚 Grammar
A place + 有 + N (Structure) There is/are N in A place
我这栋楼里有一个食堂。 wǒ zhè dòng lóu lǐ yǒu yí gè shí táng There is a cafeteria in this building of mine.
我家在42号楼2楼305室。 wǒ jiā zài sì shí èr hào lóu èr lóu sān líng wǔ shì My home is at building 42, 2nd floor, room 305.
悉尼有很多栋大楼。 xī ní yǒu hěn duō dòng dà lóu Sydney has many big buildings.
你这栋楼有多少楼？ nǐ zhè dòng lóu yǒu duō shǎo lóu How many floors does your building have?
我这栋楼有10楼。 wǒ zhè dòng lóu yǒu shí lóu My building has 10 floors.
每楼有多少户？ měi lóu yǒu duō shǎo hù How many households are there on each floor?
A 比 B + Adj + 一点/多 (Structure) A is a bit / much more Adj than B
他比我高一点。 tā bǐ wǒ gāo yì diǎn He is a bit taller than me.
中国人比澳大利亚人多很多。 zhōng guó rén bǐ ào dà lì yà rén duō hěn duō Chinese people are much more numerous than Australians.
食堂比外面便宜，也更差。 shí táng bǐ wài miàn pián yi，yě gèng chà The cafeteria is cheaper than outside, but also worse.
食堂里的吃的好吃吗？ shí táng lǐ de chī de hǎo chī ma Is the food in the cafeteria tasty?
不贵，一顿饭七块。 bù guì，yí dùn fàn qī kuài It's not expensive — one meal costs 7 yuan.
我很饿。 wǒ hěn è I'm very hungry.
我饿了。 wǒ è le I'm hungry (now).
