📚 Grammar
N + 数字 + 长 / 宽 / 高 / 厚 (Structure) measure: [object] is [number] long/wide/high/thick
我的电脑桌1米8长，零点七米宽，零点九米高，20厘米厚。 wǒ de diàn nǎo zhuō yì mǐ bā cháng，líng diǎn qī mǐ kuān，líng diǎn jiǔ mǐ gāo，èr shí lí mǐ hòu My computer desk is 1.8 m long, 0.7 m wide, 0.9 m high and 20 cm thick.
很 + Adj (Structure) very + adjective
我的卧室很小、很干净、很漂亮。 wǒ de wò shì hěn xiǎo、hěn gān jìng、hěn piào liang My bedroom is very small, very clean and very pretty.
这辆车很长。 zhè liàng chē hěn cháng This car is very long.
我的肩膀很宽。 wǒ de jiān bǎng hěn kuān My shoulders are very wide.
我的书很厚。 wǒ de shū hěn hòu My book is very thick.
我的衣服不厚。 wǒ de yī fu bù hòu My clothes are not thick.
比较 + Adj (Structure) relatively + adjective
我女朋友头发比较长。 wǒ nǚ péng you tóu fa bǐ jiào cháng My girlfriend's hair is relatively long.
