📚 Grammar
搞笑 funny; hilarious
他的玩笑很搞笑。 tā de wán xiào hěn gǎo xiào His joke is very funny.
开玩笑 to joke; to make fun of
她喜欢开玩笑，但是有时候过分了。 tā xǐ huān kāi wán xiào, dàn shì yǒu shí hou guò fèn le She likes to joke, but sometimes goes too far.
实习 to intern; internship
我当时在彭博社实习。 wǒ dāng shí zài péng bó shè shí xí I interned at Bloomberg at that time.
大部分 most; the majority of
大部分同事还是很友好的。 dà bù fen tóng shì hái shì hěn yǒu hǎo de Most of my colleagues are still quite friendly.
离开 to leave; to depart
大部分人还在彭博社，我和另一个离开了。 dà bù fen rén hái zài péng bó shè, wǒ hé lìng yī gè lí kāi le Most people are still at Bloomberg; me and another one left.
顾客 customer; client
我们是顾客。 wǒ men shì gù kè We are customers.
欢迎光临 Welcome! (store greeting)
欢迎光临我们店！ huān yíng guāng lín wǒ men diàn Welcome to our store!
蓬荜生辉 to bring honor to a humble home
你的到来让我家蓬荜生辉。 nǐ de dào lái ràng wǒ jiā péng bì shēng huī Your visit makes my humble home shine!
交费 to pay fees
我要交学费。 wǒ yào jiāo xué fèi I need to pay tuition.
每个月都要交房租。 měi gè yuè dōu yào jiāo fáng zū I have to pay rent every month.
别忘了交电费。 bié wàng le jiāo diàn fèi Don't forget to pay the electricity bill.
女士们先生们 ladies and gentlemen
女士们先生们，请坐下。 nǚ shì men xiān shēng men, qǐng zuò xià Ladies and gentlemen, please have a seat.
