📚 Grammar
刚 / 刚才 just now — 刚 is adv before verb; 刚才 can start a sentence
我刚吃早饭了。 wǒ gāng chī zǎo fàn le I just ate breakfast.
刚才我吃早饭了。 gāng cái wǒ chī zǎo fàn le I just ate breakfast (a moment ago).
我今天刚买了一件衣服。 wǒ jīn tiān gāng mǎi le yí jiàn yī fu I just bought a piece of clothing today.
我去年刚来中国。 wǒ qù nián gāng lái zhōng guó I came to China just last year.
我上个月刚来这家公司。 wǒ shàng gè yuè gāng lái zhè jiā gōng sī I came to this company just last month.
正在 + V (progressive) to be doing…
这家公司正在招聘新的程序员。 zhè jiā gōng sī zhèng zài zhāo pìn xīn de chéng xù yuán This company is recruiting new programmers.
招聘 / 应聘 / 聘用 recruit / apply / hire
他听说这家公司正在招聘新的程序员，所以他去应聘了。 tā tīng shuō zhè jiā gōng sī zhèng zài zhāo pìn xīn de chéng xù yuán, suǒ yǐ tā qù yìng pìn le He heard this company is recruiting new programmers, so he went to apply.
面试后，公司聘用了他。 miàn shì hòu, gōng sī pìn yòng le tā After the interview, the company hired him.
他打算去这家公司工作。 tā dǎ suàn qù zhè jiā gōng sī gōng zuò He plans to work at this company.
