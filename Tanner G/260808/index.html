📚 Grammar
一边 + V + 一边 + V while doing A, also doing B
我一边吃饭一边看电视。 wǒ yì biān chī fàn yì biān kàn diàn shì I eat while watching TV.
我一边上大学一边工作。 wǒ yì biān shàng dà xué yì biān gōng zuò I go to university while working.
我一边做晚饭一边给我女朋友打电话。 wǒ yì biān zuò wǎn fàn yì biān gěi wǒ nǚ péng you dǎ diàn huà I make dinner while calling my girlfriend.
