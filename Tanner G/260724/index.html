💬 Sentences
苹果手机不仅价格贵，而且电池小。 ping guo shou ji bu jin jia ge gui, er qie dian chi xiao iPhones are not only expensive, but also have small batteries.
公司的早饭不仅好吃，而且免费。 gong si de zao fan bu jin hao chi, er qie mian fei The company breakfast is not only tasty, but also free.
这个问题很难，连我都不知道。 zhe ge wen ti hen nan, lian wo dou bu zhi dao This problem is so hard, even I do not know.
这个太难吃了，连狗都不吃。 zhe ge tai nan chi le, lian gou dou bu chi This is so bad, even the dog will not eat it.
这个问题这么简单，连猪都知道，但是你不知道。 zhe ge wen ti zhe me jian dan, lian zhu dou zhi dao, dan shi ni bu zhi dao This problem is so simple, even a pig knows it, but you do not.
我早就起床了。 wo zao jiu qi chuang le I got up long ago.
我早就有女朋友了。 wo zao jiu you nv peng you le I have had a girlfriend for a long time already.
我早就开始学中文了。 wo zao jiu kai shi xue zhong wen le I started learning Chinese long ago.
我早就告诉你了。 wo zao jiu gao su ni le I told you long ago.
我这栋楼有40层。 wo zhe dong lou you si shi ceng This building of mine has 40 floors.
有人每天送早饭到我这层楼。 you ren mei tian song zao fan dao wo zhe ceng lou Someone delivers breakfast to my floor every day.
我们在工位上吃早饭。 wo men zai gong wei shang chi zao fan We eat breakfast at our workstations.
因为公司想让我们工作更长时间。 yin wei gong si xiang rang wo men gong zuo geng chang shi jian Because the company wants us to work longer hours.
我可以省两顿饭的钱。 wo ke yi sheng liang dun fan de qian I can save two meals worth of money.
这个工作更好玩。 zhe ge gong zuo geng hao wan This job is more fun.
你的压力大吗？ ni de ya li da ma Is your pressure high?
你应该对你自己有信心。 ni ying gai dui ni zi ji you xin xin You should have confidence in yourself.
金子在哪儿都发光。 jin zi zai nar dou fa guang Gold shines everywhere.
如果公司炒我的鱿鱼也没关系。 ru guo gong si chao wo de you yu ye mei guan xi If the company fires me, it is no big deal.
我们排队1小时才买到这双打折的鞋。 wo men pai dui yi xiao shi cai mai dao zhe shuang da zhe de xie We queued for an hour to buy these discounted shoes.
如果她不这么挑，她早就结婚了。 ru guo ta bu zhe me tiao, ta zao jiu jie hun le If she were not so picky, she would have gotten married long ago.
她连哪一排都挑。 ta lian na yi pai dou tiao She is even picky about which row.

📝 Class Homework
1. This building not only has 50 floors, but also has a gym.
💡 not only / floors / but also / gym
2. I can save a lot of money by eating at the company.
💡 save / money / company
3. This problem is so hard, even the teacher does not know.
💡 even / teacher / does not know
4. He not only works fast, but also works well.
💡 not only / fast / but also / well
5. I told you I wanted to learn Chinese long ago.
💡 long ago / told / Chinese
6. Her stress at work is very high; she often works until 10 PM.
💡 stress / high / works until / 10pm
7. If the company fires me, I will just go traveling.
💡 fires / just go / traveling
8. Even a child can do this, but you cannot.
💡 even / child / can / cannot
9. This restaurant is not only cheap, but also tasty.
💡 not only / cheap / but also / tasty
10. He was fired by the company long ago.
💡 long ago / by company / fired

Write order: top→bottom, left→right, outside→inside
