📖 Vocabulary
层 céng floor (measure word for storeys)
栋 dòng building (measure word)
工位 gōng wèi workstation; desk (at work)
省 shěng to save (money/time)
出差 chū chāi to travel for business
压力 yā lì pressure; stress
信心 xìn xīn confidence
金子 jīn zi gold
发光 fā guāng to shine; to glow
卷王 juǎn wáng workaholic; overachiever (slang)
炒鱿鱼 chǎo yóu yú to fire (slang, lit. stir-fry squid)
不仅…而且… bù jǐn... ér qiě... not only... but also...
连…都… lián... dōu... even... (emphasis)
早就…了 zǎo jiù... le long ago; already
📚 Grammar
层 / 栋 / 工位 floor / building / workstation
我这栋楼有40层。 wǒ zhè dòng lóu yǒu sì shí céng This building has 40 floors.
有人每天送早饭到我这层楼。 yǒu rén měi tiān sòng zǎo fàn dào wǒ zhè céng lóu Someone delivers breakfast to my floor every day.
我们在工位上吃早饭。 wǒ men zài gōng wèi shàng chī zǎo fàn We eat breakfast at our workstations.
因为公司想让我们工作更长时间。 yīn wèi gōng sī xiǎng ràng wǒ men gōng zuò gèng cháng shí jiān Because the company wants us to work longer hours.
我可以省两顿饭的钱。 wǒ kě yǐ shěng liǎng dùn fàn de qián I can save money on two meals.
出差 / 压力 / 信心 business trip / pressure / confidence
这个工作很好玩。 zhè gè gōng zuò hěn hǎo wán This job is very fun.
你的压力大吗？ nǐ de yā lì dà ma Is your stress level high?
你应该对你自己有信心。 nǐ yīng gāi duì nǐ zì jǐ yǒu xìn xīn You should have confidence in yourself.
金子在哪儿都发光。 jīn zi zài nǎr dōu fā guāng Gold shines everywhere.
如果公司炒我的鱿鱼也没关系。 rú guǒ gōng sī chǎo wǒ de yóu yú yě méi guān xì It's fine even if the company fires me.
不仅…而且… not only... but also...
苹果手机不仅价格贵，而且电池小。 píng guǒ shǒu jī bù jǐn jià gé guì, ér qiě diàn chí xiǎo The iPhone is not only expensive, but also has a small battery.
公司的早饭不仅好吃，而且免费。 gōng sī de zǎo fàn bù jǐn hǎo chī, ér qiě miǎn fèi The company breakfast is not only delicious, but also free.
连…都… even...
她连哪一排都挑。 tā lián nǎ yī pái dōu tiāo She's picky even about which row.
这个问题很难，连我都不知道。 zhè gè wèn tí hěn nán, lián wǒ dōu bù zhī dào This question is so hard, even I don't know.
这个太难吃了，连狗都不吃。 zhè gè tài nán chī le, lián gǒu dōu bù chī This is so terrible, even the dog won't eat it.
这个问题这么简单，连猪都知道，但是你不知道。 zhè gè wèn tí zhè me jiǎn dān, lián zhū dōu zhī dào, dàn shì nǐ bù zhī dào This question is so simple, even a pig knows it, but you don't.
早就…了 long ago; already
我早就起床了。 wǒ zǎo jiù qǐ chuáng le I got up long ago.
我早就有女朋友了。 wǒ zǎo jiù yǒu nǚ péng yǒu le I've had a girlfriend for a long time.
我早就开始学中文了。 wǒ zǎo jiù kāi shǐ xué zhōng wén le I started learning Chinese long ago.
我早就告诉你了。 wǒ zǎo jiù gào su nǐ le I told you long ago.
