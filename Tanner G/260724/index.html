📝 Class Homework
1. This building not only has 50 floors, but also has a gym.
💡 not only / floors / but also / gym
2. I can save a lot of money by eating at the company.
💡 save / money / company
3. This problem is so hard, even the teacher does not know.
💡 even / teacher / does not know
4. He not only works fast, but also works well.
💡 not only / fast / but also / well
5. I told you I wanted to learn Chinese long ago.
💡 long ago / told / Chinese
6. Her stress at work is very high; she often works until 10 PM.
💡 stress / high / works until / 10pm
7. If the company fires me, I will just go traveling.
💡 fires / just go / traveling
8. Even a child can do this, but you cannot.
💡 even / child / can / cannot
9. This restaurant is not only cheap, but also tasty.
💡 not only / cheap / but also / tasty
10. He was fired by the company long ago.
💡 long ago / by company / fired

Write order: top→bottom, left→right, outside→inside
