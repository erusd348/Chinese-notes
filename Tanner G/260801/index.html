📚 Grammar
长大 to grow up
在城市长大的朋友不喜欢这个电影。 zài chéng shì zhǎng dà de péng yǒu bù xǐ huān zhè gè diàn yǐng A friend who grew up in the city doesn't like this movie.
你长大了！ nǐ zhǎng dà le You've grown up!
过分 too much; excessive
他的玩笑太过（分）了。 tā de wán xiào tài guò fèn le His joke went too far.
你过分了！ nǐ guò fèn le You've gone too far!
他开玩笑开得太过分了。 tā kāi wán xiào kāi dé tài guò fèn le He took the joke way too far.
过头 over — result complement
他开玩笑开过头了。 tā kāi wán xiào kāi guò tóu le His joke went too far.
我睡过头了。 wǒ shuì guò tóu le I overslept.
随便 + V casually; as one pleases
随便吃。 suí biàn chī Eat whatever you want.
随便说。 suí biàn shuō Say whatever you want.
你想吃什么？随便。什么都可以。 nǐ xiǎng chī shén me? suí biàn. shén me dōu kě yǐ What do you want to eat? Anything. Whatever is fine.
他是一个很随便的人。 tā shì yī gè hěn suí biàn de rén He is a very casual person.
没有深度 no depth
这个电影没有深度。 zhè gè diàn yǐng méi yǒu shēn dù This movie has no depth.
白得 + V so white that...
你白得发光。 nǐ bái dé fā guāng You glow with whiteness.
永远 forever
我们永远是朋友。 wǒ men yǒng yuǎn shì péng yǒu We are friends forever.
