你是小叶吧?nǐ shì xiǎo yè ba?You're Xiaoye, right?

莉莉：太好了!我一吃完晚饭就去。lì lì： tài hǎo le! wǒ yī chī wán wǎn fàn jiù qù。Lily: Great! I'll go as soon as I finish dinner.

Dialogue 3
小雨：你看见保罗了没有?xiǎo yǔ： nǐ kàn jiàn bǎo luó le méi yǒu?Xiaoyu: Have you seen Paul?
我有急事要找他。wǒ yǒu jí shì yào zhǎo tā。I need to talk to him urgently.
英男：刚才我看见他往南门那边走了。yīng nán： gāng cái wǒ kàn jiàn tā wǎng nán mén nà biān zǒu le。Yingnan: I saw him heading toward the south gate.
小雨：你知道他去哪儿了吗?xiǎo yǔ： nǐ zhī dao tā qù nǎ ér le ma?Xiaoyu: Do you know where he went?
英男：不知道。yīng nán： bù zhī dao。Yingnan: I don't know.
他没看见我。tā méi kàn jiàn wǒ。He didn't see me.
我叫他，他也没听见。wǒ jiào tā， tā yě méi tīng jiàn。I called him, but he didn't hear me.
小雨：你记住他的手机号了吗?xiǎo yǔ： nǐ jì zhù tā de shǒu jī hào le ma?Xiaoyu: Do you remember his phone number?

小雨：他关机了。xiǎo yǔ： tā guān jī le。Xiaoyu: His phone is off.
英男：那你就给他发个短信。yīng nán： nà nǐ jiù gěi tā fā gè duǎn xìn。Yingnan: Then send him a text.

📚 Grammar
1. Complement of result
Verb + Result Complement（了 / 没 + Verb + Complement）
A complement of result expressed either by a verb or an adjective, indicates the result of an action. A verb and its complement of result are closely linked to each other and don't allow the insertion of another element between them, e.g.
你打错了。nǐ dǎ cuò le。You dialled the wrong number.
这本书我看懂了。zhè běn shū wǒ kàn dǒng le。I understood this book.
Generally speaking, a verb followed by a complement of result indicates that the action is finished. Therefore “没(有)” is used to make this type of structure negative. The form of affirmative-negative question with a complement of result is made by using “……没有”, e.g.
你做对了吗？nǐ zuò duì le ma？Did you get it right?
没做对。méi zuò duì。No, I didn’t.
你听懂了没有？nǐ tīng dǒng le méi yǒu？Did you understand?
