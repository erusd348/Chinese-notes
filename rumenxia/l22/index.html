Dialogue 1
这是英男和保罗的房间，门开着，电视也开着。zhè shì yīng nán hé bǎo luó de fáng jiān， mén kāi zhe， diàn shì yě kāi zhe。This is Yingnan and Paul's room. The door is open and the TV is on.
书和词典在书架上放着，保罗在椅子上坐着，英男在床上躺着，他们正在紧张地看着电视里的足球赛。shū hé cí diǎn zài shū jià shàng fàng zhe， bǎo luó zài yǐ zi shàng zuò zhe， yīng nán zài chuáng shàng tǎng zhe， tā men zhèng zài jǐn zhāng dì kàn zhe diàn shì lǐ de zú qiú sài。Books and dictionaries are on the shelf. Paul is sitting on the chair and Yingnan is lying on the bed. They're watching a soccer match on TV with excitement.

📝 Notes
太……了 — High Degree
太……了 indicates a high degree.
太好了tài hǎo leGreat!
麻烦您…… — Asking for Help
麻烦您…… is a common polite way to ask someone for help.
麻烦您转告他一件事má fan nín zhuǎn gào tā yī jiàn shìCould you please pass a message to him?
没问题 — ‘No Problem’
没问题 is used to respond to a question or request, confirming that you can do it.
📚 Grammar
1. Continuous aspect 着
Verb + 着
The aspect particle “着” placed after a verb indicates the continuation of an action or state.
门开着。mén kāi zhe。The door is open.
他穿着一件红衣服。tā chuān zhe yī jiàn hóng yī fu。He is wearing a red garment.
墙上挂着一幅画。qiáng shàng guà zhe yī fú huà。A painting is hanging on the wall.
2. Structural particle 地
Adj. + 地 + Verb
“地” is used after an adjective to modify a verb, indicating the manner of the action.
他慢慢地走过来。tā màn màn de zǒu guò lái。He walked over slowly.
我们高兴地唱了这首歌。wǒ men gāo xìng de chàng le zhè shǒu gē。We sang this song happily.
他认真地说：“对不起。”tā rèn zhēn de shuō：“ duì bù qǐ。”He said earnestly, ‘I’m sorry.’.
3. Adjective reduplication
AA（e.g. 红红 / 大大）
Single-syllable adjectives can be reduplicated to indicate a high degree or emotional coloring. Format: AA (e.g. “红红”, “大大”).
今天天气暖暖的。jīn tiān tiān qì nuǎn nuǎn de。The weather is nice and warm today.
他的眼睛大大的。tā de yǎn jīng dà dà de。His eyes are big.
这个房间小小巧巧的。zhè ge fáng jiān xiǎo xiǎo qiǎo qiǎo de。This room is small and cosy.
