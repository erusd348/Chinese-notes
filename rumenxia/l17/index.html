💬 Dialogue
Dialogue 1
小叶：刚才你去哪儿了?xiǎo yè： gāng cái nǐ qù nǎ ér le?Xiaoye: Where did you go just now?
莉莉：我跟直美逛商店去了。lì lì： wǒ gēn zhí měi guàng shāng diàn qù le。Lily: I went shopping with Naomi.
小叶：你买东西了吗?xiǎo yè： nǐ mǎi dōng xī le ma?Xiaoye: Did you buy anything?
莉莉：我没买东西，直美买了。lì lì： wǒ méi mǎi dōng xī， zhí měi mǎi le。Lily: I didn't buy anything, but Naomi did.
小叶：她买了什么东西?xiǎo yè： tā mǎi le shén me dōng xī?Xiaoye: What did she buy?
莉莉：她买了三件衬衣、两条裙子，还有一顶帽子。lì lì： tā mǎi le sān jiàn chèn yī、 liǎng tiáo qún zi， hái yǒu yī dǐng mào zi。Lily: She bought three shirts, two skirts, and a hat.
小叶：嗬，买了这么多!xiǎo yè： hē， mǎi le zhè me duō!Xiaoye: Wow, that's a lot!
Dialogue 2
小叶：周末你做什么了?xiǎo yè： zhōu mò nǐ zuò shén me le?Xiaoye: What did you do on the weekend?
莉莉：我朋友来北京了，周末我陪他去长城了。lì lì： wǒ péng yǒu lái běi jīng le， zhōu mò wǒ péi tā qù cháng chéng le。Lily: My friend came to Beijing. I took him to the Great Wall.
小叶：你们去颐和园了没有?xiǎo yè： nǐ men qù yí hé yuán le méi yǒu?Xiaoye: Did you go to the Summer Palace?
我觉得颐和园的风景特别美。wǒ jué de yí hé yuán de fēng jǐng tè bié měi。I think the scenery at the Summer Palace is especially beautiful.
莉莉：还没去呢。lì lì： hái méi qù ne。Lily: Not yet.
我们打算后天去。wǒ men dǎ suàn hòu tiān qù。We plan to go the day after tomorrow.
小叶：你请他吃北京烤鸭了吗?xiǎo yè： nǐ qǐng tā chī běi jīng kǎo yā le ma?Xiaoye: Did you treat him to Peking duck?
莉莉：那还用说。lì lì： nà hái yòng shuō。Lily: That goes without saying.
昨天我们在全聚德吃了一大只烤鸭。zuó tiān wǒ men zài quán jù dé chī le yī dà zhǐ kǎo yā。We had a whole Peking duck at Quanjude yesterday.

咱们抽时间去看看他吧。zán men chōu shí jiān qù kàn kàn tā ba。Let's find time to go see him.

📝 Notes
那还用说 — ‘That Goes Without Saying’
那还用说 is a colloquial expression used as an affirmative answer. It has the same meaning as 当然了 (of course).
了 Indicating Change
The modal particle 了 indicates that the situation has changed — he wasn’t sick before, but now he is.
听说小雨病了tīng shuō xiǎo yǔ bìng leI heard Xiaoyu is sick.
📚 Grammar
1. Aspect particle 了
Verb + 了 (+ Object)
The particle placed after a verb and expressing the period during which the action takes place is called an aspect particle. The aspect particle “了” placed after a verb denotes that the action is accomplished, e.g.
她买了三件衬衫、两条裙子，还有一顶帽子。tā mǎi le sān jiàn chèn shān、 liǎng tiáo qún zi， hái yǒu yī dǐng mào zi。She bought three shirts, two skirts, and a hat.
昨天我们在全聚德吃了一只烤鸭。zuó tiān wǒ men zài quán jù dé chī le yī zhī kǎo yā。Yesterday we ate a roast duck at Quanjude.
The negative form of the sentences of this type is to add “没(有)” before the verb and to omit “了”, e.g.
我没买东西，玛丽买了。wǒ méi mǎi dōng xi， mǎ lì mǎi le。I didn’t buy anything — Mary did.
If an action may happen or will certainly happen, but now it has not happened yet, one can use “还没(有)……呢”, e.g.
你们去颐和园了没有？nǐ men qù yí hé yuán le méi yǒu？Did you go to the Summer Palace?
还没去呢。hái méi qù ne。Not yet.
你已经告诉小雨了吗？nǐ yǐ jīng gào sù xiǎo yǔ le ma？Have you already told Xiaoyu?
还没呢，我下了课就给他打电话。hái méi ne， wǒ xià le kè jiù gěi tā dǎ diàn huà。Not yet — I’ll call him as soon as class is over.
The form of affirmative-negative question is:
安娜来了没有？ān nà lái le méi yǒu？Has Anna come?
昨天的作业你做了没有？zuó tiān de zuò yè nǐ zuò le méi yǒu？Did you do yesterday’s homework?
Note A. The accomplishment of the action just expresses the period of the action, and has nothing to do with the time when the action happens (past, present or future).
Note B. As for the actions taking place in the past, if they have happened frequently or if there is no need to emphasize that they have happened in the past, one doesn't add “了” after the verb, e.g.
以前他常常来我家玩儿。yǐ qián tā cháng cháng lái wǒ jiā wán ér。He used to come over to my place often.
去年八月我在北京学习汉语。qù nián bā yuè wǒ zài běi jīng xué xí hàn yǔ。Last August I was studying Chinese in Beijing.
Note C. If the verbs followed by “了” have objects, there are usually quantifiers or other attributives in the objects, e.g.
我买了一本书。wǒ mǎi le yī běn shū。I bought a book.
我又认识了几个新朋友。wǒ yòu rèn shi le jǐ gè xīn péng you。I made a few more new friends.
If the objects are simple ones, “了” is often placed at the end of the sentence, e.g.
上个星期你去哪儿了？shàng gè xīng qī nǐ qù nǎ ér le？Where did you go last week?
我朋友来北京了，周末我陪他去长城了。wǒ péng you lái běi jīng le， zhōu mò wǒ péi tā qù cháng chéng le。My friend came to Beijing, and at the weekend I went with him to the Great Wall.
2. …… 了 …… 就 ……
V1 + 了 …… 就 + V2
The construction “ …… 了 …… 就 …… ” indicates that two actions take place one after the other, e.g.
我下了课就给他打电话。wǒ xià le kè jiù gěi tā dǎ diàn huà。I’ll call him as soon as class is over.
明天我吃了早饭就去找你。míng tiān wǒ chī le zǎo fàn jiù qù zhǎo nǐ。Tomorrow I’ll come and find you right after breakfast.
