Dialogue 1
田中：你能不能把你的自行车借给我用用?tián zhōng： nǐ néng bù néng bǎ nǐ de zì xíng chē jiè gěi wǒ yòng yòng?Tanaka: Could you lend me your bike?
英男：没问题。yīng nán： méi wèn tí。Yingnan: No problem.
田中：你把自行车放在哪儿了?tián zhōng： nǐ bǎ zì xíng chē fàng zài nǎ ér le?Tanaka: Where did you park your bike?
英男：放在楼下的车棚里了。yīng nán： fàng zài lóu xià de chē péng lǐ le。Yingnan: In the bike shed downstairs.
田中：我用完就马上还给你。tián zhōng： wǒ yòng wán jiù mǎ shàng huán gěi nǐ。Tanaka: I'll return it as soon as I'm done.
英男：没关系，不用着急。yīng nán： méi guān xì， bù yòng zháo jí。Yingnan: That's fine, no rush.
Dialogue 2
田中：要是方便的话，明天你去办公室时,帮我把这张表送到办公室。tián zhōng： yào shì fāng biàn de huà， míng tiān nǐ qù bàn gōng shì shí, bāng wǒ bǎ zhè zhāng biǎo sòng dào bàn gōng shì。Tanaka: If it's convenient, when you go to the office tomorrow, help me deliver this form.
把它交给谁?bǎ tā jiāo gěi shuí?Who should I give it to?
田中：把它交给赵老师。tián zhōng： bǎ tā jiāo gěi zhào lǎo shī。Tanaka: Give it to Teacher Zhao.
英男：要是赵老师不在，怎么办?yīng nán： yào shì zhào lǎo shī bù zài， zěn me bàn?Yingnan: What if Teacher Zhao isn't there?
田中：赵老师不在的话，你就把表放在她的桌子上。tián zhōng： zhào lǎo shī bù zài de huà， nǐ jiù bǎ biǎo fàng zài tā de zhuō zi shàng。Tanaka: If she's not there, just put the form on her desk.
英男：好，我知道了。yīng nán： hǎo， wǒ zhī dào le。Yingnan: Okay, got it.
田中：你别忘了。tián zhōng： nǐ bié wàng le。Tanaka: Don't forget.
英男：放心吧，忘不了。yīng nán： fàng xīn ba， wàng bù liǎo。Yingnan: Don't worry, I won't forget.
Dialogue 3
田中：我想请你帮个忙。tián zhōng： wǒ xiǎng qǐng nǐ bāng gè máng。Tanaka: I'd like to ask a favor.
小雨：什么事?xiǎo yǔ： shén me shì?Xiaoyu: What is it?
田中：请你帮我把这篇文章翻译成英文，好吗?tián zhōng： qǐng nǐ bāng wǒ bǎ zhè piān wén zhāng fān yì chéng yīng wén， hǎo ma?Tanaka: Could you help me translate this article into English?
小雨：我的英文水平不高，怎么翻译得了啊?xiǎo yǔ： wǒ de yīng wén shuǐ píng bù gāo， zěn me fān yì dé le a?Xiaoyu: My English isn't great. How could I possibly translate it?
田中：你不是学英语的吗?tián zhōng： nǐ bú shì xué yīng yǔ de ma?Tanaka: Aren't you an English major?
小雨：哪儿啊,我是学法语的。xiǎo yǔ： nǎ ér a, wǒ shì xué fǎ yǔ de。Xiaoyu: No way! I'm a French major.
田中：真的吗?tián zhōng： zhēn de ma?Tanaka: Really?
我把你当成学英语的了。wǒ bǎ nǐ dàng chéng xué yīng yǔ de le。I mistook you for an English major.
📝 Notes
……时 — ‘When...’
……时 is equivalent to ……的时候 (when...).
你去办公室时nǐ qù bàn gōng shì shíWhen you go to the office.
哪儿啊 — Expressing Negation
哪儿啊 is used in an answer to express negation (denying something).
哪儿啊，我是学法语的nǎ ér a， wǒ shì xué fǎ yǔ deNo, I’m a French major.
📚 Grammar
1. The 把 sentence (1)
Subject + 把 + Object + Verb + Other element
“把” sentences are usually used to emphasize how the object of a verb is disposed of and what result is brought about. The word order of “把” sentences is: Subject + 把 + Object (things disposed of) + Verb + Other elements (like how to dispose of or the result of it) Grammatical features of “把” sentences: The object of a “把” sentence is a definite person or a thing in the mind of the speaker. Hence, instead of “我把一杯咖啡喝了”, we should say “我把那杯咖啡喝了”, or “我喝了一杯咖啡”. The main verb of a “把” sentence should be transitive and has a meaning of disposing or controlling something. Verbs without such a meaning, e.g. “有”, “是”, “在”, “喜欢” and “知道” etc. can't be used in “把” sentences. The main verb of a “把” sentence must be followed by some elements, such as aspect particle “了” or “着”, an object, a complement (complements of potential excepted), or the repetition of the verb, to indicate the result or effect of the action. Thus, instead of “我把那杯咖啡喝”, we should say “我把那杯咖啡喝了”, or “我把那杯咖啡喝完了”. In Chinese, if the main verb is followed by the complements of result “到”, “在”, “给”, “成”, then the “把” sentence must be used. Their meanings are: “到” shows that the things or persons that are being disposed of arrive some places. “在” shows that the things or persons that are being disposed of are in some places. “给” shows that the things that are being disposed of are given to somebody else. “成” shows that the things or persons that are being disposed of become something else. E.g.
请帮我把这张表送到办公室。qǐng bāng wǒ bǎ zhè zhāng biǎo sòng dào bàn gōng shì。Please help me take this form to the office.
我把自行车放在楼下的车棚里了。wǒ bǎ zì xíng chē fàng zài lóu xià de chē péng lǐ le。I’ve put the bike in the shed downstairs.
请把这张表交给赵老师。qǐng bǎ zhè zhāng biǎo jiāo gěi zhào lǎo shī。Please hand this form to Teacher Zhao.
我把你当成学英语的了。wǒ bǎ nǐ dàng chéng xué yīng yǔ de le。I took you for an English student.
Negative adverbs, modal verbs and adverbial adjunct denoting time must be placed before “把”, e.g.
我还没把这张表送到办公室。wǒ hái méi bǎ zhè zhāng biǎo sòng dào bàn gōng shì。I haven’t taken this form to the office yet.
你能帮我把这篇文章翻译成英文吗？nǐ néng bāng wǒ bǎ zhè piān wén zhāng fān yì chéng yīng wén ma？Could you help me translate this article into English?
我明天就把这张表交给赵老师。wǒ míng tiān jiù bǎ zhè zhāng biǎo jiāo gěi zhào lǎo shī。I’ll hand this form to Teacher Zhao tomorrow.
