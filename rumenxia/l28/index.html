💬 Dialogue
Dialogue 1
小雨：怎么这么冷?xiǎo yǔ： zěn me zhè me lěng?Xiaoyu: Why is it so cold?
快把空调关上吧。kuài bǎ kōng tiáo guān shàng ba。Turn off the AC, quick!
小雨：我可能有点儿发烧。xiǎo yǔ： wǒ kě néng yǒu diǎn ér fā shāo。Xiaoyu: I think I have a fever.
姐姐：哟，这么烫!赶快把衣服穿上，我陪你去医院。jiě jie： yō， zhè me tàng! gǎn kuài bǎ yī fu chuān shàng， wǒ péi nǐ qù yī yuàn。Sister: Wow, you're burning up! Put on your clothes — I'll go to the hospital with you.
小雨：我不愿意去，我最怕打针。xiǎo yǔ： wǒ bù yuàn yì qù， wǒ zuì pà dǎ zhēn。Xiaoyu: I don't want to go. I'm terrified of needles.
姐姐：那怎么行呢?jiě jie： nà zěn me xíng ne?Sister: How can you not go?
Dialogue 2
大夫：怎么了?dà fū： zěn me le?Doctor: What's wrong?
哪儿不舒服?nǎ ér bù shū fu?Where does it hurt?
小雨：昨天开始头疼、嗓子疼，还有点儿咳嗽。xiǎo yǔ： zuó tiān kāi shǐ tóu téng、 sǎng zi téng， hái yǒu diǎn ér ké sòu。Xiaoyu: Since yesterday, I've had a headache, sore throat, and a bit of a cough.
大夫：先量一下儿体温，把体温表放好。dà fū： xiān liàng yī xià ér tǐ wēn， bǎ tǐ wēn biǎo fàng hǎo。Doctor: First, let's take your temperature. Put the thermometer under your arm.
大夫：三十八度八。dà fū： sān shí bā dù bā。Doctor: 38.8 degrees.
请把嘴张开，我看看。qǐng bǎ zuǐ zhāng kāi， wǒ kàn kan。Open your mouth please. Let me have a look.
小雨：大夫，我不想打针。xiǎo yǔ： dà fū， wǒ bù xiǎng dǎ zhēn。Xiaoyu: Doctor, I don't want an injection.
大夫：那我给你开点儿药，要按时吃。dà fū： nà wǒ gěi nǐ kāi diǎn ér yào， yào àn shí chī。Doctor: Then I'll prescribe some medicine. Make sure to take it on time.
平时要多喝点儿水。píng shí yào duō hē diǎn ér shuǐ。Drink plenty of water in general.
Dialogue 3
姐姐：我劝你少抽点儿烟，抽烟对身体没有好处。jiě jie： wǒ quàn nǐ shǎo chōu diǎn ér yān， chōu yān duì shēn tǐ méi yǒu hǎo chù。Sister: I advise you to smoke less. Smoking is bad for your health.
小雨：哎呀，又来了，你已经说了八百遍了。xiǎo yǔ： āi yā， yòu lái le， nǐ yǐ jīng shuō le bā bǎi biàn le。Xiaoyu: Oh, here we go again! You've said it a thousand times.
姐姐：要是你把烟戒了，我就不说了。jiě jie： yào shì nǐ bǎ yān jiè le， wǒ jiù bù shuō le。Sister: If you quit smoking, I'll stop saying it.
今天取回来的药呢?jīn tiān qǔ huí lái de yào ne?Where's the medicine we got today?
小雨：我已经把它吃了。xiǎo yǔ： wǒ yǐ jīng bǎ tā chī le。Xiaoyu: I already took it.
姐姐：那就把电视关上，早点儿睡觉吧。jiě jie： nà jiù bǎ diàn shì guān shàng， zǎo diǎn ér shuì jiào ba。Sister: Then turn off the TV and go to bed early.
📝 Notes
又来了 — ‘Here You Go Again’
又来了 is used to express that the speaker doesn’t like a remark or action being repeated.
哎呀，又来了，你已经说了八百遍了āi yā， yòu lái le， nǐ yǐ jīng shuō le bā bǎi biàn leOh, here we go again, you’ve said it a thousand times.
📚 Grammar
1. The 把 sentence (2): suggestions & demands
把 + Object + Verb + 了 / 着
Besides expressing disposal, “把” sentences can also be used to express suggestions or demands. In this case, the verb often has elements like “了”, “着” etc. after it.
快把空调关上！kuài bǎ kōng tiáo guān shàng！Turn the air conditioning off, quick!
你把窗户打开吧。nǐ bǎ chuāng hu dǎ kāi ba。Open the window, will you?
别把书放在那儿。bié bǎ shū fàng zài nà ér。Don’t put the book there.
2. Negative form of the 把 sentence
Neg. + 把 + Object + Verb
The negative adverb is placed before “把”.
你不把空调关上吗？nǐ bù bǎ kōng tiáo guān shàng ma？Aren’t you going to turn the air conditioning off?
他没把作业写完。tā méi bǎ zuò yè xiě wán。He didn’t finish the homework.
你别把书放在那儿。nǐ bié bǎ shū fàng zài nà ér。Don’t put the book there.
3. The 把 sentence with modal verbs
Modal verb + 把 + Object + Verb
Modal verbs (such as “能”, “可以”, “应该” etc.) are placed before “把”.
你能把空调关上吗？nǐ néng bǎ kōng tiáo guān shàng ma？Could you turn the air conditioning off?
你应该把作业写完了。nǐ yīng gāi bǎ zuò yè xiě wán le。You should have finished the homework.
我们可以把书放在桌子上吗？wǒ men kě yǐ bǎ shū fàng zài zhuō zi shàng ma？May we put the books on the table?
