📝 Notes
这儿 / 那儿 — Location Words with People or Places
The demonstrative pronouns 这儿 (here) and 那儿 (there) can follow a personal pronoun, or even a non-place noun such as a company name, to indicate a location. ……这儿 means ‘near the speaker’; ……那儿 means ‘away from the speaker’.
刚才我去他那儿的时候gāng cái wǒ qù tā nà ér de shí houWhen I went to his place just now.
Here 他那儿 literally ‘his there’ = his place / where he is. The person’s name or pronoun does the work that a place word would normally do.
📚 Grammar
1. The progressive aspect of an action
正在 / 正 / 在 + Verb (+ 呢)
To show that an action is in progress, put one of the adverbs 正在, 正 or 在 before the verb, or add the particle 呢 at the end of the sentence. They can also be used together.
他正在等他爱人呢。tā zhèng zài děng tā ài rén ne。He is waiting for his wife.
外面正下大雨呢。wài miàn zhèng xià dà yǔ ne。It is raining hard outside.
你在给朋友打电话呢。nǐ zài gěi péng yǒu dǎ diàn huà ne。You are calling a friend.
If the verb has an object, the verb is usually repeated together with its object — you cannot simply drop the object.
他正在等他爱人呢。tā zhèng zài děng tā ài rén ne。He is waiting for his wife. — correct.
✗他正在等呢。tā zhèng zài děng ne。Wrong — the object is missing.
2. Some…, (and) others…
有的 ……，有的 ……
有的 refers to part of a group of people or things. The pattern 有的……，有的…… shows that different members of the group are doing different things.
商店里顾客真不少，有的正在挑东西，有的正在排队交钱。shāng diàn lǐ gù kè zhēn bù shǎo， yǒu de zhèng zài tiāo dōng xī， yǒu de zhèng zài pái duì jiāo qián。There are quite a lot of customers in the shop — some are choosing things, some are queuing to pay.
今天天气不好，有的想出去，有的不想出去。jīn tiān tiān qì bù hǎo， yǒu de xiǎng chū qù， yǒu de bù xiǎng chū qù。The weather is bad today — some want to go out, some don’t.
3. V1 while V2
一边 + V1，一边 + V2
一边……，一边…… shows that the same subject is carrying out two actions at the same time. Both actions must be continuous.
莉莉跟朋友们在房间里一边喝咖啡一边聊天儿。lì lì gēn péng yǒu men zài fáng jiān lǐ yī biān hē kā fēi yī biān liáo tiān ér。Lili and her friends are drinking coffee and chatting in the room.
他一边听音乐一边写作业。tā yī biān tīng yīn lè yī biān xiě zuò yè。He listens to music while doing his homework.
NB: both actions must be actions the subject does on purpose. The sentence below is wrong, because 脸红 (to blush) is not something you do deliberately.
✗他一边受表扬，一边脸红了。tā yī biān shòu biǎo yáng， yī biān liǎn hóng le。Wrong — blushing is not a voluntary action.
