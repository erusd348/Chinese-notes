💬 Dialogue
Dialogue 1
秋天了，天气凉了，山上的树叶都红了。qiū tiān le， tiān qì liáng le， shān shàng de shù yè dōu hóng le。It's autumn. The weather is cool, and the leaves on the hills have all turned red.
西蒙他们打算今天去香山看红叶，可是真不巧，天阴了，要下雨了。xī méng tā men dǎ suan jīn tiān qù xiāng shān kàn hóng yè， kě shì zhēn bù qiǎo， tiān yīn le， yào xià yǔ le。Simon and his friends plan to go to Fragrant Hills today to see the red leaves, but sadly, it's cloudy and it's about to rain.
Dialogue 2
小雨现在是职员了，工作很忙，玩儿的时间少了，也不能睡懒觉了。xiǎo yǔ xiàn zài shì zhí yuán le， gōng zuò hěn máng， wán ér de shí jiān shǎo le， yě bù néng shuì lǎn jiào le。Xiaoyu is an employee now. He's busy with work, has less time to play, and can't sleep in anymore.
前几天他病了，现在已经好了。qián jǐ tiān tā bìng le， xiàn zài yǐ jīng hǎo le。He was sick a few days ago, but now he's recovered.
最近他有点儿瘦了，不过看上去更帅了。zuì jìn tā yǒu diǎn ér shòu le， bù guò kàn shàng qù gèng shuài le。He's gotten a bit thinner lately, but he looks even more handsome.
Dialogue 3
现在十一点五十了，要下课了。xiàn zài shí yì diǎn wǔ shí le， yào xià kè le。It's 11:50 now. Class is about to end.
下课以后，西蒙打算先去食堂吃饭，然后去商店。xià kè yǐ hòu， xī méng dǎ suan xiān qù shí táng chī fàn， rán hòu qù shāng diàn。After class, Simon plans to eat at the cafeteria first, then go to the store.
他下星期就要回国了，他要给家里人和亲戚朋友买点儿礼物。tā xià xīng qī jiù yào huí guó le， tā yào gěi jiā lǐ rén hé qīn qī péng you mǎi diǎn ér lǐ wù。He's going back home next week, so he wants to buy gifts for his family and friends.

📚 Grammar
1. Modal particle 了
Sentence + 了（change of situation）
The modal particle “了” used at the end of the sentence can express that the situation has changed or that a new situation has appeared, e.g.
秋天了，天气凉了。qiū tiān le， tiān qì liáng le。It’s autumn now, and the weather has turned cool.
现在他的病已经好了。xiàn zài tā de bìng yǐ jīng hǎo le。His illness is better now.
现在十一点五十了，要下课了。xiàn zài shí yī diǎn wǔ shí le， yào xià kè le。It’s already ten to twelve — class is about to finish.
今年我二十一岁了。jīn nián wǒ èr shí yī suì le。I’m twenty-one this year.
2. 要……了
要 / 就要 / 快要 / 快 + Verb + 了
This construction indicates that a situation is about to change or a new situation is about to happen, e.g.
要下课了。yào xià kè le。Class is about to finish.
我要回国了。wǒ yào huí guó le。I’m going back home.
Before “要”, one can also add “就” or “快” as an adverbial adjunct to show immence. “快要……了” can also be said as “快……了”, e.g.
他就要回国了。tā jiù yào huí guó le。He’s going back home very soon.
快上课了。kuài shàng kè le。Class is about to start.
Before “就要……了”, one can add a noun denoting time as an adverbial adjunct, but “快要……了” cannot be used in this way, e.g.
我下个月就要结婚了。wǒ xià gè yuè jiù yào jié hūn le。I’m getting married next month.
3. Modal verbs (2): 能 / 可以
能 / 可以 + Verb
They are both used to indicate ability to do something or probability, e.g.
你能吃辣的吗？nǐ néng chī là de ma？Can you eat spicy food?
你去商店的时候，能帮我买瓶矿泉水吗？nǐ qù shāng diàn de shí hou， néng bāng wǒ mǎi píng kuàng quán shuǐ ma？When you go to the shop, could you buy me a bottle of mineral water?
他不会说汉语没关系，我可以跟他说英语。tā bù huì shuō hàn yǔ méi guān xi， wǒ kě yǐ gēn tā shuō yīng yǔ。It doesn’t matter that he can’t speak Chinese — I can speak English with him.
They are also used to express permission depending on circumstances, e.g.
病人正在休息，你们不能进去。bìng rén zhèng zài xiū xi， nǐ men bù néng jìn qù。The patient is resting, so you can’t go in.
这件衣服我可以试试吗？zhè jiàn yī fu wǒ kě yǐ shì shi ma？May I try this one on?
NB: Except when expressing prohibition where “不可以” is used, the negative forms of both “能” and “可以” are generally “不能”.
