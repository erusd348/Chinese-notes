📝 Notes
是吗 / 真的吗 — Expressing Surprise
是吗 and 真的吗 are used to show that you didn’t know something before and find it a bit surprising or hard to believe.
可……了 — Emphasis
可……了 is used to emphasise a high degree.
可好听了kě hǎo tīng leIt’s really nice to listen to.
不简单 — ‘Not Ordinary’
When 简单 means ‘ordinary’, it is usually used in the negative form.
你真不简单nǐ zhēn bù jiǎn dānYou’re amazing (lit. not ordinary).
📚 Grammar
1. Aspect particle 过
Verb + 过（experience）／没 + Verb + 过
The aspect particle “过” denotes that some action took place in the past. It is used to emphasize experience, e.g.
来北京以后，我去过香山和颐和园。lái běi jīng yǐ hòu， wǒ qù guò xiāng shān hé yí hé yuán。Since coming to Beijing, I’ve been to Fragrant Hills and the Summer Palace.
北京有名的风味菜和小吃我差不多都吃过。běi jīng yǒu míng de fēng wèi cài hé xiǎo chī wǒ chà bu duō dōu chī guò。I’ve tried almost all of Beijing’s famous dishes and snacks.
The negative form of “过” is “没(有)……过”, e.g.
我还从来没吃过烤鸭呢。wǒ hái cóng lái méi chī guò kǎo yā ne。I have never eaten roast duck.
来中国以前，我没学过汉语。lái zhōng guó yǐ qián， wǒ méi xué guò hàn yǔ。Before coming to China I had never studied Chinese.
The affirmative-negative question with “过” is “……过没有”, e.g.
来北京以后，你吃过烤鸭没有？lái běi jīng yǐ hòu， nǐ chī guò kǎo yā méi yǒu？Have you tried roast duck since you came to Beijing?
你见过保罗的女朋友没有？nǐ jiàn guò bǎo luó de nǚ péng you méi yǒu？Have you met Paul’s girlfriend?
2. 是……的
是 + Time / Place / Manner + Verb + 的
“是……的” is used to emphasize the time, place, or manner of an action. The structure “的” functions as the predicate, indicating that the action has already taken place, e.g.
我是去年八月来北京的。wǒ shì qù nián bā yuè lái běi jīng de。It was last August that I came to Beijing.
这本书是在外文书店买的。zhè běn shū shì zài wài wén shū diàn mǎi de。This book was bought at the foreign-language bookshop.
3. 遍 / 次 / 一下儿
Verb + 遍 / 次 / 一下儿
“遍” emphasizes that an action is completed from beginning to end once; “次” emphasizes the number of times an action happens; “一下儿” indicates that an action lasts a short time or is done casually, e.g.
这本书我看了三遍。zhè běn shū wǒ kàn le sān biàn。I have read this book three times.
我去过两次香山。wǒ qù guò liǎng cì xiāng shān。I have been to Fragrant Hills twice.
请你等一下儿。qǐng nǐ děng yī xià ér。Please wait a moment.
4. 除了……以外
除了 + Noun + 以外
“除了……以外” indicates “except” or “besides”, e.g.
除了香山以外，北京的公园我都去过了。chú le xiāng shān yǐ wài， běi jīng de gōng yuán wǒ dōu qù guò le。Apart from Fragrant Hills, I’ve been to all the parks in Beijing.
除了玛丽以外，别的同学都没吃过烤鸭。chú le mǎ lì yǐ wài， bié de tóng xué dōu méi chī guò kǎo yā。Except for Mary, none of the other classmates has tried roast duck.
