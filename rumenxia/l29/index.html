Dialogue 1
莉莉：你怎么了?lì lì： nǐ zěn me le?Lily: What happened to you?
直美：别提了,刚才骑车出去，被一辆三轮车撞倒了。zhí měi： bié tí le, gāng cái qí chē chū qù， bèi yī liàng sān lún chē zhuàng dào le。Naomi: Don't even mention it. I was biking and got knocked down by a tricycle.
莉莉：是吗?lì lì： shì ma?Lily: Really?
受伤了没有?shòu shāng le méi yǒu?Are you hurt?
直美：你看，胳膊和腿都被撞青了。zhí měi： nǐ kàn， gē bo hé tuǐ dōu bèi zhuàng qīng le。Naomi: Look, my arms and legs are all bruised.
真倒霉!zhēn dǎo méi!What bad luck!
莉莉：以后骑车的时候小心点儿。lì lì： yǐ hòu qí chē de shí hou xiǎo xīn diǎn ér。Lily: Be more careful when you ride from now on.
直美：不是我不小心，是那个骑三轮车的骑得太快了。zhí měi： bú shì wǒ bù xiǎo xīn， shì nà ge qí sān lún chē de qí dé tài kuài le。Naomi: It's not that I wasn't careful — that guy was going way too fast.

📝 Notes
别提了 — ‘Don’t Even Mention It’
别提了 expresses that one doesn’t want to talk about something (often something unpleasant).
真不像话 — ‘That’s Outrageous’
真不像话 is often used to criticise someone’s words or actions as unreasonable or unacceptable.
真气人！ / 真急人！ — Expressing Anger / Anxiety
真气人! (so infuriating!) and 真急人! (so frustrating!) are used to express anger or anxiety about a situation.
📚 Grammar
1. The passive sentence
Subject + 被 / 叫 / 让 (+ Agent) + Verb + Other element
There is a type of sentences in Chinese, in which the subject is the receiver of the action, and the prepositions “被”, “叫”, “让” are used to introduce the performer of the action. This type of sentences which use the prepositions “被”, “叫”, “让” to express passive voice are called passive sentences. The predicate verb of a passive sentence usually has other elements, explaining the result, degree, time, etc. of the action. They mostly imply something undesirable. “被” is mostly used in written language, while “叫” and “让” are mostly used in spoken language. In spoken language, the particle “给” which expresses passive voice can also be added after the object of the preposition. The word order of a passive sentence is generally: Subject (receiver of the action) + 被/叫/让 + Object of preposition (+ 给) + Verb + Other element E.g.
刚才我被一辆三轮车撞倒了。gāng cái wǒ bèi yī liàng sān lún chē zhuàng dào le。Just now I was knocked down by a pedicab.
上午逛商店的时候，钱包叫小偷偷走了。shàng wǔ guàng shāng diàn de shí hou， qián bāo jiào xiǎo tōu tōu zǒu le。While I was out shopping this morning, my wallet was stolen by a pickpocket.
上次我的电脑就是让小雨给修好的。shàng cì wǒ de diàn nǎo jiù shì ràng xiǎo yǔ gěi xiū hǎo de。Last time my computer was fixed by Xiaoyu.
If there is no need to or one is not able to tell the performer of an action, the agent can be replaced by the word “人” which is one of generic reference, e.g.
那本书可能让人借走了。nà běn shū kě néng ràng rén jiè zǒu le。That book has probably been borrowed by someone.
If it is unnecessary to tell the performer of an action, “被” can go without an object (the agent) and be followed immediately by the predicate verb. “叫” or “让” can't be used in this way, e.g.
啤酒都被喝完了。pí jiǔ dōu bèi hē wán le。The beer has all been drunk.
The negative adverb or modal verb, when there is one, should be placed before “被”, “叫” or “让”, e.g.
我的自行车没让人借走。wǒ de zì xíng chē méi ràng rén jiè zǒu。My bike wasn’t borrowed by anyone.
