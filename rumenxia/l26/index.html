💬 Dialogue
Dialogue 1
英男：学习结束后，我打算去旅行。yīng nán： xué xí jié shù hòu， wǒ dǎ suan qù lǚ xíng。Yingnan: After my studies, I plan to travel.
您说去西安好还是去大同好?nín shuō qù xī ān hǎo hái shì qù dà tóng hǎo?Which do you think is better, Xi'an or Datong?
老师：西安的名胜古迹比大同多，我建议你去西安看看。lǎo shi： xī ān de míng shèng gǔ jì bǐ dà tóng duō， wǒ jiàn yì nǐ qù xī ān kàn kan。Teacher: Xi'an has more historical sites than Datong. I suggest you visit Xi'an.
英男：我听说西安比北京还热，大同是不是比北京凉快一点儿?yīng nán： wǒ tīng shuō xī ān bǐ běi jīng hái rè， dà tóng shì bú shì bǐ běi jīng liáng kuai yì diǎn ér?Yingnan: I heard Xi'an is even hotter than Beijing. Is Datong a bit cooler?
老师：看天气预报，今年大同也不比北京凉快。lǎo shi： kàn tiān qì yù bào， jīn nián dà tóng yě bù bǐ běi jīng liáng kuai。Teacher: According to the forecast, Datong isn't cooler than Beijing this year either.
英男：西安比大同远得多吧?yīng nán： xī ān bǐ dà tóng yuǎn dé duō ba?Yingnan: Xi'an is much farther than Datong, right?
老师：是啊。lǎo shi： shì a。Teacher: Yes.
不过，去西安的车比较多，比去大同更方便。bù guò， qù xī ān de chē bǐ jiào duō， bǐ qù dà tóng gèng fāng biàn。But there are more trains to Xi'an, so it's more convenient.
英男：好，就听您的。yīng nán： hǎo， jiù tīng nín de。Yingnan: Okay, I'll follow your advice.
Dialogue 2
老师：我看,出去旅行你最好找个伴儿，可以互相帮助。lǎo shi： wǒ kàn, chū qù lǚ xíng nǐ zuì hǎo zhǎo gè bàn ér， kě yǐ hù xiāng. bāng zhù。Teacher: I think you'd better find a travel buddy — you can help each other.
英男：我也这么想。yīng nán： wǒ yě zhè me xiǎng。Yingnan: I think so too.
我准备跟保罗一起去。wǒ zhǔn bèi gēn bǎo luó yì qǐ qù。I'm planning to go with Paul.
老师：就是那个又高又壮的德国人吧?lǎo shi： jiù shì nà ge yòu gāo yòu zhuàng de dé guó rén ba?Teacher: Is that the tall, strong German guy?
英男：他跟我一样高，但是比我壮多了。yīng nán： tā gēn wǒ yī yàng gāo， dàn shì bǐ wǒ zhuàng duō le。Yingnan: He's the same height as me, but much stronger.
老师：他有你大吗?lǎo shi： tā yǒu nǐ dà ma?Teacher: Is he older than you?
英男：他没有我大。yīng nán： tā méi yǒu wǒ dà。Yingnan: No, he's younger.
Dialogue 3
老师：学完以后你有什么打算?lǎo shi： xué wán yǐ hòu nǐ yǒu shén me dǎ suan?Teacher: What are your plans after you graduate?
英男：我希望在中国找个工作，可是我家里人不同意，我不知道怎么办好。yīng nán： wǒ xī wàng zài zhōng guó zhǎo gè gōng zuò， kě shì wǒ jiā lǐ rén bù tóng yì， wǒ bù zhī dao zěn me bàn hǎo。Yingnan: I hope to find a job in China, but my family doesn't agree. I don't know what to do.
老师：他们是不是觉得在国外生活没有在国内方便?lǎo shi： tā men shì bú shì jué de zài guó wài shēng huó méi yǒu zài guó nèi fāng biàn?Teacher: Do they think living abroad isn't as convenient as at home?
英男：是啊，有些方面确实没有国内方便，但是我对在这儿工作很感兴趣。yīng nán： shì a， yǒu xiē fāng miàn què shí méi yǒu guó nèi fāng biàn， dàn shì wǒ duì zài zhè ér gōng zuò hěn gǎn xìng qù。Yingnan: Yes, in some ways it's not as convenient, but I'm very interested in working here.
老师：依我看,你还是跟家里人再好好儿商量商量吧。lǎo shi： yī wǒ kàn, nǐ hái shì gēn jiā lǐ rén zài hǎo hǎo ér shāng liang shāng liang ba。Teacher: In my opinion, you should talk it over properly with your family.
📝 Notes
听 + Pronoun / Noun + 的 — ‘Following Someone’s Advice’
The pattern 听 + pronoun/noun + 的 means ‘to accept someone’s advice’.
就听您的jiù tīng nín deI’ll follow your advice.
我看 / 依我看 — ‘In My Opinion’
我看 and 依我看 mean ‘in my opinion’ and introduce the speaker’s viewpoint.
📚 Grammar
1. The 比 comparison
A + 比 + B + Adj.
“比” is used between two things to indicate that the former has a higher degree of some quality than the latter. Format: A + 比 + B + Adj.
西安比北京还热。xī ān bǐ běi jīng hái rè。Xi’an is even hotter than Beijing.
他比我高。tā bǐ wǒ gāo。He is taller than me.
这本书比那本书有意思。zhè běn shū bǐ nà běn shū yǒu yì si。This book is more interesting than that one.
2. Not as … as
A + 没有 / 不如 + B + Adj.
“没有” and “不如” both indicate that the former is not as good as the latter. Format: A + 没有 + B + Adj. / A + 不如 + B.
他没有我高。tā méi yǒu wǒ gāo。He is not as tall as me.
这个不如那个好。zhè ge bù rú nà ge hǎo。This one is not as good as that one.
我的汉语没有他好。wǒ de hàn yǔ méi yǒu tā hǎo。My Chinese is not as good as his.
3. As … as
A + 跟 + B + 一样 + Adj.
“跟…一样” indicates that two things are the same in some aspect. Format: A + 跟 + B + 一样 + Adj.
我跟他一样高。wǒ gēn tā yī yàng gāo。I’m as tall as him.
这本书跟那本书一样有意思。zhè běn shū gēn nà běn shū yī yàng yǒu yì si。This book is as interesting as that one.
我的想法跟你一样。wǒ de xiǎng fǎ gēn nǐ yī yàng。My idea is the same as yours.
4. Not necessarily more … than
A + 不比 + B + Adj.
“不比” indicates that A is not necessarily more… than B. Format: A + 不比 + B + Adj.
他不比你高多少。tā bù bǐ nǐ gāo duō shǎo。He isn’t much taller than you.
这本书不比那本书难。zhè běn shū bù bǐ nà běn shū nán。This book isn’t more difficult than that one.
今天不比昨天冷。jīn tiān bù bǐ zuó tiān lěng。Today isn’t colder than yesterday.
