📖 Vocabulary
中间 zhōng jiān middle; between
附近 fù jìn nearby
旁边 páng biān next to; beside
对面 duì miàn opposite

教学楼 jiào xué lóu teaching building
📚 Grammar
中间 middle; between
我宝贝睡在我们中间。 wǒ bǎo bèi shuì zài wǒ men zhōng jiān My baby sleeps between us.
新加坡在印度尼西亚和马来西亚中间。 xīn jiā pō zài yìn dù ní xī yà hé mǎ lái xī yà zhōng jiān Singapore is between Indonesia and Malaysia.
附近 nearby
你们家附近有什么？ nǐ men jiā fù jìn yǒu shí me What is near your home?
我们家附近有便利店游乐场和广场。 wǒ men jiā fù jìn yǒu biàn lì diàn yóu lè chǎng hé guǎng chǎng Near our home there is a convenience store playground and plaza.
麦当劳在火车站附近。 mài dāng láo zài huǒ chē zhàn fù jìn McDonalds is near the train station.
旁边 next to; beside
麦当劳在地铁站旁边。 mài dāng láo zài dì tiě zhàn páng biān McDonalds is next to the subway station.
不要坐在我旁边。 bù yào zuò zài wǒ páng biān Dont sit next to me.
对面 opposite
麦当劳在我们家对面。 mài dāng láo zài wǒ men jiā duì miàn McDonalds is opposite our home.
你们在我对面。 nǐ men zài wǒ duì miàn You are opposite me.
办公楼 / 教学楼 office building / teaching building
办公楼在教学楼对面。 bàn gōng lóu zài jiào xué lóu duì miàn The office building is opposite the teaching building.
