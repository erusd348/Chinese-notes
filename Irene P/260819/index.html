📚 Grammar
S + Adj + 了 (Structure) 了 marks a new state or a completed action
我累了。 wǒ lèi le I'm tired (now).
我饿了。 wǒ è le I'm hungry (now).
我渴了。 wǒ kě le I'm thirsty (now).
我早上吃了米饭。 wǒ zǎo shang chī le mǐ fàn I ate rice this morning.
S + Adj + 吗？ (Structure) Are you tired / hungry / thirsty? (Adj = 累 / 饿 / 渴)
你累吗？ nǐ lèi ma Are you tired?
你饿吗？ nǐ è ma Are you hungry?
你渴吗？ nǐ kě ma Are you thirsty?
我不累。 wǒ bú lèi I'm not tired.
Time + 吃 + 什么？ (Structure) What do you eat in the morning / at noon / in the evening?
你早上吃什么？ nǐ zǎo shang chī shén me What do you eat in the morning?
你中午吃了什么？ nǐ zhōng wǔ chī le shén me What did you eat for lunch?
你晚上吃了什么？ nǐ wǎn shang chī le shén me What did you eat for dinner?
我中午吃了蛋白棒。 wǒ zhōng wǔ chī le dàn bái bàng I ate a protein bar for lunch.
我晚上吃了牛肉和米饭。 wǒ wǎn shang chī le niú ròu hé mǐ fàn I ate beef and rice in the evening.
S + 爱 + 吃 + N？ (Structure) What meat / food do you like to eat?
你爱吃什么肉？ nǐ ài chī shén me ròu What meat do you like to eat?
你不爱吃什么？ nǐ bú ài chī shén me What don't you like to eat?
我不爱吃面包。 wǒ bú ài chī miàn bāo I don't like to eat bread.
N + 好吃吗？ (Structure) Is ... tasty?
猪肉好吃吗？ zhū ròu hǎo chī ma Is pork tasty?
猪肉很好吃。 zhū ròu hěn hǎo chī Pork is very tasty.
蛋白棒不好吃。 dàn bái bàng bù hǎo chī The protein bar is not tasty.
面包不好吃。 miàn bāo bù hǎo chī Bread is not tasty.
💬 Sentences
我很累，也很饿。 wǒ hěn lèi，yě hěn è I'm very tired and very hungry.
我很饿，也很渴。 wǒ hěn è，yě hěn kě I'm very hungry and very thirsty.
我早上吃金枪鱼和米饭。 wǒ zǎo shang chī jīn qiāng yú hé mǐ fàn I eat tuna and rice in the morning.
