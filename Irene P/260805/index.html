📖 Vocabulary
都 dōu both; all

📚 Grammar
都 both; all
我们都有妹妹。 wǒ men dōu yǒu mèi mei We all have younger sisters.
我爸爸妈妈都很好。 wǒ bà ba mā ma dōu hěn hǎo Both my parents are fine.
我妹妹和我都是合肥人。 wǒ mèi mei hé wǒ dōu shì hé féi rén My sister and I are both from Hefei.
爸爸妈妈都很爱我们。 bà ba mā ma dōu hěn ài wǒ men My parents both love us very much.
我爱人和我都很忙。 wǒ ài rén hé wǒ dōu hěn máng My spouse and I are both busy.
爱人 / 爱 spouse / to love
我爱你。我也爱你。 wǒ ài nǐ. wǒ yě ài nǐ I love you. I love you too.
他是我爱人。 tā shì wǒ ài rén He is my spouse.
我爱人是白人。 wǒ ài rén shì bái rén My spouse is white.
公公 / 婆婆 father-in-law / mother-in-law
你爱人的爸爸妈妈是你的公公婆婆。 nǐ ài rén de bà ba mā ma shì nǐ de gōng gong pó po Your spouse's parents are your in-laws.
我和我公公婆婆关系一般。 wǒ hé wǒ gōng gong pó po guān xi yī bān My relationship with my in-laws is so-so.
忙 busy
我很忙。 wǒ hěn máng I'm busy.
你爱人忙吗？ nǐ ài rén máng ma Is your spouse busy?
我爱人也很忙。 wǒ ài rén yě hěn máng My spouse is also busy.
狗 / 猫 dog / cat
我的狗不忙。 wǒ de gǒu bù máng My dog is not busy.
我的猫也不忙。 wǒ de māo yě bù máng My cat is not busy either.
我的狗和猫都不忙。 wǒ de gǒu hé māo dōu bù máng Both my dog and cat are not busy.
