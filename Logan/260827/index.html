📚 Grammar
A 多少钱？ (Structure) How much is A? (asking price)
一瓶啤酒多少钱？ yì píng pí jiǔ duō shǎo qián? How much is a bottle of beer?
啤酒多少钱一瓶？ pí jiǔ duō shǎo qián yì píng? How much is beer per bottle?
一瓶啤酒一块五。 yì píng pí jiǔ yí kuài wǔ. A bottle of beer is 1.5 yuan.
换 + money (Structure) to exchange (money)
你要换多少钱？ nǐ yào huàn duō shǎo qián? How much money do you want to exchange?
我要换一百美元。 wǒ yào huàn yì bǎi měi yuán. I want to exchange 100 US dollars.
我换了一辆新车。 wǒ huàn le yí liàng xīn chē. I changed to a new car.
他换了一个女朋友。 tā huàn le yí gè nǚ péng yǒu. He changed girlfriends.
可以帮 V？ (Structure) Can (you) help (me) V?
可以帮我换一个碗吗？ kě yǐ bāng wǒ huàn yí gè wǎn ma? Can you help me exchange a bowl?
