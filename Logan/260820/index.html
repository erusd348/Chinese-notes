📚 Grammar
量词：个 / 杯 / 瓶 / 碗 measure words
一个人 yí gè rén one person
一个包子一个面包 yí gè bāo zi yí gè miàn bāo one baozi and one bread
一杯水 yì bēi shuǐ a glass of water
一瓶水 yì píng shuǐ a bottle of water
S + 可以 + V (Structure) S can V
我可以吃两碗饭。 wǒ kě yǐ chī liǎng wǎn fàn I can eat two bowls of rice.
请 + 给 + 我 + N (Structure) Please give me N
请给我一个碗。 qǐng gěi wǒ yí gè wǎn Please give me a bowl.
N + 多少钱？ (Structure) How much is N?
一个包子多少钱？ yí gè bāo zi duō shǎo qián How much is one baozi?
一个包子两块。 yí gè bāo zi liǎng kuài One baozi is two yuan.
S + 很少 + V (Structure) S rarely V
我很少吃猪肉。 wǒ hěn shǎo chī zhū ròu I rarely eat pork.
我很少喝酒。 wǒ hěn shǎo hē jiǔ I rarely drink alcohol.
💬 Sentences
我早上喝了一杯咖啡。 wǒ zǎo shang hē le yì bēi kā fēi I drank a cup of coffee this morning.
我买了一瓶水。 wǒ mǎi le yì píng shuǐ I bought a bottle of water.
我喝了两瓶可乐。 wǒ hē le liǎng píng kě lè I drank two bottles of cola.
我晚上吃了一碗面。 wǒ wǎn shang chī le yì wǎn miàn I ate a bowl of noodles in the evening.
我（没）有很多钱。 wǒ (méi) yǒu hěn duō qián I have a lot of money (or not).
你有多少钱？ nǐ yǒu duō shǎo qián How much money do you have?
