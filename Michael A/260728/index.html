📚 Grammar
供应商 supplier
这家公司是我们的供应商。 zhè jiā gōng sī shì wǒ men de gōng yìng shāng This company is our supplier.
摸鱼 to slack off
他上班的时候喜欢摸鱼。 tā shàng bān de shí hòu xǐ huān mō yú He likes to slack off at work.
外包 to outsource
很多公司把工作外包了。 hěn duō gōng sī bǎ gōng zuò wài bāo le Many companies outsource their work.
时区 vs 时差 time zone vs time difference
中国只有一个时区。 zhōng guó zhǐ yǒu yī gè shí qū China only has one time zone.
我和美国的同事有12个小时的时差。 wǒ hé měi guó de tóng shì yǒu shí èr gè xiǎo shí de shí chā There's a 12-hour time difference between me and my US colleagues.
割据 vs 同盟 carve up vs alliance
三国时期群雄割据。 sān guó shí qī qún xióng gē jù During the Three Kingdoms period, many warlords carved up the land.
孙刘联军一起对抗曹操。 sūn liú lián jūn yī qǐ duì kàng cáo cāo The Sun-Liu allied forces fought against Cao Cao together.
赤壁之战 Battle of Red Cliffs
赤壁之战是历史上著名的战役。 chì bì zhī zhàn shì lì shǐ shàng zhù míng de zhàn yì The Battle of Red Cliffs is a famous battle in history.
军师 strategist
诸葛亮是刘备的军师。 zhū gě liàng shì liú bèi de jūn shī Zhuge Liang was Liu Bei's strategist.
脑筋急转弯 brain teaser
我喜欢玩脑筋急转弯。 wǒ xǐ huān wán nǎo jīn jí zhuǎn wān I like brain teasers.
计策 strategy
这条计策很好。 zhè tiáo jì cè hěn hǎo This strategy is very good.
