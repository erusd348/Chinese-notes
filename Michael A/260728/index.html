📚 Grammar
供应商 supplier
这家公司是我们的供应商。 zhè jiā gōng sī shì wǒ men de gōng yìng shāng This company is our supplier.
摸鱼 to slack off
他上班的时候喜欢摸鱼。 tā shàng bān de shí hòu xǐ huān mō yú He likes to slack off at work.
外包 to outsource
很多公司把工作外包了。 hěn duō gōng sī bǎ gōng zuò wài bāo le Many companies outsource their work.
时区 vs 时差 time zone vs time difference
中国只有一个时区。 zhōng guó zhǐ yǒu yī gè shí qū China only has one time zone.
我和美国的同事有12个小时的时差。 wǒ hé měi guó de tóng shì yǒu shí èr gè xiǎo shí de shí chā There's a 12-hour time difference with my US colleagues.
割据 vs 同盟 vs 孙刘联军 carve up vs alliance
三国时期群雄割据。 sān guó shí qī qún xióng gē jù During the Three Kingdoms period, many warlords carved up the land.
孙刘联军一起对抗曹操。 sūn liú lián jūn yī qǐ duì kàng cáo cāo The Sun-Liu allied forces fought against Cao Cao.
赤壁之战 Battle of Red Cliffs
赤壁之战是历史上著名的战役。 chì bì zhī zhàn shì lì shǐ shàng zhù míng de zhàn yì The Battle of Red Cliffs is a famous battle in history.
军师 strategist
诸葛亮是刘备的军师。 zhū gě liàng shì liú bèi de jūn shī Zhuge Liang was Liu Bei's strategist.
说曹操曹操到 speak of the devil
说曹操曹操到，我刚说到他他就来了。 shuō cáo cāo cáo cāo dào, wǒ gāng shuō dào tā tā jiù lái le Speak of the devil — I just mentioned him and he arrived.
三个臭皮匠，赛过诸葛亮 two heads are better than one
三个臭皮匠，赛过诸葛亮，我们一起想办法吧。 sān gè chòu pí jiàng, sài guò zhū gě liàng, wǒ men yī qǐ xiǎng bàn fǎ ba Two heads are better than one — let's think together.
计策 strategy
这条计策很好。 zhè tiáo jì cè hěn hǎo This strategy is very good.
脑筋急转弯 brain teaser
我喜欢玩脑筋急转弯。 wǒ xǐ huān wán nǎo jīn jí zhuǎn wān I like brain teasers.
周瑜打黄盖——一个愿打一个愿挨 both parties willing
他借钱给他朋友，是周瑜打黄盖，一个愿打一个愿挨。 tā jiè qián gěi tā péng yǒu, shì zhōu yú dǎ huáng gài, yī gè yuàn dǎ yī gè yuàn āi He lent money to his friend — both were willing.
挥泪斩马谡 to reluctantly punish
老板挥泪斩马谡，开除了那个老员工。 lǎo bǎn huī lèi zhǎn mǎ sù, kāi chú le nà gè lǎo yuán gōng The boss reluctantly fired the veteran employee.
兄弟如手足，女人如衣服 brothers are like limbs
他说兄弟如手足，女人如衣服。 tā shuō xiōng dì rú shǒu zú, nǚ rén rú yī fu He said brothers are like limbs, women are like clothes.
身在曹营心在汉 body here, heart there
他虽然在这家公司工作，但是身在曹营心在汉。 tā suī rán zài zhè jiā gōng sī gōng zuò, dàn shì shēn zài cáo yíng xīn zài hàn Although he works at this company, his heart is elsewhere.
人中吕布，马中赤兔 the best of the best
他在我们公司是人中吕布，马中赤兔。 tā zài wǒ men gōng sī shì rén zhōng lǚ bù, mǎ zhōng chì tù He is the best of the best in our company.
张飞穿针——大眼瞪小眼 stare at each other
他们听了这个消息，张飞穿针，大眼瞪小眼。 tā men tīng le zhè gè xiāo xī, zhāng fēi chuān zhēn, dà yǎn dèng xiǎo yǎn When they heard the news, they just stared at each other blankly.
