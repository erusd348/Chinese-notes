Combine the Sentences

Join the two sentences with the given connector

1.
他tā抽chōu卡kǎ花huā了le很hěn多duō钱qián。
他tā拿ná到dào了le想xiǎng要yào的de角jiǎo色sè。
Hints
因为…所以 = because…so: 因为 + reason, 所以 + result. Put the reason first.
✍️ Combine: 因为…所以
2.
类lèi魂hún游yóu戏xì很hěn难nán。
他tā不bù想xiǎng半bàn途tú而ér废fèi。
Hints
虽然…但是 = although…but: 虽然 + concession, 但是 + contrast. Both clauses keep their own subject.
✍️ Combine: 虽然…但是
