📚 Grammar
程序员 programmer
我哥哥是程序员，他每天写代码。 wǒ gē ge shì chéng xù yuán, tā měi tiān xiě dài mǎ My brother is a programmer; he writes code every day.
产品经理 product manager
产品经理要了解用户需要什么。 chǎn pǐn jīng lǐ yào liǎo jiě yòng hù xū yào shén me A product manager needs to understand what users need.
会计 accountant
会计负责公司的钱。 kuài jì fù zé gōng sī de qián The accountant is in charge of the company's money.
技术人员 technical staff
技术人员帮我修电脑。 jì shù rén yuán bāng wǒ xiū diàn nǎo The technical staff helped me fix my computer.
金融 finance
她在金融公司工作。 tā zài jīn róng gōng sī gōng zuò She works at a finance company.
保持工作和生活的平衡 maintain work-life balance
他很重视保持工作和生活的平衡。 tā hěn zhòng shì bǎo chí gōng zuò hé shēng huó de píng héng He values maintaining a work-life balance.
升职 get promoted
他工作很努力，今年升职了。 tā gōng zuò hěn nǔ lì, jīn nián shēng zhí le He works very hard and got promoted this year.
佛系 laid-back; zen
佛系的人生活得很轻松。 fó xì de rén shēng huó de hěn qīng sōng Laid-back people live very relaxed lives.
医疗系统 healthcare system
中国的医疗系统很方便。 zhōng guó de yī liáo xì tǒng hěn fāng biàn China's healthcare system is very convenient.
医疗保险 medical insurance
我有医疗保险，看病不用花很多钱。 wǒ yǒu yī liáo bǎo xiǎn, kàn bìng bù yòng huā hěn duō qián I have medical insurance, so I don't spend much on doctor visits.
商业保险 commercial insurance
商业保险比医疗保险贵一些。 shāng yè bǎo xiǎn bǐ yī liáo bǎo xiǎn guì yī xiē Commercial insurance is a bit more expensive than medical insurance.
拒赔 refuse to pay
保险公司拒赔了，他很不高兴。 bǎo xiǎn gōng sī jù péi le, tā hěn bù gāo xìng The insurance company refused to pay, and he was very unhappy.
选择 to choose; choice
你有两个选择：住院或者回家休息。 nǐ yǒu liǎng gè xuǎn zé: zhù yuàn huò zhě huí jiā xiū xi You have two choices: stay in the hospital or go home and rest.
没有效率 inefficient
这样开会太没有效率了。 zhè yàng kāi huì tài méi yǒu xiào lǜ le Meetings like this are too inefficient.
预约 to make an appointment
看医生要先预约。 kàn yī shēng yào xiān yù yuē You need to make an appointment before seeing a doctor.
开单子 to write a prescription / bill
医生给我开了一张单子。 yī shēng gěi wǒ kāi le yī zhāng dān zi The doctor wrote me a prescription.
特权 privilege
会员有特权，不用排队。 huì yuán yǒu tè quán, bù yòng pái duì Members have the privilege of not waiting in line.
挂号费 registration fee
挂号费不贵，只要十块钱。 guà hào fèi bù guì, zhǐ yào shí kuài qián The registration fee is cheap, only ten yuan.
门诊 outpatient clinic
小病去门诊就可以。 xiǎo bìng qù mén zhěn jiù kě yǐ For minor illnesses, the outpatient clinic is enough.
急诊 emergency room
他病得很重，去了急诊。 tā bìng de hěn zhòng, qù le jí zhěn He was seriously ill and went to the emergency room.
账单 bill
这个月的账单太多了。 zhè gè yuè de zhàng dān tài duō le There are too many bills this month.
赊账 to buy on credit
这家店可以赊账。 zhè jiā diàn kě yǐ shē zhàng This shop allows buying on credit.
很负责的人 a very responsible person
他是一个很负责的人。 tā shì yī gè hěn fù zé de rén He is a very responsible person.
消耗体力 physically demanding
这个工作很消耗体力。 zhè gè gōng zuò hěn xiāo hào tǐ lì This job is very physically demanding.
修改器 cheat; game modifier
用修改器玩游戏就没意思了。 yòng xiū gǎi qì wán yóu xì jiù méi yì si le Playing games with a cheat is no fun.
恐怕 I'm afraid; perhaps
他恐怕不能来了。 tā kǒng pà bù néng lái le I'm afraid he can't come.
泄露 to leak
不要把密码泄露给别人。 bù yào bǎ mì mǎ xiè lòu gěi bié rén Don't leak your password to others.
个人信息 personal information
网上不要随便填个人信息。 wǎng shàng bù yào suí biàn tián gè rén xìn xī Don't casually fill in personal information online.
