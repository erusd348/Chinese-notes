📚 Grammar
A 比 B + Adj (Structure) A is more [adj] than B
我比你大。 wǒ bǐ nǐ dà I'm older than you.
我比你高。 wǒ bǐ nǐ gāo I'm taller than you.
我比他帅。 wǒ bǐ tā shuài I'm more handsome than him.
我女朋友比他女朋友漂亮。 wǒ nǚ péng yǒu bǐ tā nǚ péng yǒu piào liang My girlfriend is prettier than his.
我朋友比你朋友多。 wǒ péng yǒu bǐ nǐ péng yǒu duō I have more friends than you.
Musk的钱比我多。 Musk de qián bǐ wǒ duō Musk has more money than me.
B 没有 A + Adj (Structure) B is not as [adj] as A
我的钱没有马斯克多。 wǒ de qián méi yǒu mǎ sī kè duō I don't have as much money as Musk.
你朋友没有我多。 nǐ péng yǒu méi yǒu wǒ duō You don't have as many friends as me.
他女朋友没有我女朋友漂亮。 tā nǚ péng yǒu méi yǒu wǒ nǚ péng yǒu piào liang His girlfriend is not as pretty as mine.
他的房子没有我的新。 tā de fáng zi méi yǒu wǒ de xīn His house is not as new as mine.
Adj + 极了 (Structure) extremely [adj]
我累极了。 wǒ lèi jí le I'm extremely tired.
他努力极了。 tā nǔ lì jí le He is extremely hardworking.
