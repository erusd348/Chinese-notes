📚 Grammar
听起来 / 看起来 sound like / look like
吃了睡，睡了吃，听起来很舒服。 chī le shuì, shuì le chī, tīng qǐ lái hěn shū fu Eat then sleep, sleep then eat — sounds very comfortable.
他看起来很累。 tā kàn qǐ lái hěn lèi He looks very tired.
所有的 + N + 都 + V all + N + all + V
所有的小说我都看。 suǒ yǒu de xiǎo shuō wǒ dōu kàn I read all novels.
合适 / 适合 to fit / to suit
这件衣服很合适。 zhè jiàn yī fu hěn hé shì This piece of clothing fits well.
这件衣服不适合你。 zhè jiàn yī fu bù shì hé nǐ This clothing doesn't suit you.
我很适合这个工作。 wǒ hěn shì hé zhè gè gōng zuò I'm very suited for this job.
这个工作对我来说很合适。 zhè gè gōng zuò duì wǒ lái shuō hěn hé shì This job is very suitable for me.
他们很合适。 tā men hěn hé shì They are very compatible.
他不适合我。 tā bù shì hé wǒ He's not right for me.
有一点 + Adj a little bit...
今天有一点热。 jīn tiān yǒu yī diǎn rè It's a bit hot today.
这个有一点贵。 zhè gè yǒu yī diǎn guì This is a bit expensive.
A 比 B + Adj + 一点儿 a bit more...
你可以（卖得）更便宜一点（儿）吗？ nǐ kě yǐ mài de gèng pián yí yī diǎn ma Can you sell it a bit cheaper?
