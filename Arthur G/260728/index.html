📚 Grammar
S + 在 + Place indicating location
你在哪儿？ nǐ zài nǎr Where are you?
我在家。 wǒ zài jiā I'm at home.
Time + Location + Action time and place before the action
我昨天晚上在家玩游戏。 wǒ zuó tiān wǎn shàng zài jiā wán yóu xì I played games at home last night.
我爸妈每天在家工作。 wǒ bà mā měi tiān zài jiā gōng zuò My parents work at home every day.
Time of Day + 好 greetings by time of day
早上好。 zǎo shàng hǎo Good morning.
上午好。 shàng wǔ hǎo Good morning (late morning).
中午好。 zhōng wǔ hǎo Good afternoon (noon).
下午好。 xià wǔ hǎo Good afternoon.
晚上好。 wǎn shàng hǎo Good evening.
早 vs 晚 early vs late
我起床很晚。 wǒ qǐ chuáng hěn wǎn I get up very late.
Adj + 吗？ yes/no questions with adjectives
你们学校大吗？ nǐ men xué xiào dà ma Is your school big?
是的，我们学校很大。 shì de, wǒ men xué xiào hěn dà Yes, our school is very big.
你们学校漂亮吗？ nǐ men xué xiào piào liang ma Is your school pretty?
我们学校很漂亮。 wǒ men xué xiào hěn piào liang Our school is very pretty.
你们的老师好吗？ nǐ men de lǎo shī hǎo ma Is your teacher good?
我们的老师不好。 wǒ men de lǎo shī bù hǎo Our teacher is not good.
几点 + V？ asking what time
你们几点上学？ nǐ men jǐ diǎn shàng xué What time do you go to school?
我们8点上学。 wǒ men bā diǎn shàng xué We go to school at 8.
你们几点放学？ nǐ men jǐ diǎn fàng xué What time do you finish school?
我们两点放学。 wǒ men liǎng diǎn fàng xué We finish school at 2.
V + 吗？ yes/no questions with action verbs
你去食堂吃饭吗？ nǐ qù shí táng chī fàn ma Do you go to the canteen to eat?
我不去食堂吃饭。 wǒ bù qù shí táng chī fàn I don't go to the canteen to eat.
你想住宿舍吗？ nǐ xiǎng zhù sù shè ma Do you want to live in a dorm?
我不想住宿舍。 wǒ bù xiǎng zhù sù shè I don't want to live in a dorm.
你去图书馆吗？ nǐ qù tú shū guǎn ma Do you go to the library?
我不去图书馆。 wǒ bù qù tú shū guǎn I don't go to the library.
食堂 / 宿舍 / 图书馆 school facilities — Q&A
食堂的饭好吃吗？ shí táng de fàn hǎo chī ma Is the canteen food tasty?
不好吃，我不喜欢。 bù hǎo chī, wǒ bù xǐ huān Not tasty, I don't like it.
有很多人去食堂吃饭吗？ yǒu hěn duō rén qù shí táng chī fàn ma Do many people go to the canteen to eat?
是的。 shì de Yes.
食堂里有什么吃的？ shí táng lǐ yǒu shén me chī de What food is in the canteen?
我们学校没有宿舍。 wǒ men xué xiào méi yǒu sù shè Our school doesn't have dorms.
我的宿舍里有8个人。 wǒ de sù shè lǐ yǒu bā gè rén There are 8 people in my dorm.
日常对话 daily conversation
你爸妈做什么工作？ nǐ bà mā zuò shén me gōng zuò What do your parents do?
我不知道。 wǒ bù zhī dào I don't know.
你们家谁做饭？ nǐ men jiā shuí zuò fàn Who cooks in your family?
每个人。 měi gè rén Everyone.
你中午吃什么？ nǐ zhōng wǔ chī shén me What do you eat at noon?
我中午吃米饭和鱼。 wǒ zhōng wǔ chī mǐ fàn hé yú I eat rice and fish at noon.
你中午在哪儿吃饭？ nǐ zhōng wǔ zài nǎr chī fàn Where do you eat at noon?
我中午在家吃饭。 wǒ zhōng wǔ zài jiā chī fàn I eat at home at noon.
