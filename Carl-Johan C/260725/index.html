📚 Grammar
下个星期 next week
下个星期我要去出差。 xià gè xīng qī wǒ yào qù chū chāi Next week I am going on a business trip.
退休 to retire
他们退休了。 tā men tuì xiū le They have retired.
假期 / 年假 vacation / annual leave
年假有20天。 nián jià yǒu èr shí tiān There are 20 days of annual leave.
在东亚日本的公共假期最多。 zài dōng yà rì běn de gōng gòng jià qī zuì duō In East Asia Japan has the most public holidays.
一共 in total
公共假期一共有多少天？ gōng gòng jià qī yī gòng yǒu duō shǎo tiān How many public holidays are there in total?
古董店 / 仓库 antique shop / warehouse
这家古董店有几百件古董。 zhè jiā gǔ dǒng diàn yǒu jǐ bǎi jiàn gǔ dǒng This antique shop has hundreds of antiques.
分辨 to distinguish
我分不清真假古董。 wǒ fēn bu qīng zhēn jiǎ gǔ dǒng I cannot distinguish real from fake antiques.
很好骗 easy to fool
买古董的时候外行人很好骗。 mǎi gǔ dǒng de shí hou wài háng rén hěn hǎo piàn When buying antiques laymen are easy to fool.
鉴宝 antique appraisal
你这个东西不是商周的，是上周的。 nǐ zhè ge dōng xi bù shì shāng zhōu de, shì shàng zhōu de This thing of yours is not from Shang-Zhou its from last week.
永居 permanent residence
她有新加坡的永居。 tā yǒu xīn jiā pō de yǒng jū She has permanent residence in Singapore.
收拾行李 to pack luggage
我在收拾行李。 wǒ zài shōu shi xíng lǐ I am packing my luggage.
方面 aspect; field
我在金融方面的知识很多。 wǒ zài jīn róng fāng miàn de zhī shi hěn duō I have a lot of knowledge in finance.
她是设计方面的专家。 tā shì shè jì fāng miàn de zhuān jiā She is an expert in design.
我睡觉睡得很好。 wǒ shuì jiào shuì de hěn hǎo I sleep very well.
你昨天晚上是什么时候睡的？ nǐ zuó tiān wǎn shàng shì shén me shí hou shuì de What time did you go to bed last night?
我把我女朋友介绍给我爸妈。 wǒ bǎ wǒ nǚ péng yǒu jiè shào gěi wǒ bà mā I introduced my girlfriend to my parents.
我女朋友是韩国人但是她在新加坡长大。 wǒ nǚ péng yǒu shì hán guó rén dàn shì tā zài xīn jiā pō zhǎng dà My girlfriend is Korean but she grew up in Singapore.
在饭店的时候服务员跟我女朋友说中文但是她不知道怎么回答。 zài fàn diàn de shí hou fú wù yuán gēn wǒ nǚ péng yǒu shuō zhōng wén dàn shì tā bù zhī dào zěn me huí dá At the restaurant the waiter spoke Chinese to my girlfriend but she did not know how to reply.
