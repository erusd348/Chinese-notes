Merge the Sentences

1.
他tā喜xǐ欢huān流liú行xíng音yīn乐yuè。
他tā女nǚ朋péng友yǒu不bù喜xǐ欢huān夜yè店diàn。
Hints
但是 + contrast - he likes pop, but his girlfriend does not like nightclubs.
✍️ Combine: 但是
2.
越yuè南nán菜cài很hěn新xīn鲜xiān。
他tā们men用yòng了le很hěn多duō香xiāng料liào。
Hints
不但 + reason 1，而且 + reason 2 - both good qualities.
✍️ Combine: 不但…而且
3.
我wǒ想xiǎng去qù云yún南nán看kàn风fēng景jǐng。
我wǒ想xiǎng了liǎo解jiě那nà里lǐ的de文wén化huà。
Hints
又 + desire 1，又 + desire 2 — both things I want to do.
✍️ Combine: 又…又
4.
退tuì休xiū以yǐ后hòu他tā每měi天tiān去qù公gōng园yuán。
他tā还hái是shì很hěn忙máng。
Hints
虽然 + situation，但是 + result - he is still busy after retirement.
✍️ Combine: 虽然…但是

Rephrase

1.
云yún南nán的de景jǐng色sè特tè别bié美měi。
Say it differently — same meaning.
Hints
Change 很 into 极了 - the scenery is EXTREMELY beautiful.
✍️ Rephrase
2.
这zhè家jiā店diàn的de菜cài味wèi道dào很hěn好hǎo。
Say it differently — same meaning.
Hints
Change into 是…的 强调 - it IS this restaurant's food that tastes good.
✍️ Rephrase
3.
夜yè店diàn太tài吵chǎo了le。
Say it differently — same meaning.
Hints
Use 不能不 (double negative) — you CANNOT NOT find the nightclub too noisy.
✍️ Rephrase

Scenario — Complete the Dialogue

Class activity
1.
Situation: Your friend invites you to a nightclub on the weekend. You want to go to a restaurant to eat Vietnamese food instead. Respond politely. — respond appropriately.
同tóng学xué：周zhōu末mò我wǒ们men去qù夜yè店diàn蹦bèng迪dí吧ba！
你nǐ：______
同tóng学xué：为wèi什shén么me？夜yè店diàn多duō好hǎo玩wán啊a！
你nǐ：______
Hints
Politely suggest an alternative: 我觉得夜店太吵了 / 我更喜欢去餐厅吃越南菜 / 下次再去夜店吧.
