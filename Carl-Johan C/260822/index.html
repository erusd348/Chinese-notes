Merge the Sentences

1.
新xīn工gōng作zuò很hěn累lèi。
他tā觉jué得de很hěn好hǎo。
Hints
虽然 + concession，但是 + contrast — although the new job is tiring, he still thinks it is good.
✍️ Combine: 虽然…但是
2.
他tā能néng设shè计jì。
他tā能néng分fēn析xī风fēng险xiǎn。
Hints
不但 + ability 1，而且 + ability 2 — two skills building up.
✍️ Combine: 不但…而且
3.
他tā能néng去qù旅lǚ行xíng。
他tā能néng在zài家jiā休xiū息xī。
Hints
要么 + option A，要么 + option B — present two options, pick one.
✍️ Combine: 要么…要么

Rephrase — Say It Another Way

1.
他tā在zài家jiā设shè计jì。
Say it differently — same meaning.
Hints
Use 是…的 to emphasize WHERE (place): 他是在家设计的。
✍️ Rephrase
2.
买mǎi古gǔ董dǒng要yào问wèn专zhuān家jiā。
Say it differently — same meaning.
Hints
Change 要 into the double negative 不能不 — you CANNOT NOT ask an expert when buying antiques.
✍️ Rephrase
3.
他tā退tuì休xiū以yǐ后hòu，常cháng常cháng看kàn古gǔ董dǒng。
Say it differently — same meaning.
Hints
Add 还 to mean "still" (仍然): 他退休以后，还常常看古董。
✍️ Rephrase

Scenario — Complete the Dialogue

Class activity
1.
Situation: A friend invites you to travel during the National Day holiday. You want to rest at home. Refuse politely and suggest another day. — respond appropriately.
朋péng友yǒu：国guó庆qìng假jià期qī，我wǒ们men去qù旅lǚ行xíng吗ma？
你nǐ：______
朋péng友yǒu：为wèi什shén么me？
你nǐ：______
Hints
Refuse politely: thank them first (谢谢), then give a reason (我觉得很累 / 我打算在家休息), then suggest 下个星期再去.
🗣 Complete the dialogue
Class activity
2.
Situation: Ask your sister about her National Day holiday plan, then share yours. — respond appropriately.
妹mèi妹mèi：国guó庆qìng假jià期qī，你nǐ打dǎ算suàn做zuò什shén么me？

你nǐ：______
Hints
1) State your plan (我打算…). 2) Respond to her plan — show interest or suggest something together (那我们也…).
