📚 Grammar
潜水 diving; to dive
我朋友喜欢潜水。 wǒ péng you xǐ huan qián shuǐ. My friend likes diving.
腌 to marinate
炒肉前可以把肉腌一下。 chǎo ròu qián kě yǐ bǎ ròu yān yí xià. You can marinate the meat before stir-frying it.
调料 seasoning
中国菜用的调料很多。 zhōng guó cài yòng de tiáo liào hěn duō. Chinese food uses a lot of seasonings.
味精 MSG
做菜的时候，味精不要放太多。 zuò cài de shí hou, wèi jīng bú yào fàng tài duō. When cooking, don't add too much MSG.
生抽 light soy sauce
炒菜可以加一点生抽。 chǎo cài kě yǐ jiā yì diǎn shēng chōu. You can add a little light soy sauce when stir-frying.
生粉 starch
肉加一点生粉，会更嫩。 ròu jiā yì diǎn shēng fěn, huì gèng nèn. Add a little starch to the meat, and it will be more tender.
搅拌 to stir
把调料和肉搅拌一下。 bǎ tiáo liào hé ròu jiǎo bàn yí xià. Stir the seasonings and the meat together.
嫩 tender
牛肉炒得嫩一点才好吃。 niú ròu chǎo de nèn yì diǎn cái hǎo chī. Beef tastes better when stir-fried a bit more tender.
柴 dry, tough (of meat)
肉炒太久会柴。 ròu chǎo tài jiǔ huì chái. If you fry the meat too long it becomes tough and dry.
热锅冷油 hot wok, cold oil
炒肉要用热锅冷油。 chǎo ròu yào yòng rè guō lěng yóu. For stir-frying meat, use a hot wok with cold oil.
配菜 side dish
做菜的时候可以加点配菜。 zuò cài de shí hou kě yǐ jiā diǎn pèi cài. You can add some side vegetables when cooking.
