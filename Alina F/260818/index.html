📚 Grammar
S 是 n. (Structure) S is a noun
他是好人。 tā shì hǎo rén He is a good person.
你是好人。 nǐ shì hǎo rén You are a good person.
他不是好人。 tā bú shì hǎo rén He is not a good person.
V + 不起 (Structure) cannot afford to / to look down on
看不起 kàn bu qǐ to look down on
我看不起他，他不是好人。 wǒ kàn bu qǐ tā，tā bú shì hǎo rén I look down on him; he is not a good person.
买不起 mǎi bu qǐ cannot afford to buy
我买不起。 wǒ mǎi bu qǐ I can't afford it.
