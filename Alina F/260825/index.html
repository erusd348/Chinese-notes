📚 Grammar
Statement + 吗？ Yes/no question with 吗
你好吗？ nǐ hǎo ma How are you?
他是好人吗？ tā shì hǎo rén ma Is he a good person?
你是艾莉娜吗？ nǐ shì ài lì nà ma Are you Alina?
S + 和 + N + 关系 + 好吗？ How is the relationship between S and N?
你爸爸妈妈好吗？ nǐ bà ba mā ma hǎo ma Are your parents well?
你和你爸爸妈妈关系好吗？ nǐ hé nǐ bà ba mā ma guān xi hǎo ma How is your relationship with your parents?
是的，我们关系很好。 shì de，wǒ men guān xi hěn hǎo Yes, we have a good relationship.
Pronoun + 呢？ And...? / What about...?
你爸爸妈妈呢？ nǐ bà ba mā ma ne How about your parents?
我很好，你呢？ wǒ hěn hǎo，nǐ ne I'm fine, and you?
S + 也 + Adj also... (也 = also, too)
我爸爸妈妈也很好。 wǒ bà ba mā ma yě hěn hǎo My parents are also very well.
我也很好。 wǒ yě hěn hǎo I'm fine too.
我有 / 没有 + N I have / don't have...
我有哥哥。 wǒ yǒu gē ge I have an older brother.
我没有哥哥。 wǒ méi yǒu gē ge I don't have an older brother.
他是我哥哥，我是他妹妹。 tā shì wǒ gē ge，wǒ shì tā mèi mei He is my older brother; I am his younger sister.
我们是兄妹。 wǒ men shì xiōng mèi We are brother and sister.
你妈妈有姐姐吗？ nǐ mā ma yǒu jiě jie ma Does your mom have an older sister?
我爸爸有哥哥。 wǒ bà ba yǒu gē ge My dad has an older brother.
你是我的好姐妹。 nǐ shì wǒ de hǎo jiě mèi You are my dear sister (sisterhood).
你有兄弟姐妹吗？ nǐ yǒu xiōng dì jiě mèi ma Do you have siblings?
