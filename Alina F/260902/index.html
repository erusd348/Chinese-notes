📚 Grammar
了 (Particle) 了 after a verb = the action is finished .
我吃饭了。 wǒ chī fàn le. I have eaten.
我吃早饭了。 wǒ chī zǎo fàn le. I have had breakfast.
S V 了吗？ (Structure) have you V-ed yet?
你吃饭了吗？ nǐ chī fàn le ma? Have you eaten?
你吃早饭了吗？ nǐ chī zǎo fàn le ma? Have you had breakfast?
我没（有）V。 (Structure) 没 for PAST: didn't / haven't
我没吃饭。 wǒ méi chī fàn. I haven't eaten.
我没吃早饭。 wǒ méi chī zǎo fàn. I didn't eat breakfast.
我不 V。 (Structure) 不 for HABIT: don't (not past)
我不吃饭。 wǒ bù chī fàn. I don't eat.
我不吃早饭。 wǒ bù chī zǎo fàn. I don't eat breakfast.
什么 what (question word)
你晚上吃什么？ nǐ wǎn shang chī shén me? What do you eat in the evening?
我晚上吃米饭。 wǒ wǎn shang chī mǐ fàn. I eat rice in the evening.
