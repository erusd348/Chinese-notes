📚 Grammar
去年 last year
去年我申请了去中国留学。 qù nián wǒ shēn qǐng le qù zhōng guó liú xué Last year I applied to study in China.
除了 except; besides
他除了打游戏什么都不做。 tā chú le dǎ yóu xì shén me dōu bù zuò He does nothing except play games.
精彩 wonderful; splendid
昨天的电影很精彩。 zuó tiān de diàn yǐng hěn jīng cǎi Yesterdays movie was wonderful.
无聊 boring
周末在家很无聊。 zhōu mò zài jiā hěn wú liáo Staying home on weekends is boring.
申请 to apply
我想申请去中国留学。 wǒ xiǎng shēn qǐng qù zhōng guó liú xué I want to apply to study in China.
放弃 to give up
遇到困难不要放弃。 yù dào kùn nán bù yào fàng qì Do not give up when facing difficulties.
充满 to be full of
她的眼睛充满了泪水。 tā de yǎn jīng chōng mǎn le lèi shuǐ Her eyes were full of tears.
真诚 sincere
他是一个很真诚的人。 tā shì yī gè hěn zhēn chéng de rén He is a very sincere person.
受洗 to be baptized
他在教堂受洗了。 tā zài jiào táng shòu xǐ le He was baptized at church.
醒过来 to wake up
他从休克中醒过来了。 tā cóng xiū kè zhōng xǐng guò lái le He woke up from the coma.
考验 test; trial
这是上帝对我的考验。 zhè shì shàng dì duì wǒ de kǎo yàn This is God's test for me.
彼此 each other
我们需要彼此帮助。 wǒ men xū yào bǐ cǐ bāng zhù We need to help each other.
浪漫 romantic
他们有一个浪漫的约会。 tā men yǒu yī gè làng màn de yuē huì They had a romantic date.
祷告 to pray
他每天早上都祷告。 tā měi tiān zǎo shang dōu dǎo gào He prays every morning.
耶稣基督爱的四个维度：宽度长度高度深度。 yē sū jī dū ài de sì gè wéi dù The four dimensions of Jesus Christs love.
第二个弟兄讲的是关于精神和信仰的统一。 dì èr gè dì xiōng jiǎng de shì guān yú jīng shén hé xìn yǎng de tǒng yī The second brother spoke about the unity of spirit and faith.
上帝无法解开的神秘。 shàng dì wú fǎ jiě kāi de shén mì The mystery that God cannot unravel.
我有一个室友在做生意。 wǒ yǒu yī gè shì yǒu zài zuò shēng yì I have a roommate who does business.
他在监狱里接受审讯。 tā zài jiān yù lǐ jiē shòu shěn xùn He is undergoing interrogation in prison.
唱诗班唱得非常好听。 chàng shī bān chàng de fēi cháng hǎo tīng The choir sang beautifully.
