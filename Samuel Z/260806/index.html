📚 Grammar
打包 + V + 给 + sb to pack up and give to someone
我们打包食物送给老人。 wǒ men dǎ bāo shí wù sòng gěi lǎo rén We pack food and give it to the elderly.
又 + V + 又 + V both...and... (double pattern)
又怕兄弟苦，又怕兄弟开路虎。 yòu pà xiōng dì kǔ, yòu pà xiōng dì kāi lù hǔ Afraid your brother suffers, but also afraid he drives a Land Rover.
是 + time + V + 的 it was...that... (emphasis)
这个墨西哥卷是我前天买的。 zhè gè mò xī gē juǎn shì wǒ qián tiān mǎi de This burrito is the one I bought the day before yesterday.
有一点 + Adj a little bit...
墨西哥卷有一点辣、一点咸、很多汁、口感很好。 mò xī gē juǎn yǒu yī diǎn là, yī diǎn xián, hěn duō zhī, kǒu gǎn hěn hǎo The burrito is a bit spicy, a bit salty, very juicy, and has a great texture.
