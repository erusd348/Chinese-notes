📚 Grammar
签合同 to sign a contract
我下个月签合同。 wǒ xià gè yuè qiān hé tong. I will sign the contract next month.
提前 in advance
他提前来了。 tā tí qián lái le. He came early.
录取 to admit; to be accepted
我被大学录取了。 wǒ bèi dà xué lù qǔ le. I was accepted by the university.
推迟 to postpone; push back
小测验推迟了。 xiǎo cè yàn tuī chí le. The quiz was postponed.
小测验 quiz
明天有小测验。 míng tiān yǒu xiǎo cè yàn. There is a quiz tomorrow.
贷款 loan
他贷款买房子。 tā dài kuǎn mǎi fáng zi. He took a loan to buy a house.
奖学金 scholarship
她拿到了奖学金。 tā ná dào le jiǎng xué jīn. She got a scholarship.
学费 tuition
大学的学费很贵。 dà xué de xué fèi hěn guì. University tuition is expensive.
嫩 tender
这个鸡肉很嫩。 zhè ge jī ròu hěn nèn. This chicken is very tender.
老 tough (of meat); old
肉太老了。 ròu tài lǎo le. The meat is too tough.
味道 taste; flavor
这个菜味道很好。 zhè ge cài wèi dào hěn hǎo. This dish tastes very good.
卷饼 wrap; rolled pancake
卷饼要厚一点。 juǎn bǐng yào hòu yì diǎn. The wrap should be a bit thicker.
糖 sugar
我要少糖。 wǒ yào shǎo táng. I want less sugar.
泡沫 foam
多加泡沫。 duō jiā pào mò. Add more foam.
波霸 boba
我要波霸。 wǒ yào bō bà. I want boba.
被 + V (Structure) 被 — passive voice (subject is acted upon)
我被大学录取了。 wǒ bèi dà xué lù qǔ le. I was accepted by the university.
V + 得 + 补语（一点） (Structure) Verb + 得 + complement (a little bit)
鸡肉要切得细一点。 jī ròu yào qiē de xì yì diǎn. Cut the chicken a bit thinner.
烤得嫩一点。 kǎo de nèn yì diǎn. Grill it a bit more tender.
多 / 少 + N (Structure) more / less + noun
少糖，多加泡沫和波霸。 shǎo táng, duō jiā pào mò hé bō bà. Less sugar, add more foam and boba.
生菜和酱都多一点。 shēng cài hé jiàng dōu duō yì diǎn. More lettuce and sauce.
味道辣一点。 wèi dào là yì diǎn. Make the flavor a bit spicier.
