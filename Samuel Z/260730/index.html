📚 Grammar
如果 + condition + 就 + result if...then...
如果不看iMax，电影的画面显示不全。 rú guǒ bù kàn iMax, diàn yǐng de huà miàn xiǎn shì bù quán If you don't watch in iMax, the picture won't display fully.
如果电影里有血腥或者裸露的场景就会被删减。 rú guǒ diàn yǐng lǐ yǒu xuè xīng huò zhě luǒ lù de chǎng jǐng jiù huì bèi shān jiǎn If a movie has gory or nude scenes, they will be cut.
被 + V passive voice
他的门被胶带封住了。 tā de mén bèi jiāo dài fēng zhù le His door was sealed with tape.
我在中学的时候被人欺负过。 wǒ zài zhōng xué de shí hou bèi rén qī fu guò I was bullied in middle school.
从……开始…… since (starting from)
他们从中学开始就认识了。 tā men cóng zhōng xué kāi shǐ jiù rèn shi le They've known each other since middle school.
给 + sb + 起 + 外号 give someone a nickname
有人给她起了一个外号。 yǒu rén gěi tā qǐ le yī ge wài hào Someone gave her a nickname.
情节 / 演技 / 结局 movie vocabulary
一个电影我只看一遍。 yī ge diàn yǐng wǒ zhǐ kàn yī biàn I only watch a movie once.
我不在乎情节，我在乎演技。 wǒ bù zài hu qíng jié, wǒ zài hu yǎn jì I don't care about the plot, I care about the acting.
我想成为她在这个世界上最爱的人。 wǒ xiǎng chéng wéi tā zài zhè ge shì jiè shàng zuì ài de rén I want to be the person she loves most in this world.
