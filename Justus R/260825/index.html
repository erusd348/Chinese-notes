📚 Grammar
真 + Adj (Structure) really + Adj (emphasizes the adjective)
这个孩子真可爱。 zhè ge hái zi zhēn kě ài This child is really cute.
这个菜真好吃。 zhè ge cài zhēn hǎo chī This dish is really delicious.
我太太真漂亮。 wǒ tài tai zhēn piào liang My wife is really pretty.
真的假的？ zhēn de jiǎ de Really? / Is that true?
想 + V (Structure) want to do sth
你想要孩子吗？ nǐ xiǎng yào hái zi ma Do you want to have kids?
我年纪太大了。 wǒ nián jì tài dà le I'm too old.
