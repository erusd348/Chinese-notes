📖 Vocabulary
几岁 jǐ suì how old (for children)
多大 duō dà how old (for adults/peers)
年纪 nián jì age
岁 suì years old
今年 jīn nián this year
太太 tài tai wife
📚 Grammar
几岁 asking a child's age
小朋友，你几岁？ xiǎo péng yǒu, nǐ jǐ suì Little friend, how old are you?
我5岁。 wǒ wǔ suì I'm 5 years old.
多大 asking a peer's age
你多大？ nǐ duō dà How old are you?
我今年45岁。 wǒ jīn nián sì shí wǔ suì I'm 45 this year.
你太太今年多大？ nǐ tài tai jīn nián duō dà How old is your wife this year?
我太太今年40岁。 wǒ tài tai jīn nián sì shí suì My wife is 40 this year.
多大年纪 asking an elder's age (polite)
您多大年纪？ nín duō dà nián jì How old are you? (polite)
我今年70岁。 wǒ jīn nián qī shí suì I'm 70 this year.
你爸爸多大年纪？ nǐ bà ba duō dà nián jì How old is your dad?
我爸爸今年68岁。 wǒ bà ba jīn nián liù shí bā suì My dad is 68 this year.
你妈妈今年多大年纪？ nǐ mā ma jīn nián duō dà nián jì How old is your mom this year?
我妈妈今年65岁。 wǒ mā ma jīn nián liù shí wǔ suì My mom is 65 this year.
年纪 age (as a noun)
他年纪很大。 tā nián jì hěn dà He is very old.
你年纪不小了。 nǐ nián jì bù xiǎo le You're not young anymore.
多大 how big (non-age usage)
你的房子多大？ nǐ de fáng zi duō dà How big is your house?
