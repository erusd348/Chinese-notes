📚 Grammar
开始 / 结束 to start / to end
我们的课9点开始，9点50结束。 wǒ men de kè jiǔ diǎn kāi shǐ, jiǔ diǎn wǔ shí jié shù Our class starts at 9 and ends at 9:50.
我们的关系结束了。 wǒ men de guān xi jié shù le Our relationship is over.
刚 + V + 了 just (did something recently)
我刚开始了一个新关系。 wǒ gāng kāi shǐ le yī gè xīn guān xi I just started a new relationship.
我的新关系刚开始了。 wǒ de xīn guān xi gāng kāi shǐ le My new relationship has just started.
迟到 to be late
他上学迟到了。 tā shàng xué chí dào le He was late for school.
他常常上班迟到。 tā cháng cháng shàng bān chí dào He is often late for work.
楼上 / 楼下 upstairs / downstairs
他在楼下。 tā zài lóu xià He is downstairs.
