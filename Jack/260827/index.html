📚 Grammar
Pronoun + 们 (Structure) pronoun + 们 = plural form
我们 wǒ men we
你们 nǐ men you all
他们 tā men they (mixed or male)
她们 tā men they (female)
S + Adv + Adj (Structure) subject + adverb + adjective
我很好。 wǒ hěn hǎo. I'm very well.
他很好。 tā hěn hǎo. He is very well.
Statement + 吗？ (Structure) adding 吗 makes a yes/no question
你好吗？ nǐ hǎo ma? How are you?
S + 是 + N (Structure) subject + 是 + noun
你是杰克吗？ nǐ shì jié kè ma? Are you Jack?
是的，我是杰克。 shì de, wǒ shì jié kè. Yes, I am Jack.
