📚 Grammar
谢谢 + N (Structure) to thank someone
谢谢你！ xiè xie nǐ! Thank you!
谢谢您！ xiè xie nín! Thank you (polite)!
谢谢老师！ xiè xie lǎo shī! Thank you, teacher!
谢谢你们！ xiè xie nǐ men! Thank you all!
不用 + V (Structure) no need to do (something)
不用谢！ bú yòng xiè! You're welcome!
你不用谢他。 nǐ bú yòng xiè tā. You don't need to thank him.
你不用谢我，我是你老师。 nǐ bú yòng xiè wǒ, wǒ shì nǐ lǎo shī. You don't need to thank me, I'm your teacher.
不 + predicate (Adj / 是+N) (Structure) not + adjective / not + to be
他不好。 tā bù hǎo. He is not good.
我不老。 wǒ bù lǎo. I am not old.
他很老。 tā hěn lǎo. He is very old.
我不是何泰乐。 wǒ bú shì hé tài lè. I am not He Taile.
不客气 / 客气 You're welcome / polite
不客气！ bú kè qi! You're welcome!
他是我客人。 tā shì wǒ kè rén. He is my guest.
我很气。 wǒ hěn qì. I am very angry.
