📚 Grammar
对不起 对 = to face + 不起 = cannot rise .
我对不起你！ wǒ duì bu qǐ nǐ! I let you down — I can't face you.
V + 不起 (Structure) cannot bear to do (something)
我看不起他。 wǒ kàn bu qǐ tā. I look down on him.
他不是好人。我看不起他。 tā bú shì hǎo rén. wǒ kàn bu qǐ tā. He is not a good person. I look down on him.
不好意思 不 = not + 好意思 = at ease → embarrassed (LIGHT; cf. 对不起 = SERIOUS)
他很不好意思。 tā hěn bù hǎo yì si. He is very embarrassed.
没关系 it doesn't matter
没关系！ méi guān xi! It doesn't matter!
有 / 没有 + N (Structure) to have / not have (something)
你有老师吗？ nǐ yǒu lǎo shī ma? Do you have a teacher?
我有老师。 wǒ yǒu lǎo shī. I have a teacher.
我没有女朋友。 wǒ méi yǒu nǚ péng you. I don't have a girlfriend.
