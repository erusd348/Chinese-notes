Combine the Sentences

Join the two sentences with the given connector

1.
他tā经jīng验yàn很hěn丰fēng富fù。
大dà家jiā都dōu喜xǐ欢huān他tā。
Hints
因为…所以 = because…so: 因为 + reason (his rich experience), 所以 + result (people like him). Put the reason first.
✍️ Combine: 因为…所以
2.
你nǐ已yǐ经jīng工gōng作zuò了le。
你nǐ可kě以yǐ考kǎo证zhèng了le。
Hints
既然…就 = since…then: 既然 + a known fact, 就 + the natural conclusion. Use 就 before the verb.
✍️ Combine: 既然…就
