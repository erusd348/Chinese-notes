📚 Grammar
时区 time zone
船每开到一个新时区，我们都要调时间。 chuán měi kāi dào yī gè xīn shí qū, wǒ men dōu yào tiáo shí jiān Every time the ship enters a new time zone, we have to adjust the time.
全面检修 full overhaul (of the ship's engine)
船靠港以后，轮机舱要做一次全面检修。 chuán kào gǎng yǐ hòu, lún jī cāng yào zuò yī cì quán miàn jiǎn xiū After the ship docks, the engine room needs a full overhaul.
六十多个小时 more than sixty hours
这次的全面检修做了六十多个小时，大家都累了。 zhè cì de quán miàn jiǎn xiū zuò le liù shí duō gè xiǎo shí, dà jiā dōu lèi le This overhaul took more than sixty hours — everyone is tired.
轮机舱 engine room
我每天在轮机舱工作八个小时。 wǒ měi tiān zài lún jī cāng gōng zuò bā gè xiǎo shí I work in the engine room eight hours a day.
长痘 to get pimples (from stress or fatigue)
最近总是加班，我脸上长了很多痘。 zuì jìn zǒng shì jiā bān, wǒ liǎn shàng zhǎng le hěn duō dòu I've been working overtime a lot lately, and I've gotten a lot of pimples on my face.
加班费 overtime pay
加班有加班费，所以他愿意周末来。 jiā bān yǒu jiā bān fèi, suǒ yǐ tā yuàn yì zhōu mò lái Overtime comes with overtime pay, so he's willing to come on weekends.
巡逻 to patrol
海警的船每天早上出去巡逻。 hǎi jǐng de chuán měi tiān zǎo shang chū qù xún luó The coast guard ships go out on patrol every morning.
文案工作 paperwork, desk work
他不喜欢文案工作，喜欢动手修东西。 tā bù xǐ huan wén àn gōng zuò, xǐ huan dòng shǒu xiū dōng xi He doesn't like desk work — he likes to fix things with his hands.
危险 dangerous
这个工作有点危险，所以一定要小心。 zhè ge gōng zuò yǒu diǎn wēi xiǎn, suǒ yǐ yī dìng yào xiǎo xīn This job is a bit dangerous, so you must be careful.
有的 some (people or things)
船上有的人很开朗，有的人很内向。 chuán shàng yǒu de rén hěn kāi lǎng, yǒu de rén hěn nèi xiàng Some people on the ship are very outgoing, and some are very introverted.
瑞典 Sweden
我们的船去过瑞典，那里的夏天白天很长。 wǒ men de chuán qù guò ruì diǎn, nà lǐ de xià tiān bái tiān hěn cháng Our ship has been to Sweden — the summer days there are very long.
性格 personality
每个人的性格都不一样。 měi gè rén de xìng gé dōu bù yī yàng Everyone's personality is different.
性格好 to have a good personality
我们船长性格好，大家都愿意跟他工作。 wǒ men chuán zhǎng xìng gé hǎo, dà jiā dōu yuàn yì gēn tā gōng zuò Our captain has a great personality — everyone likes working with him.
好相处 easy to get along with
他很外向，也特别好相处。 tā hěn wài xiàng, yě tè bié hǎo xiāng chǔ He's very outgoing and really easy to get along with.
内向 introverted
我比较内向，跟很多人在一起的时候，我觉得很累。 wǒ bǐ jiào nèi xiàng, gēn hěn duō rén zài yī qǐ de shí hou, wǒ jué dé hěn lèi I'm rather introverted — when I'm with a lot of people, I feel tired.
外向 extroverted
我女朋友很外向，到哪里都能交到朋友。 wǒ nǚ péng you hěn wài xiàng, dào nǎ li dōu néng jiāo dào péng you My girlfriend is very extroverted — she makes friends wherever she goes.
特点 (personality) trait, characteristic
你能说说你女朋友的性格特点吗？ nǐ néng shuō shuo nǐ nǚ péng you de xìng gé tè diǎn ma Can you tell me about your girlfriend's personality traits?
开朗 cheerful, outgoing
他性格很开朗，总是笑着跟人说话。 tā xìng gé hěn kāi lǎng, zǒng shì xiào zhe gēn rén shuō huà He has a very cheerful personality and always talks to people with a smile.
互补 complementary (personalities that fit each other)
他外向，我内向，我们的性格很互补。 tā wài xiàng, wǒ nèi xiàng, wǒ men de xìng gé hěn hù bǔ He's extroverted and I'm introverted — our personalities complement each other well.
有的时候 sometimes
有的时候我想一个人待着，什么都不想。 yǒu de shí hou wǒ xiǎng yī gè rén dāi zhe, shén me dōu bù xiǎng Sometimes I want to be alone and think about nothing at all.
例如 for example
我喜欢有规律的生活，例如每天早上拉伸、冥想。 wǒ xǐ huan yǒu guī lǜ de shēng huó, lì rú měi tiān zǎo shang lā shēn, míng xiǎng I like a regular life — for example, stretching and meditating every morning.
交朋友 to make friends
在船上工作，交朋友的机会不多。 zài chuán shàng gōng zuò, jiāo péng you de jī hui bù duō Working on a ship, there aren't many chances to make friends.
开玩笑 to joke, to make jokes
他喜欢开玩笑，跟他在一起很轻松。 tā xǐ huan kāi wán xiào, gēn tā zài yī qǐ hěn qīng sōng He likes to joke around — being with him feels relaxed.
过头 to go too far
玩笑开过头，别人会不高兴。 wán xiào kāi guò tóu, bié rén huì bù gāo xìng If a joke goes too far, people will get upset.
介绍 to introduce
我来介绍一下，这位是我的同事。 wǒ lái jiè shào yī xià, zhè wèi shì wǒ de tóng shì Let me introduce — this is my coworker.
