🚢 课文：船上的一天 text: a day on the ship
总的来说，船上的生活很好，很有规律。 zǒng de lái shuō, chuán shàng de shēng huó hěn hǎo, hěn yǒu guī lǜ Overall, life on the ship is very good and regular.
我每天7点起床，起床后，我做拉伸、洗脸、刷牙、刮胡子。做完这些后我会冥想五分钟，因为冥想可以让我的工作更专注。 wǒ měi tiān qī diǎn qǐ chuáng, qǐ chuáng hòu, wǒ zuò lā shēn, xǐ liǎn, shuā yá, guā hú zi。zuò wán zhè xiē hòu wǒ huì míng xiǎng wǔ fēn zhōng, yīn wèi míng xiǎng kě yǐ ràng wǒ de gōng zuò gèng zhuān zhù I get up at 7 every day. After getting up, I stretch, wash my face, brush my teeth, and shave. After that, I meditate for five minutes, because meditation makes my work more focused.
早上我不吃早饭，我只喝蛋白奶昔，因为我没有时间。 zǎo shang wǒ bù chī zǎo fàn, wǒ zhǐ hē dàn bái nǎi xī, yīn wèi wǒ méi yǒu shí jiān I don't eat breakfast in the morning. I only drink a protein shake, because I don't have time.
我从上午八点工作到下午五点，中午有一个小时休息。我的主要工作是维护和修理船上的机器。 wǒ cóng shàng wǔ bā diǎn gōng zuò dào xià wǔ wǔ diǎn, zhōng wǔ yǒu yí gè xiǎo shí xiū xi。wǒ de zhǔ yào gōng zuò shì wéi hù hé xiū lǐ chuán shàng de jī qì I work from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., with one hour off at noon. My main job is to maintain and repair the machines on the ship.
中午我在食堂吃午饭，船上的菜很好吃。因为船上只有韩国人和缅甸人，所以食堂里也只有韩国菜和缅甸菜。 zhōng wǔ wǒ zài shí táng chī wǔ fàn, chuán shàng de cài hěn hǎo chī。yīn wèi chuán shàng zhǐ yǒu hán guó rén hé miǎn diàn rén, suǒ yǐ shí táng lǐ yě zhǐ yǒu hán guó cài hé miǎn diàn cài At noon I eat lunch in the cafeteria. The food on the ship is delicious. Because there are only Koreans and Burmese on the ship, the cafeteria only has Korean and Burmese food.
下班后吃完晚饭，我去健身房健身。健完身，我先洗澡，然后给我女朋友打电话、拉伸、看书，最后11点睡觉。这就是我的一天。 xià bān hòu chī wán wǎn fàn, wǒ qù jiàn shēn fáng jiàn shēn。jiàn wán shēn, wǒ xiān xǐ zǎo, rán hòu gěi wǒ nǚ péng yǒu dǎ diàn huà, lā shēn, kàn shū, zuì hòu shí yī diǎn shuì jiào。zhè jiù shì wǒ de yì tiān After work and dinner, I go to the gym to work out. After working out, I take a shower, then call my girlfriend, stretch, and read. Finally, I go to bed at 11. That is my day.
