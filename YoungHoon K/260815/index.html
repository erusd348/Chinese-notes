🧘 课文：冥想练习 text: meditation practice
先坐下来，设一个5分钟的计时器，闭上眼睛，深呼吸，放空大脑，把注意力集中在呼吸上。如果出现杂念，不要跟着它走，重新把注意力拉回到呼吸上，直到计时器响。 xiān zuò xià lái, shè yí gè wǔ fēn zhōng de jì shí qì, bì shàng yǎn jīng, shēn hū xī, fàng kōng dà nǎo, bǎ zhù yì lì jí zhōng zài hū xī shàng。rú guǒ chū xiàn zá niàn, bú yào gēn zhe tā zǒu, chóng xīn bǎ zhù yì lì lā huí dào hū xī shàng, zhí dào jì shí qì xiǎng Sit down, set a 5-minute timer, close your eyes, take deep breaths, empty your mind, and focus your attention on your breathing. If distracting thoughts come up, don't follow them — just bring your attention back to your breath until the timer goes off.
