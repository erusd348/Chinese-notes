📚 Grammar
想 + V (Structure) want to + verb
你想买什么？ nǐ xiǎng mǎi shén me What do you want to buy?
我想买书。 wǒ xiǎng mǎi shū I want to buy a book.
有 / 没有 (Structure) have / don't have
你有书吗？ nǐ yǒu shū ma Do you have any books?
我有书。 wǒ yǒu shū I have books.
我没有笔，我想买笔。 wǒ méi yǒu bǐ，wǒ xiǎng mǎi bǐ I don't have a pen, so I want to buy one.
