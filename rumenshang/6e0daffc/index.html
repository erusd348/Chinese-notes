💬 Dialogue
💬 D1_
现在几点？Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?What time is it now?
现在七点一刻。Xiànzài qī diǎn yī kè.It's 7:15.
你几点吃早饭？Nǐ jǐ diǎn chī zǎofàn?What time do you eat breakfast?
我七点半吃早饭。Wǒ qī diǎn bàn chī zǎofàn.I eat breakfast at 7:30.
你什么时候上课？Nǐ shénme shíhou shàngkè?When do you have class?
我从上午八点到十二点上课。Wǒ cóng shàngwǔ bā diǎn dào shí'èr diǎn shàngkè.I have class from 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM.
💬 D2_
今天几月几号？Jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào?What's today's date?
今天七月二十二号。Jīntiān qī yuè èrshí'èr hào.Today is July 22nd.
今天星期几？Jīntiān xīngqī jǐ?What day of the week is it?
今天星期三。Jīntiān xīngqīsān.Today is Wednesday.
二十五号是星期天吧？Èrshíwǔ hào shì xīngqītiān ba?The 25th is Sunday, right?
二十五号不是星期天，是星期六。Èrshíwǔ hào bú shì xīngqītiān, shì xīngqīliù.The 25th is not Sunday — it's Saturday.

好啊！什么时候出发？Hǎo a! Shénme shíhou chūfā?Great! What time do we leave?

📝 Notes
Days Around Today
Chinese builds the calendar out of 天 plus a direction word: 前天 (the day before yesterday) and 后天 (the day after tomorrow). Add 大 for one more step: 大前天, 大后天.
昨天是星期一。zuó tiān shì xīng qī yī。Yesterday was Monday.
明天见。míng tiān jiàn。See you tomorrow.
咱们 vs. 我们 — Who Is Included
咱们 means “you and I” — it pulls the listener in and sounds warm, so it is the natural choice for suggestions. 我们 is plain “we” and may or may not include you.
咱们去颐和园吧。zán men qù yí hé yuán ba。Let’s go to the Summer Palace.
我们去颐和园。wǒ men qù yí hé yuán。We are going to the Summer Palace.
📚 Grammar
1. 几点 — Asking the Time
几点
Ask with 现在几点？ and answer with number + 点, then the minutes. 点 is the only time word you must have; the rest come with practice.
现在几点？xiàn zài jǐ diǎn？What time is it now?
现在七点一刻。xiàn zài qī diǎn yī kè。It is 7:15 now.
2. Telling the Time
点 / 刻 / 半 / 分 / 差
点 is the hour, 刻 a quarter, 半 half, 分 the minute. For “to” use 差 in front: 九点差五分 = five to nine (8:55).
七点一刻。qī diǎn yī kè。7:15.
现在七点半。xiàn zài qī diǎn bàn。It is 7:30 now.
九点差五分。jiǔ diǎn chà wǔ fēn。Five to nine (8:55).
3. 从……到…… — From … To …
从 + Start + 到 + End
从 marks the starting point and 到 the end. The pair works for time and for place alike, so one pattern covers a lot of ground.
从八点到十二点。cóng bā diǎn dào shí èr diǎn。From 8:00 to 12:00.
从星期一到星期五。cóng xīng qī yī dào xīng qī wǔ。From Monday to Friday.
4. 怎么样 / 好吗 / 吧 — Making Suggestions
Suggestion + 怎么样 / 好吗 / 吧
Three ways to turn a plan into an invitation: 怎么样 or 好吗 ask for agreement, while 吧 at the end makes it a firm “let us…”.
咱们去颐和园，怎么样？zán men qù yí hé yuán， zěn me yàng？Let’s go to the Summer Palace, OK?
上午九点，行吗？shàng wǔ jiǔ diǎn， xíng ma？9:00 in the morning, all right?
我们去颐和园吧。wǒ men qù yí hé yuán ba。Let’s go to the Summer Palace.
