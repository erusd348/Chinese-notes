💬 Dialogue
💬 D1_
保罗：你们好，认识你们很高兴。Nǐmen hǎo, rènshi nǐmen hěn gāoxìng.Hello everyone, nice to meet you.
我来介绍一下。Wǒ lái jièshào yíxià.Let me introduce.
我是德国留学生保罗。Wǒ shì Déguó liúxuéshēng Bǎoluó.I am Paul, a German exchange student.
我学习汉语。Wǒ xuéxí Hànyǔ.I study Chinese.
我是A班的学生。Wǒ shì A bān de xuésheng.I am a student of Class A.
这是我们的老师。Zhè shì wǒmen de lǎoshī.This is our teacher.
那是我朋友西蒙和李英男。Nà shì wǒ péngyou Xīméng hé Lǐ Yīngnán.Those are my friends Simon and Li Yingnan.
他们也是留学生。Tāmen yě shì liúxuéshēng.They are also exchange students.
他们都不是德国人。Tāmen dōu bú shì Déguó rén.None of them are German.
西蒙是法国人。Xīméng shì Fǎguó rén.Simon is French.
李英男是韩国人。Lǐ Yīngnán shì Hánguó rén.Li Yingnan is Korean.
💬 D2_
保罗：这是我们的教室。Zhè shì wǒmen de jiàoshì.This is our classroom.
我们的教室不大。Wǒmen de jiàoshì bú dà.Our classroom is not big.
这是我的汉语书。Zhè shì wǒ de Hànyǔ shū.This is my Chinese book.
我的书很新。Wǒ de shū hěn xīn.My book is very new.
那不是我的词典，那是我同学的词典。Nà bú shì wǒ de cídiǎn, nà shì wǒ tóngxué de cídiǎn.That's not my dictionary — it's my classmate's.
他的词典很厚。Tā de cídiǎn hěn hòu.His dictionary is very thick.
💬 D3_
保罗：这是我爸爸。Zhè shì wǒ bàba.This is my dad.
这是我妈妈。Zhè shì wǒ māma.This is my mom.
这是我爱人，她漂亮极了。Zhè shì wǒ àiren, tā piàoliang jí le.This is my wife — she is extremely pretty.
这是我们的女儿，她很聪明，也很可爱。Zhè shì wǒmen de nǚ'ér, tā hěn cōngming, yě hěn kě'ài.This is our daughter — she's smart and cute.
你们看，这是我，很帅。Nǐmen kàn, zhè shì wǒ, hěn shuài.Look — this is me, very handsome.
📝 Notes
When 的 Can Be Dropped
With close family, your own teacher, or your own class and school, 的 is usually left out: 我爸爸, 我老师, 我们班. Add it back and the sentence is still correct, just slightly more formal.
我爸爸。wǒ bà ba。My dad.
我老师。wǒ lǎo shi。My teacher.
我们班。wǒ men bān。Our class.
一下儿 — Softening a Verb
Adding 一下儿 after a verb makes the action short and casual — the difference between “Please introduce them” and “Just tell me a bit about them”. It is one of the easiest ways to sound less blunt.
介绍一下儿。jiè shào yī xià ér。Let me introduce (briefly).
你看一下儿。nǐ kàn yī xià ér。Have a quick look.
📚 Grammar
1. 是 — Linking Two Nouns
A + 是 + B
是 connects two nouns to identify someone or something. Do NOT use it with adjectives — for those, go straight to 很 (see point 3).
我是留学生。wǒ shì liú xué sheng。I am an international student.
这是我们的老师。zhè shì wǒ men de lǎo shi。This is our teacher.
他们都是留学生。tā men dōu shì liú xué sheng。They are all international students.
2. 的 — Possession and Modification
A + 的 + B
的 links the owner or modifier to what follows: 我的汉语书 (my Chinese book). It also links a long modifier to a noun: 我同学的词典.
我们的教室。wǒ men de jiào shì。Our classroom.
我的汉语书。wǒ de hàn yǔ shū。My Chinese book.
我同学的词典。wǒ tóng xué de cí diǎn。My classmate’s dictionary.
3. 很 + Adjective — Describing
Subject + 很 + Adjective
Adjective predicates need no verb. Put 很 in front and negate with 不 (不大, 不新). Note that 很 here is often just a linker, not a strong “very”.
她很漂亮。tā hěn piào liang。She is pretty.
他很帅。tā hěn shuài。He is handsome.
我们的教室不大。wǒ men de jiào shì bù dà。Our classroom is not big.
