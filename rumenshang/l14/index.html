💬 Dialogue
💬 D1_
听说四川菜很好吃，咱们去尝尝，好吗？Tīngshuō Sìchuān cài hěn hǎochī, zánmen qù chángchang, hǎo ma?I heard Sichuan food is delicious — let's go try it, OK?
好啊，什么时候去？Hǎo a, shénme shíhou qù?Great! When should we go?
今天晚上怎么样？Jīntiān wǎnshang zěnmeyàng?How about tonight?
今天晚上我有事，明天中午好吗？Jīntiān wǎnshang wǒ yǒu shì, míngtiān zhōngwǔ hǎo ma?I have plans tonight — tomorrow noon?
好。Hǎo.OK.
💬 D2_
这是菜单，请点菜。Zhè shì càidān, qǐng diǎn cài.Here's the menu — please order.
来一个鱼香肉丝，一个麻婆豆腐，再来两碗米饭，一个酸辣汤。Lái yī gè yúxiāng ròusī, yī gè mápó dòufu, zài lái liǎng wǎn mǐfàn, yī gè suānlà tāng.Fish-Fragrant Shredded Pork, Mapo Tofu, 2 bowls rice, Hot & Sour Soup.
要什么饮料？Yào shénme yǐnliào?What drinks?
来一壶茶。Lái yī hú chá.A pot of tea.
请稍等。Qǐng shāo děng.Please wait a moment.
你觉得中国菜好吃吗？Nǐ juéde Zhōngguó cài hǎochī ma?Do you think Chinese food is tasty?
吃是好吃，不过油太多。Chī shì hǎochī, búguò yóu tài duō.It IS tasty, but too oily.
你喜欢吃什么菜？Nǐ xǐhuān chī shénme cài?What food do you like?
我最喜欢吃韩国菜。我喜欢吃辣的。你呢？Wǒ zuì xǐhuān chī Hánguó cài. Wǒ xǐhuān chī là de. Nǐ ne?I like Korean food best. I like spicy food. And you?
我呀，酸的、辣的、咸的、苦的都喜欢吃，就是不喜欢吃甜的。Wǒ ya, suān de, là de, xián de, kǔ de dōu xǐhuān chī, jiùshì bù xǐhuān chī tián de.Me? I like sour, spicy, salty, bitter — just don't like sweet.
💬 D3_
小姐，结账。Xiǎojiě, jiézhàng.Miss, the bill please.
再给我们两张餐巾纸。Zài gěi wǒmen liǎng zhāng cānjīnzhǐ.And two napkins, please.
我来付钱，今天我请客。Wǒ lái fù qián, jīntiān wǒ qǐngkè.I'll pay — today is my treat.

📝 Notes
The Restaurant Script
A Chinese meal out runs on fixed lines: call 服务员, ask for the 菜单, 点 your dishes, and finish with 结账. Knowing these four words covers most of what you will need to say.
服务员，点菜。fú wù yuán， diǎn cài。Waiter, we would like to order.
请结账。qǐng jié zhàng。The bill, please.
请客 — Treating Someone
请客 means to treat — the host pays. Refusing too hard is not expected, but offering once is polite; the stock reply is 下次我请 (my turn next time).
今天我请客。jīn tiān wǒ qǐng kè。It is on me today.
下次我请你。xià cì wǒ qǐng nǐ。I will treat you next time.
📚 Grammar
1. ……是……，但是/不过/就是…… — Conceding
X + 是 + Adj， 但是 / 不过 / 就是 + Drawback
Repeat the adjective around 是 to admit a point, then add the reservation. 就是 is the most colloquial of the three, 但是 the most formal.
吃是好吃，不过油太多。chī shì hǎo chī， bù guò yóu tài duō。It is tasty, but far too oily.
好是好，就是太远。hǎo shì hǎo， jiù shì tài yuǎn。It is good, just too far.
2. 好吗 / 吧 — Making Suggestions
Suggestion + 好吗 / Sentence + 吧
好吗 at the end asks “OK?” and leaves the decision to the other person. 吧 nudges: it means “let us just do it”.
咱们去尝尝，好吗？zán men qù cháng cháng， hǎo ma？Let’s go and try it, OK?
明天中午好吗？míng tiān zhōng wǔ hǎo ma？How about tomorrow at noon?
我们去吃饭吧。wǒ men qù chī fàn ba。Let’s go and eat.
3. Taste Words
酸 / 甜 / 苦 / 辣 / 咸 / 油
The six basic tastes are 酸 (sour), 甜 (sweet), 苦 (bitter), 辣 (spicy), 咸 (salty) and 油 (oily). Add 的 and they become nouns: 我喜欢吃辣的.
我喜欢吃辣的。wǒ xǐ huan chī là de。I like spicy food.
酸的、辣的、咸的、苦的都喜欢吃。suān de、 là de、 xián de、 kǔ de dōu xǐ huan chī。I like sour, spicy, salty and bitter alike.
就是不喜欢吃甜的。jiù shì bù xǐ huan chī tián de。I just do not like sweet things.
