💬 Dialogue
💬 D1_
我们学校里边有教学楼、办公楼、图书馆和留学生宿舍楼。Wǒmen xuéxiào lǐbian yǒu jiàoxuélóu, bàngōnglóu, túshūguǎn hé liúxuéshēng sùshè lóu.Our school has a teaching building, office building, library and intl. student dorm.
办公楼在教学楼北边。Bàngōnglóu zài jiàoxuélóu běibian.The office building is north of the teaching building.
图书馆在办公楼西边。Túshūguǎn zài bàngōnglóu xībian.The library is west of the office building.
留学生宿舍楼在图书馆南边。Liúxuéshēng sùshè lóu zài túshūguǎn nánbian.The intl. student dorm is south of the library.
💬 D2_
学校附近有很多饭馆和商店。Xuéxiào fùjìn yǒu hěn duō fànguǎn hé shāngdiàn.There are many restaurants and shops near the school.
东边还有一个电影院。Dōngbian hái yǒu yī gè diànyǐngyuàn.There's also a movie theater to the east.
电影院对面是一个药店。Diànyǐngyuàn duìmiàn shì yī gè yàodiàn.Opposite the theater is a pharmacy.
药店旁边是一个超市。Yàodiàn pángbiān shì yī gè chāoshì.Next to the pharmacy is a supermarket.

左边是小雨，右边是英男。Zuǒbian shì Xiǎoyǔ, yòubian shì Yīngnán.Xiaoyu is on the left, Yingnan on the right.

📝 Notes
边 and 面 — Location Suffixes
Directions are made by adding 边 or 面 to a noun: 东边, 西边, 南边, 北边, 旁边, 对面, 后边. The two suffixes are interchangeable in northern speech.
银行在学校西边。yín háng zài xué xiào xī biān。The bank is west of the school.
超市在药店旁边。chāo shì zài yào diàn páng biān。The supermarket is next to the pharmacy.
们 — Plural for People Only
们 turns a person word into a plural: 我们, 他们, 同学们, 朋友们. Never add it after a number (三个人, not 三个人们) and never to objects.
同学们都在这儿。tóng xué men dōu zài zhè ér。The classmates are all here.
他们都是我朋友。tā men dōu shì wǒ péng you。They are all my friends.
📚 Grammar
1. 有 vs. 是 — Two Kinds of “There Is”
Place + 有 / 是 + Object
Place + 有 simply states that something exists there. Place + 是 identifies what it is — it answers “what is that place?” and is used when the thing is specific or unique.
学校附近有很多饭馆。xué xiào fù jìn yǒu hěn duō fàn guǎn。There are many restaurants near the school.
电影院对面是一个药店。diàn yǐng yuàn duì miàn shì yī gè yào diàn。Opposite the cinema is a pharmacy.
药店旁边是一个超市。yào diàn páng biān shì yī gè chāo shì。Next to the pharmacy is a supermarket.
2. 在 + Location — Stating Position
Subject + 在 + Location
The location can be a direction word (北边), a position word (旁边, 中间) or a place name. Between two things use 在 A 和 B 中间.
办公楼在教学楼北边。bàn gōng lóu zài jiào xué lóu běi biān。The office building is north of the classroom building.
图书馆在办公楼西边。tú shū guǎn zài bàn gōng lóu xī biān。The library is west of the office building.
西蒙在小雨和英男中间。xī méng zài xiǎo yǔ hé yīng nán zhōng jiān。Simon is between Xiaoyu and Yingnan.
3. Location Words as Modifiers — Use 的
Noun + Location + 的 + Noun
When a location word comes in front of a noun it needs 的: 桌子上边的书. Note 里 instead of 里边 before 的: 抽屉里的巧克力.
桌子上边的书。zhuō zi shàng biān de shū。The book on the desk.
抽屉里的巧克力。chōu tì lǐ de qiǎo kè lì。The chocolate in the drawer.
桌子下边的书包。zhuō zi xià biān de shū bāo。The schoolbag under the desk.
