💬 Dialogue
💬 D1_
去邮局怎么走？Qù yóujú zěnme zǒu?How do I get to the post office?
一直往前走，到十字路口往右拐。Yīzhí wǎng qián zǒu, dào shízì lùkǒu wǎng yòu guǎi.Go straight, turn right at the intersection.
离这儿多远？Lí zhèr duō yuǎn?How far is it from here?
二百多米。Èrbǎi duō mǐ.A little over 200 meters.
💬 D2_
从这儿到百货大楼有多远？Cóng zhèr dào bǎihuò dàlóu yǒu duō yuǎn?How far to the department store?
十三四公里。Shísānsì gōnglǐ.About 13 or 14 km.
怎么坐车？Zěnme zuò chē?How do I take the bus?
过马路，先坐2路公共汽车然后换地铁。Guò mǎlù, xiān zuò èr lù gōnggòng qìchē ránhòu huàn dìtiě.Cross the street, take bus #2, transfer to subway.
💬 D3_
请问，我去天安门，应该走哪条路？Qǐngwèn, wǒ qù Tiān'ānmén, yīnggāi zǒu nǎ tiáo lù?Excuse me, which road to Tiananmen?
走这条路或者那条路都行。Zǒu zhè tiáo lù huòzhě nà tiáo lù dōu xíng.Either this road or that road works.
哪条路近？Nǎ tiáo lù jìn?Which road is closer?
这条路近，不过有点儿乱。Zhè tiáo lù jìn, búguò yǒudiǎnr luàn.This road is closer, but it's a bit chaotic.
我骑车技术不太高，还是走那条路吧。Wǒ qíchē jìshù bú tài gāo, háishì zǒu nà tiáo lù ba.My cycling skills aren't great — I'd better take that road.
💬 D4_
周末咱们去大同，好吗？Zhōumò zánmen qù Dàtóng, hǎo ma?Let's go to Datong this weekend, OK?
好啊。坐火车还是坐飞机？Hǎo a. Zuò huǒchē háishì zuò fēijī?Great! By train or by plane?
坐飞机吧，又快又舒服。Zuò fēijī ba, yòu kuài yòu shūfu.Let's fly — it's both fast and comfortable.
可是坐飞机太贵了，还是坐火车吧。Kěshì zuò fēijī tài guì le, háishì zuò huǒchē ba.But flying is too expensive — let's take the train instead.
📝 Notes
或者 vs. 还是 — Or, Two Ways
In statements use 或者; in questions use 还是. If the sentence ends in 吗 or carries a question mark, you almost certainly need 还是.
坐地铁或者坐公共汽车。zuò dì tiě huò zhě zuò gōng gòng qì chē。Take the subway or the bus.
坐地铁还是坐公共汽车？zuò dì tiě hái shì zuò gōng gòng qì chē？Take the subway or the bus?
Ways to Get Around
Chinese names the vehicle with a verb: 坐 for things you sit in (地铁, 公共汽车, 火车, 飞机, 出租车), 骑 for a bicycle, and plain 走 for going on foot.
我骑自行车去。wǒ qí zì xíng chē qù。I go by bicycle.
我们坐地铁吧。wǒ men zuò dì tiě ba。Let’s take the subway.
📚 Grammar
1. 往 + Direction + Verb
往 + Direction + Verb
往 points the action in a direction: 往前走 (go straight ahead), 往右拐 (turn right). It always comes before the verb, never after.
往前走。wǎng qián zǒu。Go straight ahead.
往右拐。wǎng yòu guǎi。Turn right.
2. Approximate Numbers (概数)
Adjacent Numbers / Number + 多
Two ways to round off: put two adjacent numbers together (十三四 = 13 or 14, 二三十 = 20 to 30), or add 多 after a round number (二百多 = just over 200). With a measure word, 多 goes AFTER it: 二百块多.
十三四公里。shí sān sì gōng lǐ。About 13 or 14 km.
二百多米。èr bǎi duō mǐ。A little over 200 metres.
二百块多。èr bǎi kuài duō。Just over 200 kuai.
3. 还是……吧 — Better to …
还是 + Option + 吧
When weighing two options, 还是 plus 吧 means “better to go with…”. It is the natural way to settle a plan after comparing.
还是走那条路吧。hái shì zǒu nà tiáo lù ba。Better take that road.
还是坐火车吧。hái shì zuò huǒ chē ba。Better take the train.
4. 先……，然后…… — First …, Then …
先 + Step 1，然后 + Step 2
For giving directions or describing a sequence, put 先 before the first step and 然后 before the next. Both come after the subject, before the verb.
你先往前走，然后往右拐。nǐ xiān wǎng qián zǒu， rán hòu wǎng yòu guǎi。First go straight ahead, then turn right.
我们先坐地铁，然后换公共汽车。wǒ men xiān zuò dì tiě， rán hòu huàn gōng gòng qì chē。We take the subway first, then change to the bus.
