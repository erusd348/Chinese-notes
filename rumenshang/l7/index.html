💬 Dialogue
💬 D1_
最近你身体好吗？Zuìjìn nǐ shēntǐ hǎo ma?How's your health recently?
我身体很好。Wǒ shēntǐ hěn hǎo.My health is very good.
你学习忙吗？Nǐ xuéxí máng ma?Are you busy with studies?
我学习比较忙。Wǒ xuéxí bǐjiào máng.My studies are pretty busy.
你成绩好吗？Nǐ chéngjì hǎo ma?Are your grades good?
马马虎虎。Mǎmǎhūhū.So-so.
你们班同学学习努力吗？Nǐmen bān tóngxué xuéxí nǔlì ma?Do your classmates study hard?
他们学习非常努力。Tāmen xuéxí fēicháng nǔlì.They study extremely hard.
💬 D2_
你的宿舍大不大？Nǐ de sùshè dà bu dà?Is your dorm big?
我的宿舍不太大。Wǒ de sùshè bú tài dà.My dorm is not very big.
宿舍里有没有空调？Sùshè li yǒu méiyǒu kōngtiáo?Is there AC in the dorm?
宿舍里有空调。Sùshè li yǒu kōngtiáo.There is AC in the dorm.
你有没有同屋？Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu tóngwū?Do you have a roommate?
我没有同屋。Wǒ méiyǒu tóngwū.I don't have a roommate.
你的宿舍离教室远不远？Nǐ de sùshè lí jiàoshì yuǎn bu yuǎn?Is your dorm far from the classroom?
我的宿舍离教室很近。Wǒ de sùshè lí jiàoshì hěn jìn.My dorm is very close to the classroom.
💬 D3_
你们学校留学生多不多？Nǐmen xuéxiào liúxuéshēng duō bu duō?Are there many international students at your school?
我们学校留学生挺多的。Wǒmen xuéxiào liúxuéshēng tǐng duō de.Our school has quite a lot.
你们学校食堂怎么样？Nǐmen xuéxiào shítáng zěnmeyàng?How's your school cafeteria?
我们学校食堂不错。Wǒmen xuéxiào shítáng búcuò.Our school cafeteria is pretty good.
📝 Notes
有 vs. 没有 — Having and Not Having
有 never takes 不 for its negative: the opposite is always 没有. This holds for possession (“I have no time”) and for existence (“there is no air conditioning”).
宿舍有空调。sù shè yǒu kòng diào。The dorm has air conditioning.
宿舍没有空调。sù shè méi yǒu kòng diào。The dorm has no air conditioning.
Dialling Degree Up and Down
比较 (fairly) < 挺 (rather) < 很 < 非常 (extremely) < 太 (too). 马马虎虎 is the everyday shrug for “so-so”, and 太…了 usually carries a complaint.
我最近比较忙。wǒ zuì jìn bǐ jiào máng。I have been fairly busy lately.
她挺漂亮。tā tǐng piào liang。She is rather pretty.
他非常努力。tā fēi cháng nǔ lì。He works extremely hard.
📚 Grammar
1. Topic-Comment Sentences
Topic + (Subject + Predicate)
Chinese often names the topic first and then says a full sentence about it: 我身体很好 = “as for me, health is good”. This is the usual way to talk about body parts and personal attributes, and 的 is often dropped.
你身体好吗？nǐ shēn tǐ hǎo ma？How is your health?
我身体很好。wǒ shēn tǐ hěn hǎo。My health is good.
他个子很高。tā gè zi hěn gāo。He is tall.
2. Yes/No Questions — 吗 and A-不-A
Statement + 吗 / A-不-A
Two options: add 吗 to the end, or repeat the verb / adjective with 不 in the middle (大不大, 有没有). The A-不-A form sounds snappier and is very common in speech.
你学习忙吗？nǐ xué xí máng ma？Are you busy with your studies?
宿舍大不大？sù shè dà bù dà？Is the dorm big or not?
有没有空调？yǒu méi yǒu kòng diào？Is there air conditioning?
3. ……怎么样？ — Asking How Something Is
Topic + 怎么样
Put 怎么样 after a topic to ask for a description or an opinion. The answer is usually an adjective predicate: 不错, 很好, 马马虎虎.
你们学校食堂怎么样？nǐ men xué xiào shí táng zěn me yàng？How is your school cafeteria?
我们学校食堂不错。wǒ men xué xiào shí táng bù cuò。Our cafeteria is pretty good.
