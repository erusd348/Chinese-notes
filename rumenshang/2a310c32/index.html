📝 Notes
左右 — Rounding a Number Off
Put 左右 after a number to mean “or so”: 一千块左右, 二十个左右. It is the safest way to give an approximate figure without committing.
一千块左右。yī qiān kuài zuǒ yòu。About a thousand kuai.
二十个左右。èr shí gè zuǒ yòu。Twenty or so.
Measure Words for Clothes
Garments have their own counters: 件 for tops and jackets, 条 for trousers and long thin things, 双 for anything in pairs. Getting these right makes you sound instantly more fluent.
一件衬衣。yī jiàn chèn yī。A shirt.
一条裤子。yī tiáo kù zi。A pair of trousers.
一双鞋。yī shuāng xié。A pair of shoes.
📚 Grammar
1. 想 vs. 要 — Two Kinds of “Want”
想 / 要 + Verb
想 is a wish (I would like to); 要 is firmer (I want / I will). Negatives: 不想 for “do not want”, 不用 for “no need”, and 不要 means “do not!” — a command, not a refusal.
我想买个手机。wǒ xiǎng mǎi gè shǒu jī。I would like to buy a phone.
我不想买。wǒ bù xiǎng mǎi。I do not want to buy it.
你不用买。nǐ bù yòng mǎi。You do not need to buy it.
您要买点儿什么？nín yào mǎi diǎn ér shén me？What would you like to buy?
2. 又……又…… — Both … And …
又 + Adj + 又 + Adj
Two 又 before two adjectives say that both qualities hold at the same time. It is a favourite in sales talk and in everyday praise.
质量又好，价钱又便宜。zhì liàng yòu hǎo， jià qián yòu pián yi。Good quality and cheap too.
又快又舒服。yòu kuài yòu shū fu。Both fast and comfortable.
3. 有点儿 vs. 一点儿
有点儿 + Adj / Adj + 一点儿
有点儿 goes BEFORE an adjective and usually complains (a bit too small). 一点儿 goes AFTER an adjective in comparisons (a bit bigger), or before a noun for “a little of something”.
这双有点儿小。zhè shuāng yǒu diǎn ér xiǎo。This pair is a bit too small.
这双比那双大一点儿。zhè shuāng bǐ nà shuāng dà yī diǎn ér。This pair is a bit bigger than that pair.
有大一点儿的吗？yǒu dà yī diǎn ér de ma？Do you have a slightly bigger one?
4. Verb Reduplication — Softer, Shorter
VV / V 一 V
Repeat a verb (看看, 试试, 介绍介绍) to make the action brief and casual — it takes the pressure off, which is why shopkeepers use it constantly.
你给我介绍介绍。nǐ gěi wǒ jiè shào jiè shào。Tell me a bit about them.
我试试。wǒ shì shi。Let me try.
