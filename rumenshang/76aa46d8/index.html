💬 Dialogue
💬 D1_
保罗：买两支圆珠笔。Mǎi liǎng zhī yuánzhūbǐ.I'd like two ballpoint pens.
售货员：要红的还是要蓝的？Yào hóng de háishì yào lán de?Do you want the red one or the blue one?
保罗：一样一支。Yī yàng yī zhī.One of each.
售货员：还要别的吗？Hái yào bié de ma?Anything else?
保罗：不要了。Bú yào le.That's all.
💬 D2_
保罗：请问，哪儿卖啤酒？Qǐngwèn, nǎr mài píjiǔ?Excuse me, where can I buy beer?
售货员：那儿卖。Nàr mài.Over there.
保罗：有青岛啤酒吗？Yǒu Qīngdǎo píjiǔ ma?Do you have Tsingtao beer?
售货员：有，要几瓶？Yǒu, yào jǐ píng?Yes, how many bottles?
保罗：要四瓶，再要两听可口可乐。Yào sì píng, zài yào liǎng tīng Kěkǒukělè.Four bottles, and two cans of Coca-Cola.
💬 D3_
保罗：一共多少钱？Yīgòng duōshǎo qián?How much in total?
售货员：十九块四。Shíjiǔ kuài sì.19.40 yuan.
保罗：给你钱。Gěi nǐ qián.Here's the money.
售货员：您有零钱吗？Nín yǒu língqián ma?Do you have change?
保罗：没有。Méiyǒu.No.
售货员：收您五十块，找您三十块零六毛。请数一下儿。Shōu nín wǔshí kuài, zhǎo nín sānshí kuài líng liù máo. Qǐng shǔ yīxiàr.Received 50 yuan — change is 30.60. Please count it.
💬 D4_
保罗：橘子多少钱一斤？Júzi duōshǎo qián yī jīn?How much per jin for the oranges?
售货员：大的三块钱一斤，小的十块钱四斤。Dà de sān kuài qián yī jīn, xiǎo de shí kuài qián sì jīn.Big ones 3 yuan/jin, small ones 10 yuan for 4 jin.
保罗：甜不甜？Tián bu tián?Are they sweet?
售货员：您尝一下儿，不甜不要钱。Nín cháng yīxiàr, bù tián bú yào qián.Taste one — if not sweet, no charge.
💬 D5_
保罗：西红柿怎么卖？Xīhóngshì zěnme mài?How do you sell the tomatoes?
售货员：一斤三块八。Yī jīn sān kuài bā.3.80 yuan per jin.
保罗：新鲜不新鲜？Xīnxiān bu xīnxiān?Are they fresh?
售货员：这是今天早上摘的，新鲜极了。Zhè shì jīntiān zǎoshang zhāi de, xīnxiān jí le.These were picked this morning — extremely fresh.
📝 Notes
还是 (Questions) vs. 或者 (Statements)
Both mean “or”, but 还是 only appears in questions and 或者 only in statements. Mixing them up is a classic slip, so keep the pair together in your notes.
你要红的还是蓝的？nǐ yào hóng de hái shì lán de？Do you want the red one or the blue one?
我要红的或者蓝的。wǒ yào hóng de huò zhě lán de。I want the red one or the blue one.
斤 — Shopping by the Half-Kilo
Loose fruit and vegetables are sold by the 斤 (500 g). Prices are quoted per jin: 多少钱一斤. Two jin is a normal bag of oranges.
多少钱一斤？duō shǎo qián yī jīn？How much per jin?
两斤橘子。liǎng jīn jú zi。Two jin of oranges.
📚 Grammar
1. 还是 — Choice Questions
Option A + 还是 + Option B
Put 还是 between two options to ask “A or B?”. Answer by picking one side — or, when you want both, say 一样一个.
要红的还是要蓝的？yào hóng de hái shì yào lán de？Do you want the red one or the blue one?
一样一支。yī yàng yī zhī。One of each.
2. 的 — Turning Adjectives Into Nouns
Adjective + 的
的 after an adjective creates “the … one”: 红的 (the red one), 大的 (the big one). It also freezes question words: 别的 (other things).
我要红的。wǒ yào hóng de。I want the red one.
有大一点儿的吗？yǒu dà yī diǎn ér de ma？Do you have a slightly bigger one?
还要别的吗？hái yào bié de ma？Anything else?
3. 了 — A Change of Situation
Sentence + 了
At the end of a sentence, 了 signals that the situation has changed — not that an action is finished. 不吃了 means “I am not eating any more”, not “I did not eat”.
不要了。bù yào le。I do not want any more.
不吃了。bù chī le。I am not eating any more.
