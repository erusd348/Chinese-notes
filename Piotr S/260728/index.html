📚 Grammar
🏨 入住退房对话 hotel check-in/out dialogue
你好，我订了一个房间。 nǐ hǎo, wǒ dìng le yī gè fáng jiān Hello, I booked a room.
您叫什么名字？ nín jiào shén me míng zi What's your name?
我叫……这是我的护照。 wǒ jiào... zhè shì wǒ de hù zhào My name is... This is my passport.
您住几个晚上？ nín zhù jǐ gè wǎn shàng How many nights will you stay?
我住三个晚上。 wǒ zhù sān gè wǎn shàng I'll stay three nights.
您的房间在5楼，510号。 nín de fáng jiān zài wǔ lóu, wǔ yāo líng hào Your room is on the 5th floor, room 510.
这是您的房卡。 zhè shì nín de fáng kǎ This is your room card.
谢谢。早饭几点开始？ xiè xie. zǎo fàn jǐ diǎn kāi shǐ Thanks. What time does breakfast start?
早上7点到10点。 zǎo shang qī diǎn dào shí diǎn From 7 AM to 10 AM.
Wi-Fi密码是多少？ wài fài mì mǎ shì duō shǎo What's the Wi-Fi password?
你好，我要退房。 nǐ hǎo, wǒ yào tuì fáng Hello, I'd like to check out.
几点前退房？ jǐ diǎn qián tuì fáng What time is check-out?
12点前退房。 shí èr diǎn qián tuì fáng Check-out is before 12 PM.
请把房卡给我。 qǐng bǎ fáng kǎ gěi wǒ Please give me the room card.
可以刷卡吗？ kě yǐ shuā kǎ ma Can I pay by card?
可以。这是您的账单。 kě yǐ. zhè shì nín de zhàng dān Yes. Here's your bill.
可以帮我存行李吗？ kě yǐ bāng wǒ cún xíng li ma Can I store my luggage here?
可以，存到下午5点。 kě yǐ, cún dào xià wǔ wǔ diǎn Yes, you can store it until 5 PM.
