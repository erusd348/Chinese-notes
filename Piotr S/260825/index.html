📚 Grammar
S + 怎么样？ How is...? / How about...?
你今天怎么样？ nǐ jīn tiān zěn me yàng How are you today?
你的车怎么样？ nǐ de chē zěn me yàng How is your car?
你周末怎么样？ nǐ zhōu mò zěn me yàng How was your weekend?
怎么 + V？ how to do sth
苹果怎么说？ píng guǒ zěn me shuō How do you say "apple"?
很多 + N a lot of N
中国有很多人。 zhōng guó yǒu hěn duō rén China has a lot of people.
我吃了很多水果。 wǒ chī le hěn duō shuǐ guǒ I ate a lot of fruit.
很少 + V rarely do sth
我很少吃鸡肉。 wǒ hěn shǎo chī jī ròu I rarely eat chicken.
我很少走路。 wǒ hěn shǎo zǒu lù I rarely walk.
我很少喝汤。 wǒ hěn shǎo hē tāng I rarely drink soup.
为什么 + S + V？ 因为 + 原因 Why...? Because...
没有为什么！ méi yǒu wèi shén me No reason!
我不喜欢，因为太辣了。 wǒ bù xǐ huān，yīn wèi tài là le I don't like it because it's too spicy.
V + 了 completed action (了 marks it done)
你做什么了？ nǐ zuò shén me le What did you do?
我吃了很多牛肉，还看了一个电影。 wǒ chī le hěn duō niú ròu，hái kàn le yí gè diàn yǐng I ate a lot of beef and also watched a movie.
我周末学了中文和IT。 wǒ zhōu mò xué le zhōng wén hé IT I studied Chinese and IT this weekend.
最 + V the most
你最喜欢吃什么？我最喜欢吃牛肉。 nǐ zuì xǐ huān chī shén me？wǒ zuì xǐ huān chī niú ròu What do you like eating most? I like beef the most.
