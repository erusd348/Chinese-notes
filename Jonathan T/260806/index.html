📚 Grammar
比 + N + Adj comparison — more...than...
新加坡的印度人比英国的印度人多。 xīn jiā pō de yìn dù rén bǐ yīng guó de yìn dù rén duō There are more Indians in Singapore than in the UK.
陪 + sb + V to accompany someone to do something
我陪我老婆去逛街。 wǒ péi wǒ lǎo pó qù guàng jiē I accompany my wife shopping.
在 + Place + 出生 to be born in...
在英国出生的印度人很好。 zài yīng guó chū shēng de yìn dù rén hěn hǎo Indians born in the UK are very good.
💬 Sentences
我们的公司有180人，是中等公司。 wǒ men de gōng sī yǒu yī bǎi bā shí rén, shì zhōng děng gōng sī Our company has 180 people; it's a medium company.
少于100人的公司是小公司。 shǎo yú yī bǎi rén de gōng sī shì xiǎo gōng sī Companies with fewer than 100 people are small companies.
我跟我同事陪客人吃饭。 wǒ gēn wǒ tóng shì péi kè rén chī fàn I accompany guests to dinner with my coworkers.
昨天的访客是我们公司姐妹公司的人。 zuó tiān de fǎng kè shì wǒ men gōng sī jiě mèi gōng sī de rén Yesterday's visitor was from our company's sister company.
我白天跟他们聊了很多工作的事。 wǒ bái tiān gēn tā men liáo le hěn duō gōng zuò de shì I talked a lot about work with them during the day.
