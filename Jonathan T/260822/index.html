📚 Grammar
Num + 量词 + N（一支/一枝/一瓶/一听/一斤） (Structure) number + measure word + noun — pens use 支, flowers use 枝
我要买两支圆珠笔。 wǒ yào mǎi liǎng zhī yuánzhūbǐ. I want to buy two ballpoint pens.
一支笔。 yì zhī bǐ. One pen.
一枝花。 yì zhī huā. One flower.
一斤苹果。 yì jīn píngguǒ. Half a kilo of apples.
S + 要 + V + 的 + A 还是 B？ (Structure) do you want A or B? — choosing between options
你要红的还是蓝的？ nǐ yào hóng de háishì lán de? Do you want the red one or the blue one?
S + 还要 + N + 吗？ (Structure) do you still want/need anything else?
你还要别的吗？ nǐ hái yào bié de ma? Do you need anything else?
一共 + 多少钱？ (Structure) how much is it in total?
一共多少钱？ yígòng duōshao qián? How much is it all together?
十九块四。 shíjiǔ kuài sì. 19.40 yuan.
S + 收 + 钱 + 找 + 钱 (Structure) receive money and give back change
我收你五十块，找你三十块六。 wǒ shōu nǐ wǔshí kuài, zhǎo nǐ sānshí kuài liù. I take your 50 yuan and give you 30.60 back.
V + 一下 (Structure) verb + a bit / briefly (softens the request)
请数一下。 qǐng shǔ yíxià. Please count it.
