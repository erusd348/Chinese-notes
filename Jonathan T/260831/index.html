📖 课文：买水果和蔬菜 (Text) Buying fruit and vegetables
橘子多少钱一斤？ jú zi duō shao qián yì jīn? How much are the tangerines per half-kilo?
大的三块一斤，小的十块四斤。 dà de sān kuài yì jīn, xiǎo de shí kuài sì jīn. The big ones are 3 yuan per half-kilo, the small ones 10 yuan for four half-kilos.
甜不甜？ tián bù tián? Are they sweet?
您可以尝一下，不甜不要钱。 nín kě yǐ cháng yí xià, bù tián bú yào qián. You can have a taste; if it's not sweet, it's free.
西红柿怎么卖？ xī hóng shì zěn me mài? How are the tomatoes sold?
三块八一斤。 sān kuài bā yì jīn. 3.80 yuan per half-kilo.
新不新鲜？ xīn bù xīn xian? Are they fresh?
它们是今天早上摘的，新鲜极了。 tā men shì jīn tiān zǎo shang zhāi de, xīn xian jí le. They were picked this morning — extremely fresh.
