📖 Vocabulary
孤独 gū dú lonely
前任 qián rèn ex (boyfriend/girlfriend)
异地恋 yì dì liàn long-distance relationship
毕业 bì yè to graduate
联系 lián xì to keep in touch
背叛 bèi pàn to betray
难过 nán guò sad
表妹 biǎo mèi younger female cousin (maternal)
堂妹 táng mèi younger female cousin (paternal)
收拾 shōu shi to tidy up
📚 Grammar
孤独 lonely
我一个人住在牛津有一点孤独。 wǒ yī gè rén zhù zài niú jīn yǒu yī diǎn gū dú I live alone in Oxford a bit lonely.
前任 / 毕业 ex; to graduate
毕业两年后，我前任去上海工作了。 bì yè liǎng nián hòu, wǒ qián rèn qù shàng hǎi gōng zuò le Two years after graduation my ex went to Shanghai to work.
联系 to keep in touch
你们还有联系吗？ nǐ men hái yǒu lián xì ma Do you still keep in touch?
背叛 / 难过 to betray; sad
她背叛了我。 tā bèi pàn le wǒ She betrayed me.
异地恋 long-distance relationship
异地恋很难成功。 yì dì liàn hěn nán chéng gōng Long-distance relationships are hard to succeed.
表妹 / 堂妹 younger female cousin
我的表妹住在香港，所以我们很少见面。 wǒ de biǎo mèi zhù zài xiāng gǎng, suǒ yǐ wǒ men hěn shǎo jiàn miàn My cousin lives in Hong Kong so we rarely see each other.
我的大表妹这个月大学毕业，所以她来英国旅游。 wǒ de dà biǎo mèi zhè ge yuè dà xué bì yè, suǒ yǐ tā lái yīng guó lǚ yóu My older cousin graduated from university this month so she came to the UK to travel.
收拾 to tidy up
我在收拾房间。 wǒ zài shōu shi fáng jiān I am tidying my room.
比 + 以前 + 更 + Adj compared to before; even more
房租比以前更贵。 fáng zū bǐ yǐ qián gèng guì The rent is even more expensive than before.
他的书房在我的房间对面。 tā de shū fáng zài wǒ de fáng jiān duì miàn His study is opposite my room.
我每天早上从旁边经过。 wǒ měi tiān zǎo shang cóng páng biān jīng guò I pass by it every morning.

📝 Class Homework
1.
After work I feel a bit lonely going home alone.
💡 下班 / 以后 / 一个人 / 有点 / 孤独
2.
My ex and I had a long-distance relationship, and it did not work out.
💡 前任 / 异地恋 / 没成功
3.
She moved to Beijing. We still keep in touch.
💡 搬到 / 北京 / 还有 / 联系
4.
My friend was betrayed and he is very sad.
💡 朋友 / 被 / 背叛 / 很难过
5.
My younger cousin lives in Singapore, so they rarely see each other.
💡 小表妹 / 新加坡 / 很少 / 见面
6.
Her family graduates this year and plans to travel in the UK.
💡 今年毕业 / 打算 / 英国 / 旅游
7.
Every night I tidy my room a little, and then it feels much nicer.
💡 每天晚上 / 收拾 / 一点 / 好多了
8.
Her rent is much more expensive than before.
💡 房租 / 比以前 / 贵多了
9.
The subway station is opposite my apartment, very convenient.
💡 地铁站 / 在 / 公寓 / 对面 / 方便
10.
My older cousin is in Shanghai, I rarely see her, so I really miss her.
💡 堂姐 / 在上海 / 很少 / 见面 / 很想

Write order: top→bottom, left→right, outside→inside
