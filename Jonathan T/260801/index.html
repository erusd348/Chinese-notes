📚 Grammar
应该 + V should + verb
你明天应该去教室上课。 nǐ míng tiān yīng gāi qù jiào shì shàng kè You should go to class in the classroom tomorrow.
最近 + S + V recently...
最近我很忙。 zuì jìn wǒ hěn máng I've been very busy recently.
如果 + S + V，S + 可以 + V if...then...can...
如果我赚很多钱，我可以提前还。 rú guǒ wǒ zhuàn hěn duō qián, wǒ kě yǐ tí qián huán If I earn a lot of money, I can pay back early.
因为 + S + 小气，只让 + O + V because...only let...
因为我的学校很小气，他们只让学生住一年宿舍。 yīn wèi wǒ de xué xiào hěn xiǎo qì, tā men zhǐ ràng xué shēng zhù yī nián sù shè Because my school is stingy, they only let students live in the dorm for one year.
大学宿舍经历 university dorm experience
我大学第一年住在宿舍。 wǒ dà xué dì yī nián zhù zài sù shè In my first year of university I lived in the dorm.
第二年，我跟四个室友一起租房。 dì èr nián, wǒ gēn sì gè shì yǒu yī qǐ zū fáng In the second year, I rented an apartment with four roommates.
第三年我一个人住。 dì sān nián wǒ yī gè rén zhù In the third year I lived alone.
我要还30年。 wǒ yào huán sān shí nián I have to pay it back for 30 years.
我爸爸妈妈给我房租钱和生活费。 wǒ bà ba mā ma gěi wǒ fáng zū qián hé shēng huó fèi My parents give me rent money and living expenses.
